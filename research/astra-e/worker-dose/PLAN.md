# Fixed worker dose at equal concurrency

Keep every worker and queue slot. Assign worker indices congruent to zero modulo four to ordinary eager Cells and every other index to the measured edge constructor. At W7/8/9/10 this yields 2/2/3/3 eager workers. The worker calls its kernel's Default once from its already named thread, so the fraction is local to that pool even when two separate decoders run concurrently. The ordinary decoder outside a numbered pool uses edge. Each worker holds one enum variant. A per-block portfolio would have different retention and scheduling costs. The scanner, job distribution, RLE1, block/stream CRCs and delivery remain those of the existing workers.

For a shared queue with constant independent service rates and a long common workload, the mixed service rate is e times the eager rate plus (W−e) times the edge rate, bounded by W times the faster rate. A gain over both homogeneous arms at the same W therefore requires a change of service rates under shared load, or effects of finite work, scanner and ordered delivery. On the older i9 one-copy fixed-affinity runs, eager/Lazy wall at W4 was 0.911–0.931 across the three receipts and rose to 1.105–1.121 at W10. The five-arm edge CI measured edge/eager at 1.2532, 1.2185 and 1.0534 at W1/2/4. Neither result measures mixed workers; the i9 edge result uses four copies, unpinned, on a different source. The proposed dose tests whether enough eager workers can keep their low-concurrency advantage while using edge's memory headroom.

Run a diagnostic executable to log constructor indices and actual run calls, including W7/8/9/10, then the complete refreshed 338-case scorer gate with diagnostics compiled out. The local design is 16 paired rotations at W2 and W4, two Wikidata copies per process, edge/eager/mixed, two-second settles and 4W slots. Preserve all direct wall and peak contrasts. A contour matching a separable blend gives no reason to claim synergistic speed. A mixed interval wholly below edge with RSS within Lazy justifies an independent direct comparison, contingent on current target evidence.
