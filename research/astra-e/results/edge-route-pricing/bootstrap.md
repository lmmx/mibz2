# Why this conditional model cannot resolve a close rank

Each paired bootstrap draw in v1 selects 48 full blocks with replacement and 16 tiny blocks with replacement. The price of changing the admission policy on ordinal 165 is the sole source of difference between the two conditional reconstructions. A draw which omits that ordinal gives identical sums for cycles, output bytes and traffic in both arms. All measured slope blocks are carried forward, so both arms also have identical relative DRAM slopes. They therefore receive identical inputs to the fixed-point solver and their ratio is exactly 1 at all three worker counts. This follows for any possible measured cost of the new route on 165.

Under independent uniform draws the probability of omitting the changed ordinal is (47/48)^48 = 0.3640. In the scorer's frozen 400 draws with seed 1 it is omitted 177 times. Consequently the ordinary paired 95% percentile interval for the ratio of these two **conditional** reconstructions contains 1, regardless of the total point-estimate effect. The code reproduces the scorer's exact full-then-tiny draw order. The calculation neither samples the four absent route switches nor estimates their variance.
