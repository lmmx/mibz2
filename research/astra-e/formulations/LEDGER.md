# Formulation ledger

Each numbered round tests an object from which the marked, RLE1-coded text could be recovered. An exact structural test can retire an object before it reaches worker timing. All point estimates from three-round local timing runs are exploratory. The performance register and the frozen i9 criterion continue separately.

| Round | Object and phase it could replace | Proposed invariant and decisive first test | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | An interval return map on phrase anchors, built without the retained-row successor array | Compose affine return bands with stable-select intervals and extend a representative until its smallest right slack cuts the band. Audit label, destination and mark, then charge all interval pieces, predecessor searches and ordering. | 55,302 small cases and ten sampled blocks matched. Frozen needs 47,547 affine pieces and 160,275 extra comparisons. The 12-byte pieces alone exceed the removed edge table on the nine admitted samples. [Proof and counts](f1/) |
| F2 | BWT of delimiter-terminated phrases, produced directly from backward extension of run intervals | Distinct delimiter-ended trie words are prefix-free. Test whether merging the disjoint input intervals into the phrase BWT preserves a net reduction in construction plus output work. | 80,896 marks passed. Frozen quote projection recovers 135,716 token rows in 6,133 runs; the new constructor pays 81,863 selector intersections, 18,994 merge pieces and 73,276 run searches. [Proof and measurements](f2/) |
| F3 | Independent alphabet-projected BWTs and a binary interleaving | Test commutation of symbol projection with cyclic stable ordering on all small marked examples. Where it fails, derive and price the exact extra ordering operation. | Two primitive length-three witnesses defeat all re-markings. On frozen, 322,406 next colours are wrong; 50,133 correction bands approach the original 51,184 runs. An exact interleaved factorization survives. [Counterexamples and counts](f3/) |
| F4 | A reduced BWT on maximal increasing phrases | At a phrase boundary the next byte breaks strict increase. Test whether ordinary lexicographic token ranks induce the correct marked first return even on tied periodic rotations, then charge token discovery, ranks, and expansion. | [Increasing phrase experiment](f4/) |
| F0 | The common cyclic linear recurrence of the eight output bitplanes | Compute the degree of the minimal common shift annihilator on one true marked period. If the degree is near the period, an explicit seed costs almost the whole output before any phase reconstruction or recurrence evaluation. | [Cyclic recurrence screen](f0/) |

Rounds are recorded by hypothesis, including failures. The supplementary track began after the original engineering screens were in flight. We alternate subsequent engineering rounds with formulation rounds. F1 accompanies the streamed RLE1 experiment, F2 accompanies the combined narrow/immediate/lookahead candidate, F3 accompanies the confirmation of short endpoint words, and F4 and F0 investigate new representations while the frozen measurements run.
