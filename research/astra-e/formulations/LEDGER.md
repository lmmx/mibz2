# Astra E formulation ledger

The active programme is the exact BWT of delimiter returns. Its rounds below stay with that object through proof, counterexamples, charged construction, whole-worker measurements and different exact constructors. Each link preserves programs and observations. Wall ratios in this ledger refer to the local worker benchmark against LazyCells unless another comparator is named. The [performance register](../REGISTER.md) identifies scorer revisions, memory and the separate engineering experiments.

## Delimiter programme

| [F2 · BWT of delimiter-ended words](f2/) | Construct the token last column from the original run column. Prefix-free FM intervals are disjoint even at different trie depths. | [F2-word · direct return to a compound marker](f2-word/) | Remove the entire first dictionary and token BWT. For a delimiter d and first-level token w, Ψ raised to the length of dw is an order-preserving conjugacy between starts of dw and the second-level after-occurrence section. ## Round allocation

F1 accompanied the bounded streaming-output experiment, F2 the combined narrow/short/lookahead implementation, F3 the repeat on inline short words, and F4 and F0 the first independent measurements. F1b, F2b and F2c continued through complete reconstructions while edge admission and final-slot repair progressed. The new-scorer engineering series runs alongside the compound-marker, run-jump and dual-gap investigations within F2. The decisive comparison for an engineering gain below roughly five percent is independent paired CI or the workstation.
