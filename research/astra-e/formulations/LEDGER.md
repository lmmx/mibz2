## What remains after delimiter discovery

**Root projection.** Each selector stream is a stable projection of the root labels to one subtree. Synchronized cursors can therefore emit exactly one maximal root token run per descent, with no intermediate merge streams. All 228 real outputs matched; storage fell from 5,390,514 merge pieces to 1,786,472. The 7,739,733 path visits made this merge slower at 1.642 [1.310,2.058] of the materialized dynamic implementation. An independent realizable byte-BWT family attains the n-visit upper bound. A mixed subtree cut can trade visits for stored pieces. [Proof, measurements and independent audit](f2-rootstream/)

**Exact output edits.** In a protected RLE1 component, cut the parser at the mark and clean entries. Each omitted LF cycle contributes strictly positive corrected mass. The length of one corrected lap can therefore certify full coverage, even with overlapping hazard cylinders and a primary inside one. The checker included 917,506 binary marked instances, including unfinished first laps and off-mark protected cycles. Storing only edit offsets removed extra word copies and five dictionary-budget fallbacks; 227 of 228 density-admitted real blocks then wrote directly. [Certificate](f2-rle/count-only/) · [Sparse edit-list kernel](f2-rle/)

**Cost of the formulation.** Independent five-arm CI confirmed that dynamic rank discovery beats static F2 across 1/2/4 workers, while its complete worker was 1.5–2.5% slower than Lazy on that Xeon. Reusing the run-front histogram and hardware popcount reduces the next implementation's paid rank cost. The free-rank oracle's 0.675 ratio applies only to nine blocks; the 228 admissions own 73.58% of the local additive scanner, worker and copy CPU. Extending that ratio everywhere would permit only about 0.053 of admitted Lazy inverse CPU to buy all rank answers. ## Choosing the reconstructed section

A priority queue of original-LF interval widths finds the most frequent return using at most ⌊N/f⌋ expanded intervals, with N bytes on covered cycles and winning frequency f. The winning interval itself is the next marker; repeating needs no intermediate dictionary or token BWT. Forty nested choices on ten real blocks agreed with the dense independent oracle; 15,702 small overlapping or periodic selections also agreed. The native implementation skipped two intermediate levels and passed the full 338-case gate. On 228 admissions it reduced final visits from 5,566,622 to 705,565, while original-column construction lookups rose from 28,053,831 to 89,002,161. Its whole stage was 2.356 [2.238,2.480] of the fixed compound implementation. An explicit dictionary must store at least N/f bytes. [Proof, code, costs and raw results](f2-heaviest/)

For several selected parent return words, h contiguous source components suffice to cap **colored** token-BWT runs at rₚ+h−1, including when the raw marker lengths differ. The bound is sharp even for strictly top-k choices. The tag is the destination component: raw words alone can have disconnected outgoing source buckets. A direct affine-band implementation reconstructs each of ten complete real marked blocks at k=1,2,4,8. A separate dense audit checked 159,176 arbitrary-subset cases. On frozen k=8 increases section rows from 24,165 to 74,284 while lowering band-discovery probes from 245,434 to 164,338. [Construction and real checks](f2-heaviest/union/) · [Sharp witnesses and independent audit](f2-heaviest/union-audit/)

For the native k=8 constructor, the lower lookup and spelling counts reduced discovery CPU to 0.926 [0.886,0.967] of k=1. The residual expansion took 2.610 [2.341,2.911] of k=1, and the whole inverse took 1.048 [1.001,1.097]. The native worker passed all 338 cases. The all-block tests at k=1,4,8 independently checked 205,197,794 inverse bytes for each k and matched the band constructor's run counts on the systematic admissions. **Incomplete first-return permutation.** Stopping as soon as a backward suffix has one row avoids eagerly spelling the rest of that return. Hot links determine the set E of missing source ranks without knowing the missing permutation. On the ten actual quote dictionaries, the optimum charges 649,685 nodes against 908,546 backwards. The experimental question is what forward interval fragmentation costs on the original run index. **Meeting inside a word.** A closed bipartite cut gives the exact smallest number of distinct prefix/suffix expansions across all interior seam choices.
