# Deleting the shifted levels preserves one interval per live spelling

Let U be a nonempty interval of rotation ranks, h ≥ 1, and V_j = Ψ^j(U) for 0 ≤ j ≤ h. At levels j < h all selected ranks must share the outgoing byte P[j]. Their sorted ordinal is preserved by Ψ through the next level, including periodic ties. Write V = V_h, B = union of V_1 through V_h, and D = the complement of B. Row numbers in D and V belong to separate order-compressed coordinate systems. The first column of the original BWT still determines edge bytes.

**One-step closure.** For x in D, LF(x) belongs to D or V. Landing in V_j for j < h would place x in Ψ(V_j) = V_{j+1}, contradicting x in D. Thus on D the original LF step becomes an exact descriptor comprising its byte, a live/terminal tag and a translation into compressed D or compressed V.

**Interval preservation.** If a row x in D reaches U after t forward steps for the first time (with t=0 allowed), its first forward hit of V occurs after precisely t+h steps. A hit at j<h would put x in V_{h−j} and thus in B. At j≥h membership follows from Ψ^j(x) in V iff Ψ^(j−h)(x) in U. Therefore once the common backwards suffix P has been removed at the root, every live query for a spelling w consists of exactly the old U-query interval J_w restricted to D. Single-interval clipped induction gives the old J_w; order compression makes J_w∩D a single interval including when the corresponding original set has holes. Each live (parent, next byte) names a single child. The compressed row index can run the ordinary unary lane operation on it, without a synthetic end position for sparse nodes. The earlier independent partition study counted the physical packets in original coordinates. [Physical partition proof](../../shift-constructor/proof.md)

**Early returns and bases.** Let φ_j(i) be the ith rank of V_j. Backwards from destination φ_h(i), the jth row reached for 1 ≤ j ≤ h is φ_(h−j)(i). At its first hit of V publish the early incoming word P[h−j..h), its true destination ordinal i and the source ordinal rank_V(φ_(h−j)(i)). All instances first returning at this same depth have the same spelling. If no such hit occurs, φ_0(i) lies in D. Indeed if φ_0(i) were Ψ^k(u) for 1 ≤ k ≤ h, then φ_(h−k)(i) would be in V, giving a hit within k backwards steps of φ_h(i). Conversely an earlier hit puts φ_0(i) in B. The late root thus walks U∩D in increasing compressed D order. Append P after the particular earlier bytes when building each late word. Interleave the early entries by their *destination* ordinals, preserving the order of the late stream. Equal type occurrences have contiguous, stable source ordinals by conjugacy with U, and the first terminal occurrence gives the source base. A root with no survivors publishes the early stream alone.

**Marks.** A mark in V has zero skip. A mark x in B outside V belongs to some V_j; rewind one byte by preserving its ordinal and taking the row in V_{j−1}. This gives exactly LF(x) for *every* such j, even when several layers overlap. There are at most h−1 consecutive rows of B outside V on any trajectory. From the earliest of h consecutive such rows, membership in V_j would ensure a V hit among those next h−1 forward steps. Consequently at most h−1 virtual rewinds are needed before entering V or D. Inside D, the projected descriptors give ordinary one-byte backwards steps and the same marked skip. If that projected walk cycles without a terminal, the original marked orbit misses V and is disjoint from all deleted levels. Its bytes can be reconstructed on the retained cycle. A mark starting on a deleted row necessarily lies on a cycle reaching V.

## Exact number of descriptors

Let r be the number of maximal byte runs in the original last column, f the component count of B, v the component count of V, and d the component count of D. Splitting original runs by their intersections with D gives exactly d+s physical pieces, where s counts the original byte seams with both neighboring source ranks in D. Removing a whole interval can add at most one connected piece; hence d+s ≤ r+f.

An additional cut is required within one such physical piece exactly when the translated adjacent arrivals y−1,y lie on a D/V interface. Count an interface once if Ψ(y−1), Ψ(y) are consecutive original D rows in a single original L-byte run. Write e for this number. An arrival from D cannot pass through B outside V, so no other destination boundary needs splitting. There are at most 2 min(f,v) D/V interfaces. Therefore the number of physical descriptors is **exactly d+s+e**, bounded above by r+f+2 min(f,v). The maximal affine descriptors on the compressed row line arise by joining adjacent physical pieces that have the same byte, tag and compressed translation. Only gaps between original D components can permit an additional coalescence after distinct physical pieces have been cut. Consequently at most max(0,d−1) joins are possible, and the normalized count is d+s+e minus the actual join count. These counts also cover the empty complement.

In a stream of C capped records, including skipped deleted pieces, at most C+2f+2 min(f,v) iterations are needed for the source/arrival splits. Source B boundaries already at a cap edge cost no extra iteration. The retained table must still coalesce matching entries across cap boundaries. Per-byte cursors initialized at their F bucket starts cost their initial searches; each relevant component end subsequently belongs to at most one byte bucket. Source, destination-forbidden and destination-terminal cursor advances therefore total at most f, f and v respectively.

### Sharp one-shift bound

For h=1, B=V consists of a consecutive slice of occurrences of one byte c in L. For v≥2 its interior v−2 components are complete original c-runs, and its first and last components can trim only one end of their original runs. Thus d+s ≤ r−v+2 and the normalized descriptor count is at most **r+v+2**. For v=1 the initial bound already gives r+3. If U is the whole c bucket all v original runs disappear, strengthening the bound to **r+v**.

Both bounds are attained for arbitrarily large v. For the first, start L with 33; append b_i,3 for i from 1 to v−1, with b_i alternating 1 and 2. Append one final 3 and then 2v+1 zeroes. Select the F bucket of byte 3 except its two end occurrences. V consists of odd ranks from 1 through 2v−1 in the initial portion. After deletion the surviving nonzero prefix contributes v+1 descriptors. For v=1 its two byte-3 rows have a discontinuity in their compressed destinations. The long zero run maps onto alternating live and terminal ranks, producing 2v+1 more descriptors. There are exactly 3v+2 normalized descriptors from r=2v original runs. For the full-bucket bound, start with 1, append (3,2), (3,1) alternately v times, and append 2v+1 zeroes. Selecting the entire 3 bucket yields the same odd target ranks, and the result is exactly r+v. Explicit runs and counts were checked for every v from 1 to 257. ## Verification and limits

A three-shift witness with L=01000 and U=[0,2) has common prefix 000, a holed final target with two different outgoing bytes, one length-1 early return and one later return. L=101000000, U=[0,3) places an early destination at ordinal 1 between two late ones, and its source is ordinal 0. Filling the original root hull would process the early row again. L=101001000, U=[3,6) has common prefix 010, return lengths 2 and 3 in the early stream, and a surviving late word. L=10, U=[0,1) has an entirely early root after that same prefix. The targeted checker verifies source and destination ordinals and marked bytes on each visited example. [Minimal targeted witnesses](witnesses.json)

Across all binary columns through seven rows, all intervals and all valid shifts through h=4, an independent program verified 17,744 complete arcs and 67,188 marked rewind results in 10,528 cases. It includes 11,544 early arcs, 132 empty complements, 27,472 section-disjoint marks, and 3,818 marks where a nonzero virtual rewind attained h−1. Across the ten systematic real tapes the independent compressed table at h=3 has 683,429 normalized descriptors for 690,484 original runs. For every one of the 30 real shifted tables, the normalized descriptor count and both component counts agree with native Compact. The h=3 frozen table has 50,716 source intersections, 262 effective destination cuts and 237 joins, leaving 50,741 descriptors versus 51,184 original runs. At h=1 ordinal 168 needs one more descriptor than its original run count. These are construction counts; the native work on index startup, dictionary construction, second induction, marked output and memory determines decoder time.
