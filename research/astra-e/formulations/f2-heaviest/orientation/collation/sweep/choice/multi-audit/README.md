# Review of variable multi-byte cut movements

The proposed lower bound holds after removing vacuous maximum displacements and handling h=0 directly. It needs only the finite cyclic first-return arcs and their common prefix P. The original cut set need not be a rank interval for the proof itself. No source-rank order, token injectivity, common per-type offset, primitive cycle or single-cycle assumption enters the suffix count.

Let each visit i spell Wᵢ before its positive successor τ(i). Fix k=|P|, let 0≤tᵢ≤k≤|Wᵢ|, and require that the moved cuts remain distinct. They cannot overtake on their own cycle. The new positive arc from the cut at i ends at the moved cut for τ(i) and has spelling Wᵢ[tᵢ:] P[:t_{τ(i)}]. A disjoint cycle with no original visits contributes no words to either dictionary. Q counts the distinct proper suffixes of all words, including the empty suffix.

## Which cycles to reset

If on a visited cycle every tᵢ=k=|Wᵢ|, the old words on that cycle all equal P. Each cut moves exactly to its old successor and the **physical cut set and all physical word labels are unchanged**. Reset its displacements to zero. This changes the identification of old occurrences with physical occurrences on that cycle; the dictionary argument only needs the physical labels. Keep its old P words in the dictionary when forming tail sets at the new maximum. Set h to the maximum of the adjusted displacements. When h=0 the dictionary is the original one.

Suppose h>0. Choose a cut moved by h and inspect the arc into it. If the preceding word W and preceding move t have |W|>t, that arc ends in P[:h] and has length |W|−t+h>h. Thus **all h nonempty suffixes of P[:h] are proper** somewhere in the shifted dictionary, including P[:h] itself. Otherwise |W|=t implies |W|=t=h=k. The preceding cut is another maximum. Continuing backward either proves the claim or encounters a cycle already reset. Returning to the first maximum proves every cut on that cycle was vacuous. This explains why evidence about a maximal move on an unrelated cycle cannot certify the compulsory suffix set before normalization.

If all return words equal P, uniform movement by k gives Q=k, because P is a whole word and its suffix of length k is not proper. Any positive reassignment keeps the same number and total length of arcs and hence has a word of length at least k. That word alone has at least k proper suffixes, counting the empty one. For example L=1100, U=[0,2) has two tied 2-cycles each spelling 01. Both cuts moved by two simply exchange each cut with itself, giving Q=2. With t=(1,2), reset only the second cycle; h becomes one, and the two new spellings are 10 and 01, giving Q=3.

## Tail-family charge and uniform comparison

Write H=P[:h] and Vᵢ=Wᵢ[h:]. For every nonempty proper suffix u of a Vᵢ, the shifted word from i contains u P[:t_{τ(i)}] as a proper suffix. The strictness comes from u being shorter than Vᵢ. If the sets Xᵤ={u P[:j] : 0≤j≤h} are pairwise disjoint and avoid the h compulsory suffixes, each u forces its own additional state. Counting the empty state gives Q≥1+h+|S_h|.

At a normalized positive maximum some W has length greater than h, or all W=P and h<k. Under a uniform h shift the words are precisely Vᵢ H. Their proper suffixes are the empty one, all h suffixes of H, and uH for u in S_h. Hence equality for that uniform shift is exact. Each successive uniform unit movement can only decrease Q: write the current words cV. If any V is nonempty, the old set contains every suffix of every V. After movement the additional proper suffixes beyond the empty one are c and u c for nonempty proper suffixes u of V. At least one longest nonempty V was present before and is not a proper suffix of another V. If all V are empty the single word c remains. This proves Q_uniform(h)≥Q_uniform(k).

The terminal-byte test is sufficient. When the last byte of every u lies outside the alphabet of H, the last such outside byte in u P[:j] identifies the end of u. Different candidates coincide only for equal u,j, and no candidate consists solely of letters of H. For a single fixed h, this can be stronger than necessary. Across **every h=1,…,k** the simple test and the full two-part certificate are equivalent. If a suffix u ends in an offending byte x, let j≤h be the first one-based occurrence of x in P. Its old word W has |W|−h≥2, hence the singleton x is a proper suffix of W[j:]. Its family at displacement zero contains x, also the mandatory length-one suffix of P[:j]. Thus mandatory-state avoidance necessarily fails at j. Equivalently, record the first one-based index of each byte in P and check every old word W: its final byte x is absent from P, or |W|≤firstₚ(x)+1. This tests the all-h certificate in O(k+number of types) operations once lengths and last bytes are supplied. The same equivalence need not hold for a sparse set of effective maxima that omits j.

Testing only the largest possible h is insufficient. A weaker general lower bound charges the h forced states, then the minimum hitting-set size of the families Xᵤ disjoint from them. A stronger relaxation retains suffixes of one old occurrence as a group: for each distinct W at least one j must place every u P[:j] from **that same** W[h:] into the resulting union. Choices of j across old words can be relaxed independently to give a lower bound. This refinement does not require building or solving that relaxation to verify the disjoint-family certificate.

## Actual cyclic BWT counterexamples

- **Checking only k, or dropping compulsory-state avoidance.** The binary last column 101100, U=[1,3), has source words 01 and 0110 in one cycle. With P=01 and t=(0,1), the shifted words are 010 and 110. Their Q=3 beats uniform Q values (5,4,4) at h=0,1,2. The two structural tests pass at k=2. At effective h=1 the families remain mutually disjoint but one intersects the mandatory suffix 0. This is the shortest witness in the enumerated binary and ternary columns.
- **Dropping family disjointness while respecting compulsory-state avoidance.** In the one-cycle text 01010002010020 on three letters, the physical 01-cylinder is U=[4,7). In orbit order the words are 01,010002,010020. Displacements (0,2,1) keep positive arcs with effective h=k=2. All the possible tail suffix extensions avoid 1 and 01. Two candidate families intersect and the new Q is 8, below the completely shifted Q of 9. The program independently reconstructs the actual stable BWT, physical cut set, and shifted words. This targeted witness is not a minimality claim. [Targeted search](targeted.py) · [Witness and actual words](targeted.json)
- **The certificate is sufficient, not necessary.** For L=10100, U=[1,3), original words 010 and 01, P=01, the mandatory-state avoidance test fails at h=1 but the true minimum over all positive moves is still 3, attained by uniform k. The exhaustive receipt separately records an interval for which the simpler terminal-byte test fails despite the full disjointness certificate at the individual h=k; the earlier h may still fail.

The exhaustive check considers all binary L through n=10 and all ternary L through n=7, every nonempty interval, every common-prefix length k≥2, and all occurrence-specific shifts. It performs physical rank walks to verify the algebraic spelling and successor for every positive assignment. Across 5,071 columns and 133,928 interval/prefix-length cases it checked 781,460 positive assignments. There were 55,596 cases resetting at least one cycle, including 12,518 with a lower positive maximum, and 1,226 assignments improving on uniform k when some certificate condition fails.
