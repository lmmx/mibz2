# Fixed literal discovery on the ten training tapes

In the old Compact trailing orientation, the exact per-tape best singleton is the same fixed word on all ten systematic tapes, hexadecimal 6974656d22 (item followed by a quote), followed deterministically by 2c22 (comma followed by quote). Selecting it alone accounts for 3,808 of the exact 7,716 maximally removable stock queries (49.35%). Its ten per-tape counts are 493, 724, 98, 456, 86, 583, 315, 264, 524 and 265. The second-highest literal by unconditional singleton reward, snaks-order followed by quote, aggregates 1,092. Only the first pair will be tested. For a cardinality budget b, discard support sets of size greater than b, split each set's reward evenly among its member classes, and sum the b largest per-class totals. Any mask with at most b members earns at most this sum because each fully present support is covered by its full unit credit. This upper bound allows incompatible classes; for cap one every individual candidate is feasible. A separate feasible lower bound greedily drops conflicts from the credit leaders. Across ten, the one-class upper/lower both equal 3,808; cap four gives 5,468 / 5,390, cap 16 gives 6,148 / 6,018. These are training data. The independent reference generated native-oriented literals from original caps and matched their source bases to the preserved eligibility/support inputs.
