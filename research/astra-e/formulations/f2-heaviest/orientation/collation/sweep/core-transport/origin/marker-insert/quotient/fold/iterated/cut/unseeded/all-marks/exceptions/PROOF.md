# Modal predecessors and their marked clock

Let F be the sorted original byte column, Ψ its stable permutation from F occurrences to L occurrences, and LF its inverse. A mark p emits F[p], F[Ψp], … to the requested horizon N, which normally equals the physical column length n. Define C_k(r) as the next k forward bytes at r on its own cycle, including F[r]. At k=0 every context is empty. In each context c choose M(c) as the most frequent L byte among its physical rows, ties resolved by the smaller byte. An exceptional physical row has L[r]≠M(C_k(r)).

## Finite-horizon representation and proof

Set r_end=Ψ^N(p). Begin with the ID of C_k(r_end). At backward time t=0,…,N−1 the physical row would be LF^t(r_end). The clock supplies the strictly ordered exceptional pairs (t, actual byte) visited on precisely this path. Use that byte on a hit, otherwise use M(c). Write the byte at N−1−t and update the context to the byte prepended to the first k−1 letters of c. For k=0 the next state is again empty. The update is exact because L[r]=F[LF(r)] and Ψ(LF(r))=r. Induction therefore recovers the original N bytes at every primary of every stable column, including a marked cycle shorter than n and foreign cycles with different words.

Precompute the modal edge c→D(c) once. The entry clock can carry the successor state ID at an exception. For k≥1 its first byte is the actual exception byte, so a state-to-first-byte table makes a separate payload redundant. For k=0 the state contains no byte; an explicit payload or a separately agreed encoding of the nonmodal byte is necessary. For zero horizon the clock is empty and no starting state is consumed.

Alternatively supply C_k(p), the true marked orbit length ℓ and the exceptional times in one backward lap from p. Reverse that lap to obtain the forward ℓ-byte tape starting at p and copy cyclically to any N. A certified multiple of the byte period can also serve if the supplied clock covers the whole corresponding lap. Assuming r_end=p on an arbitrary column can rotate a clipped result incorrectly. Physical exceptional rows, their frequencies or their kth-order extension counts do not specify their chronological positions without further permutation work. With e marked exceptions per true lap, any N-step path visits e⌊N/ℓ⌋ exceptions plus the visits in the residual arc. Pure-default cycles require their initial state but have no clock entries.

For a default stretch of length u starting in state c, follow the functional graph D. The forward time word is a tail followed by repetitions of a directed cycle and a possible prefix. The path tail and first cycle together contain at most the number K of observed contexts. The N-byte output is built in reversed time order, so reversed chunks land at the known output address. Precompiling phases, copies and jump/spelling descriptors costs space and work; naive binary doubling with explicit strings may cost O(KN) bytes. A DAG of doubling lengths and successor IDs costs O(K log N) records, plus the cost of emitting or copying the resulting bytes. Merely admitting a long default stretch does not locate its boundary.

## Acquiring physical contexts

Physical rows for a fixed future context are contiguous in lexicographic rank. For every observed depth-j interval [l,h) enumerate the L symbols on its intersection with the original runs. For each byte b the LF images of its occurrences occupy precisely [C[b]+rank_b(l), C[b]+rank_b(h)), the interval for b followed by that depth-j context. On one depth the two partitions have at most R+K_j−1 overlapping fragments, where R is the original maximal run count. Streaming them in rank order with per-byte counters and touched symbols can output all child intervals in stable byte-bucket order. One further refinement or overlay aligns the (k+1)-contexts to k-context destinations. This builds the exact modal table without materializing k literal bytes per source row, paying the repeated run/context passes and all stored interval endpoints. The final overlay identifies exceptional physical ranges. The representation above additionally requires a marked endpoint and a temporal clock.

The construction relates to the historical k-mer spectrum ambiguity and the one-chosen-predecessor context forest in Astra 1. The earlier forest cut exceptional edges and one edge per remaining cycle to refine physical intervals. Here the missing data is supplied as marked backward event times so the default automaton spells output directly. The archived study predates this proposal. Its frozen spectrum example distinguishes the one-cycle uniqueness thresholds at 4,744 and 4,745 bytes.
