# Construct the weighted first-return bands without enumerating exceptional rows

Output records are (s,t,w,h): each of w consecutive physical exceptional ranks starting at s returns to the corresponding rank starting at t after exactly h physical LF steps. The primary is included as one synthetic exceptional rank where required. A separate order-preserving compression of source and target coordinates into the retained union produces the ordinary return-band ABI.

Let P be the number of **positive exceptional birth intervals** after splitting the original run/context overlay, isolating the synthetic primary, and, when needed, clipping an LF image at a destination context boundary. An interval of width w maps under its first LF edge onto w consecutive rows in one destination state. Its real origin labels are consecutive in the same increasing order and have equal age one. There is one birth record regardless of w. Consecutive stopped rows in any source context form an interval; across all contexts their count is at most P. In a fixed-length context the LF image for each (predecessor byte, source context) lies in one destination context. The original scan costs the number of run/context contacts and emitted positive ranges; it uses no loop over a represented width.

**DAG.** Use the same virtual anchor range, incoming packet order and stable one-edge transport as the occurrence constructor. At each stopped source range, split the incoming rope at its endpoints. An extracted leaf of consecutive real origins gives a return record of equal width, with the current source range as increasing targets and the leaf's uniform age. A virtual leaf instead annotates the corresponding anchor hole interval U by its increasing true terminal labels and final age. At an anchor destination, each arriving real leaf annotates V by its increasing real origins and entry age. The remaining virtual leaves describe a single increasing return between the complements; all share the weighted default-state-cycle duration. Whole event-free contexts can be composed first.

There are P initial real leaves and at most K virtual leaves. A split inside an affine leaf creates one extra logical fragment, regardless of its width. There are at most two splits per stop range and at most one per outgoing default packet. Complete default images give at most K positive packets plus one extra for the synthetic-primary gap. Joins, uniform age additions and rebalancing require no new logical fragments. Each terminated leaf leaves the DAG once and each final virtual or real leaf is examined once at its final anchor. Consequently the total number of direct return records and positive U,V annotation intervals is O(K+P). The tree may allocate two new nodes when replacing one split leaf, still O(K+P) nodes created.

**Stable-hole sweep by ranges.** Split the physical anchor line at annotation endpoints of U and V. On each resulting region their two membership bits are constant and each present payload is affine with slope +1 and constant age. The cumulative excess of V over U is constant, increases by one per row, or decreases by one per row. A region therefore requires at most one additional interior split to separate a positive from a negative excursion. Balanced stretches with only shared holes publish direct translations; balanced ordinary stretches are disconnected fixed rows. In an unbalanced excursion scan toward its sink (increasing ranks for positive excess, decreasing for negative). Keep the outstanding sources as an ordered rope of labellings and ages.

* A V-only range appends one affine birth interval. A U-only range drains the first w labels, charging one full default lap to each and adding the terminal annotation age. Equal-size prefix fragments zip to increasing target labels.
* A shared range joins its incoming real origins immediately to the corresponding real terminals at zero full laps. It leaves the waiting queue unchanged.
* An ordinary interval of length l with h outstanding labels adds floor(l/h) full laps to the entire queue, one additional lap to its first l mod h labels, and rotates that prefix to the end.

Every operation is the batched version of the stable FIFO proof. For negative excursions mirror increasing payloads into negative source labels while traversing the physical ranks backwards. The queued labels again increase by one within each fragment. Converting a drained segment back to increasing physical source and target coordinates preserves slope +1. The lap ages are carried unchanged through this mirror. A coincident end and start is processed exactly once with zero laps.

The U,V sweep has O(K+P) endpoint and sign subregions. Each region requires O(1) rope splits/joins and creates O(1) new logical fragments. Every drained fragment is consumed once; a partially drained fragment is charged to the split. Thus the weighted return map has O(K+P) raw records, constructible after input geometry in O((K+P) log(K+P+1)) with a deterministic balanced sequence, or expected time of that order with the independent-priority treap. The output-size bound is unconditional. Age arithmetic, leaf widths and the lap product use sufficient-width integer words. Sorting and coalescing the records in the two retained interval orders fits the same bound. Materializing or walking one temporal event per exceptional occurrence is a separate downstream operation.
