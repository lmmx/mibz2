# Retain an already sufficient ordinary cut

A nonzero diagonal mass e only weakens the *guaranteed lower bound* on the original greedy cut; it need not weaken its actual mass below m/4. Compute the original coloring from the paid ordinal/bucket overlay. Sum its outside→inside mass q. If 4q≥m, apply the existing cut directly, retaining its original alias/spelling rules. If 4q<m, use power normalization and cut its zero-diagonal result. The former branch leaves at most 3m/4 rows by the exact q, and the latter leaves at most 3(m−e)/4. Therefore this guarded constructor retains the same unconditional 3/4 progress and total-row bound. It does not require any power-index setup, alias sort or macro publication on the accepted branch. A failed provisional coloring incurs the original adjacency and coloring work in addition to the normalized coloring. If e=0 the original quarter bound certifies the branch without a separate mass check.

The work and output of this fixed quarter rule will be measured on the same original frozen primary and weighted tiny marks. The prior three nonzero-diagonal depths belong to the eager-power history; the guarded history can take different cuts and must be checked independently.
