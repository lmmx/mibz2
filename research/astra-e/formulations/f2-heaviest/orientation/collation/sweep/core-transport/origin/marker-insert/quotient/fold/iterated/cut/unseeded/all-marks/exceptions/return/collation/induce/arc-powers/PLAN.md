# Contract affine arc identities without constructing an ordinary child alphabet

Use the two ordered lists of a word-labelled interval exchange. Initially isolate p as a singleton source band. Unlike the earlier quarter-cut ordinary BWT, discard lexical class order and label each geometric arc separately. In a source band I=[a,a+w) with translation d≠0, repeated visits to the same arc form arithmetic chains. Their entry rows are I outside I+d, a prefix/suffix of length min(w,|d|). From one such row the number of steps before leaving I takes either of two consecutive values, computable by Euclidean division. The exit strips partition (I+d) outside I in at most two contiguous pieces, just a rotation inside the old image. Thus all self edges over all arc identities can be removed in two ordered linear passes, with at most 2B strips and no per-row scan. If I maps to itself identically it contains isolated fixed loops of the current map. A primary singleton there yields the terminal weighted spelling; otherwise omit it.

Select a weighted directed independent cut on the surviving geometric arc identities: XOR coloring of the two IDs on a one-step transition, conditional expectation over a linear parity mask, followed by orientation protecting the mark. Every remaining edge is between different parent arcs after the self contraction. At least one quarter of the surviving population can be removed without consecutive deletions. A three-coordinate overlay determines each surviving return in one or two old steps, with O(B) translated pieces, and radix ordering supplies both child lists. Retain exact power and concatenation spellings. The marked singleton stays selected at every pass.
