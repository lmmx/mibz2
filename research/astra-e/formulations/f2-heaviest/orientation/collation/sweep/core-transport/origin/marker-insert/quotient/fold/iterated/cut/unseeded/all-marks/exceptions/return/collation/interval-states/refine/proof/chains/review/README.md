# Independent review of free seam cycles in a fiber slab

A nontrivial support interval has endpoints at fiber boundaries. The two physical occurrences delimiting it are the last and first occurrence in different constant-predecessor fibers. A chosen free offset inside one fiber can therefore be moved anywhere in its slab without changing whether it supports such an interval. Each complete cycle selects the offset in every phase of the necklace. Keeping at least one of them preserves all external flexible supporters. Old seams in any phase mark that lane offset as special in the entire necklace.

On a slab the old source label (preceding byte, old LF destination class) is constant in each fiber. In a neutral set it suffices to evaluate these old labels inside each newly cut source interval. Multiple internal offsets create homogeneous intervening segments. Delete all except the extreme pair, then move that pair outward to the extremal allowed positions. Every row transferred to an interior homogeneous segment gives exactly one modal hit there and removes at most one hit from its former neighbor. This also covers an outside neighbor which spans two adjacent fibers and loses rows at both ends. For a single offset, each fixed segment/label count is affine in the shared offset, even when the segment has two moving ends. The sum of the per-segment maxima is convex and attains a maximum at an endpoint. Nonnegative seam charges permit removal of surplus internal cuts.

The first-lane orbit of each distinct necklace meets at least one L-run start. Otherwise moving every phase back by one physical rank preserves its LF adjacency at every step and spells the same infinite periodic sequence. This contradicts the definition of the first lane. Each such run start can belong to only one necklace.

The first fixture has the unary necklace at multiplicity two and (01) at multiplicity six. An old seam in just one phase splits the slab. The score of a chosen pair at offsets 2 and 3 rises from 10 to 11 when moved to offsets 1 and 4. Its singleton scores are 11, 10, 10, 10. The second fixture has (01) at multiplicity four and (001) at three. With old seams 3 and 4 and the cuts outside the free slab fixed, retaining both extreme offsets gives mass 14; the best singleton gives 13, and empty gives 10. At charge 1/4 per physical seam the pair strictly beats all singleton choices under those fixed outside decisions. The unrestricted global optimum may choose other outside seams.
