# Exact census of reciprocal seam pairs

This deferred screen would test whether the shortest possible mutually supporting birth can gain enough physical modal hits to cover two seams in the true ten blocks. The initial partition P refines the F-byte buckets. Charge one, two or four hits per new physical seam. Enumerate exactly the neutral selections {t,u} for which neither seam is old-supported or self-supported. Such a pair cannot be obtained through either order of singleton addition.

Write I(t)=(Ψ(t−1),Ψ(t)] for the possible source supporters. When t<u, a reciprocal pair with no individual support has I(t) strictly to the right of t and I(u) strictly to the left of u, with u∈I(t) and t∈I(u). The source-support intervals in any one F-byte bucket are disjoint and increasing in physical coordinate. Therefore t and u lie in different F-byte buckets and hence different P states. The exact neutral gain is the sum of the two independent old-target source-split gains. For t in an old physical interval J=[l,r), that gain is the modal old-label mass on [l,t) plus the modal mass on [t,r), minus the old modal mass on J. A row's old destination state already determines its preceding byte because P refines F.

Build dense stable LF, Ψ and physical P-state IDs as offline structural work. A prefix scan and reverse scan per P interval give all conditional split gains in linear total time, using a histogram with touched-label clearing. In a sweep of u from 1 through n−1, keep right-pointing eligible seams t with u∈I(t) in an ordered set. At u, remove the interval ending at the source position u−1 and add the following same-byte interval beginning at u. Both are located from LF(u−1). For a left-pointing eligible u, an ordered range query reports exactly the active t in I(u). At most one right-pointing interval per F byte is active. With σ appearing bytes and z emitted pairs the total structural work after obtaining P is O(n log(σ+1)+z), with O(n+|P|+σ) temporary storage. The experiment charges input preparation separately and makes no claim about a production path.

The campaign retained this method as a continuation after the primitive fixed-charge obstruction was proven. No real panel ran. The preregistered outputs would be the number of all reciprocal pairs and positive pairs at each charge, the best raw gain, and an achievable aggregate of independently positive pairs sharing no parent P state. Greedily use available pairs by decreasing gain with deterministic row-order ties. A parent-disjoint union remains neutral by persistence of support and has exactly additive raw mass. This licenses a lower bound on the best such union. The sum over profitable pairs, even when their parents overlap, only describes pair opportunities. It is not an upper bound on larger neutral selections. Ordinary old-supported and immediately self-supported seams are counted separately.
