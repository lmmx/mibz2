# All-primary output offsets through one ordinary cut

Build one mark-independent source-rank and local-byte-offset transport atlas per cut. On a surviving interval rank_S is a translation. On a deleted interval the old predecessor is LF(x) inside an outside source bucket, every row of which survives. Hence rank_S(LF(x)) is another translation and the byte increment is the predecessor's word length. Emit one descriptor per original incoming-run/source-bucket overlay piece and coalesce equal actions. Look for dense or disconnected counterexamples, then test every original row on the frozen primitive block after the entire ordinary-cut history. Compare the final circular offset for every row to an independently constructed dense cyclic suffix-position map. Measure total descriptors, temporary and retained bytes, maximal fragmentation, grammar and query costs. Power-pass indexing needs a separate proof. Preserve its applicability conditions.
