# Carry the owner to the next step

The implicit inverse returns the old source owner for free: it is the zero partner on a join and the current owner on a gap. Positive compact source intervals and positive compact image intervals each partition the same retained rank line. In one monotone endpoint sweep per side, record the first and one-past-last image-owner indices intersecting each compact source interval. Once the first source owner of the marked rank is located, every subsequent query only searches this known contiguous range. A singleton range requires no primary search. For k candidate image owners the first start is already known to be at or below the query rank, so at most ceil(log2 k) binary comparisons suffice. Subsequent calls require no global predecessor lookup.

Across ten first cuts with the historical byte coloring, the 617,640 source/image owner intersections cover all 6,598,608 marked steps, individually checked against the explicit permutation and its exact words. 4,107,733 queries have a singleton primary range. 1,964,139 have both a singleton primary and an empty secondary hit slice. 3,610,624 have at most two primary owners and at most one secondary hit. The sum of the two binary search worst-case counts, weighted by the visited rows, is 16,517,127 or 2.503 comparisons/step. The primary maximum is 673 candidates; the secondary maximum is 876.
