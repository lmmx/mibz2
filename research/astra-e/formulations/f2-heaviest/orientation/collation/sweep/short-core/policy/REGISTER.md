# Frozen-prefix allocation for the short-safe original-return atlas

The training input is Wikidata ordinal 0, on the heaviest quote-return source interval selected by `heavy_set(..., 1)` (stable leftmost tie). Each tested block uses that same section selection rule. The fitted object is a literal byte-prefix policy, shared unchanged across blocks. Its prefix-closed enabled set may contain prefixes of lengths 0 through 15. A missing prefix stops its source packet. An enabled live packet attempts one forward step, charging every intercepted affine Ψ run, including the lookahead that would enter the section. On that final lookahead it stops in place. We use the uncoalesced affine packets of the short-core construction at 11cc7ee. The reference is the same atlas with every prefix of length below eight enabled.

## Fitting and validation rule

Minimize distinct proper nonempty core suffixes plus the empty root, Q, subject to two independent upper bounds on ordinal 0. F counts all charged forward intersections. B counts all terminal atlas bands. Each bound equals the respective cap-eight census on that block. Post-fit direct reconstruction of the cap and literal policies must agree with their computed F, B and Q. An exact solution or a feasible incumbent with a rigorous optimization bound qualifies for a transfer experiment. Ties among equal objective policies need no secondary optimality. Record implementation tie choices, solver tolerance and limits. The training optimizer may consult the exact dense return vocabulary recovered from the original BWT. The applied policy receives the current prefix (or trie state) and Ψ geometry alone.

After fixing and committing the literal policy, evaluate ordinals 24, 48, 72, 96, 120, 144, 168, 192 and 216. Compare against cap eight on each same block. Advance to paid native testing only if total Q is at least 5% lower, both total F and total B fit the respective held-out totals, and at least seven of nine individual blocks improve Q at both of their own budgets. Report individual misses separately, including possible gains below that gate. No adaptation to these nine results. Runtime policy queries, finite-trie storage, and remaining original discovery and writer costs are additional to this geometry comparison.
