# Compose two consecutive move-to-front ranks

A bzip2 nonzero MTF rank is a permutation of a prefix of the current list. Applying two such ranks leaves their output bytes in reverse order in the first two positions. This holds across an arbitrary intervening run of RUNA/RUNB digits, which repeat the front and leave the permutation invariant. The run length and cap records associated with either literal are still due. The two tested fronts index an aligned constant shuffle table by the two ranks, move the low 16 or 32 bytes once, extract two outputs together and pay the exact original length, cap and per-byte record/row work. High ranks continue through the existing two-level list. [Proof and registrations](PLAN.md) · [Worker registration](worker/PLAN.md)

On 431 corpus blocks the production run gate selected 383 with 15,147,621 literal updates. In a single bitmap word the 16-entry prefix admits 5,860,671 disjoint pairs. The 32-entry prefix admits 7,311,529 pairs, covering 96.54% of literals, including 1,969,296 pairs separated by run symbols. Permitting cross-word pairing adds 94,743 pairs. All 961 nonzero two-bank table entries occur. The most frequent 256 account for 80.85% of selected pairs. The AVX2 table occupies 64 KiB shared. The actual candidate tried its private front on 265 blocks, accepted the marked inverse on 233, and retried 32 comprising 4,572 Huffman symbols. The maximum single-worker retained vector capacities in corpus order were 864,472 bytes for stock and 893,140 bytes across the treatment's two fronts. The extra front also has two 256-entry u32 counters and three Vec headers. Other streams with large fallbacks can retain more. Two topology-matched 36-round single-CPU panels exposed a control instability. A low-single specialization and composed low pair cost 1.272 and 1.370 of stock front CPU in the first executable. In the next executable the two-bank pair cost 0.550 of its same-binary stock control. The shared low-pair median was nearly unchanged at 69.94 and 69.30 milliseconds. The same stock routine moved from 52.38 to 96.55 milliseconds. Its normalized 853 disassembled instructions (including padding) agreed exactly, as did the normalized 901 low-pair instructions. These runs do not identify a cause of that change. The independent follow-up rotated both generated arms through the same reusable scratch and introduced the shipped library front as a reference. In three fresh processes the median library lap was 70–72 milliseconds, the copied stock 71–74, and the wide pair about 75. Those trials included all 383 record routes. Their intervals and inputs are preserved separately. The 4,410 synthetic cases exercise all low rank pairs, RLE2 weights at legal and illegal boundaries, high moves, clamped synthetic symbols, truncated streams, EOB, and independent row bounds. All three generated AVX2 implementations agreed with the frozen original on fast-path acceptance, capped records, both counters, and length where accepted. The 32-rank and 16-rank implementations passed the additional corpus front checks. At the complete worker level the treatment, matched copied stock front and original scalar inverse each passed the 338-case scorer gate. The immutable complete candidate is `01e44cbe011c1f965b0e54fa724a738083512976`. It uses the public research symbol hook, and the regular scalar fallback handles every declined stream or invalid mark. The three complete frozen workers ran against the original corpus in 18 balanced rounds at W1 and W4, one warmup per arm and worker count followed by two-second settles. All 108 observations returned 209,700,196 output bytes. The two-bank pair over scalar-cap had paired wall ratios **1.0617 [0.9098, 1.2390] at W1** and **1.0289 [0.8650, 1.2237] at W4**. Against the matching wrapper containing the unaltered stock front the ratios were 1.0442 [0.9095, 1.1989] and 1.0454 [0.9340, 1.1701]. W4 median process peaks were 66,896 KiB for paired MTF, 66,474 KiB for the copied front and 66,460 KiB for original scalar-cap. Their maxima were 69,412, 67,640 and 70,420 KiB. The shared host and broad intervals leave small effects unresolved. These direct estimates support retaining scalar-cap as the independently verified worker. A subsequent exact table identity halves the wide mask storage to 32 KiB by encoding an output's source bank in bit 7. The two input-bank controls differ by XOR with 0x80. Every one of the 30,752 two-rank destination-lane identities checked directly. The compact front passed 11,770 synthetic cases, 431 corpus front comparisons and three fresh order-balanced stage panels. Compact/wide ratios ranged from 0.985 to 1.025. The 32 KiB storage result remains independently reproducible. [Proof and complete prices](compact-proof.md)

A separate stock-anchored official scorer round repeated the corpus twice per process and ran nine paired rounds at W1 and W4. Two-bank/scorer LazyCells wall ratio was **1.0333 [0.7999, 1.3348] at W1** and **0.8265 [0.7246, 0.9427] at W4**. Median RSS ratios were 0.8877 and 0.7491. Direct treatment/opend at W4 was 0.9516 [0.7675, 1.1800]. This measures the entire coupled scalar-cap inverse and MTF treatment; the 18-round scalar-only comparisons above control for the inverse. Neither a separate MTF gain nor the target i9 objective has been verified. An independent adversarial block demonstrated a large, correct double-front fallback with 644,228 vector bytes per front and an exact complete CRC-checked libbzip2 output.
