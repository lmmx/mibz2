# Elide Huffman constructors whose group is absent from every selector

For a small declaration with at most 64 selectors, form a six-bit used-group mask from all validated selector bytes. Every block still parses and validates every length in every declared group, including unused ones. Instantiate canonical tables only for the groups appearing in the supplied selectors. Keep the order of the selected groups; before entering the body rewrite the small selector buffer to their compact indices. If all groups are present, the original selector bytes need no rewrite. Larger declarations follow the full group set. The mask and six-byte map fit on the stack; the group Vec has no greater capacity than the source. This removes 190 of 1,796 genuine Huffman constructions on the corpus. No unused table could have affected an attempted symbol, even on malformed input or when EOB is early.
