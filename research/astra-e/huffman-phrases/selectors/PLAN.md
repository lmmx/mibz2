# Charge phrase-table setup according to the declared selector budget

The first four-symbol BMI2 scanner spent less time outside table construction than stock in one attributed panel, but matched stock on complete scanner CPU. The frozen corpus has 198 blocks with fewer than ten consumed selector groups per table on average. They contain only 0.0332% of symbol output while constructing 398 of the 1,796 tables. These blocks motivate retaining the complete four-symbol route on large inputs and suppressing its extra setup on small inputs.

Choose the implementation policy solely from the header before reading its coded symbols. When the number of declared selectors is at most 64, one treatment constructs all ordinary full-ID pairs and skips three/four-symbol promotion. A second treatment retains the single-symbol LUT and canonical long/bad paths for that block. Both can emit every legal 9-bit ID, including EOB. All larger blocks use the unchanged four-symbol scatter route. The cutoff is fixed before looking at paid outcomes. No selector-use histogram, cache, scratch allocation, new output store or extra worker stage is needed by the treatments. The separate census records exact declared and consumed use per table as an audit.

Use stock, its same-code twin, original scatter, small-pair and small-single arms in one 40-round five-position-balanced stage panel after full corpus and historical malformed gates. Compare both treatments directly to stock, twin and original scatter. A small local scanner win would still need independent full-pipeline verification. This selector policy is a separate secondary experiment. The complete baseline packed pair/triple/quad measurements remain preserved.
