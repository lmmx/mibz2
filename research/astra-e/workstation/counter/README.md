## Fixed protocol

The 40 complete matched rounds use the canonical Wikidata corpus concatenated four times, producing 838,800,784 decoded bytes per invocation. The order within every round is W7, W8, W9, W10. For each W the scorer rotates the five arms by round+W and reverses on alternate five-round rotations. Every arm occupies each position eight times at each W. Every sample uses the explicit W-worker pool with 4W slots, followed by a two-second settle. All 800 rows, absolute wall seconds, worker-process CPU seconds and peak RSS survive in the bundle. There is no optional stopping or replacement of observations. An incomplete run has no decision.

For each direct reference separately, pair the candidate’s wall time to that reference in the same round and W. Within each round take the arithmetic mean of the four log ratios. Form a two-sided 95% Student t interval over these 40 round means (39 degrees of freedom), then exponentiate. The joint incremental criterion requires **both** pooled upper endpoints strictly below 1, and every per-W paired wall upper endpoint against **both** direct references at most 1.02. It also requires all three Lazy memory checks at every W: paired geometric RSS ratio upper at most 1, ratio of sample median peaks at most 1, and candidate maximum at most Lazy maximum. Report each pooled contrast, all individual per-W intervals and each guard even when the joint criterion fails. Each pooled result is a four-W matched result; each W has its own output.

The stretch rule at each W is the two-sided paired geometric wall ratio upper at most 0.80 against Lazy and all three Lazy memory checks above. Meeting the campaign objective requires all four W to pass. Ratios against opend and direct-reference RSS are reported separately. Numeric agreement between a CPU-stage study and a target-wall measurement carries no weight in these rules.
