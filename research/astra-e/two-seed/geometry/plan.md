# Two-seed marked-walk structural test

Use the original width-16 packed successor and exact spelling tapes for all 233 edge admissions. For m≥2, choose the second seed by adding floor(m/2) to the marked physical rank modulo m. Also report mark XOR 1, fixed rank 0 and fixed rank floor(m/2) as diagnostics. A diagnostic seed outside the array or identical to the mark gives a single lane. The cyclic half-shift is always a valid distinct seed for m≥2. None of these seeds inspects the orbit order.

For seeds in one cycle, measure both forward arcs before the opposite seed. In a round issuing at most one successor read per active lane, paired rounds equal the shorter arc and rounds until completion equal the longer arc. Report the maximum possible paired rounds at an ideal split, the actual fraction of that maximum, emitted literals and dictionary visits, each lane's exact byte count, and double-copy exposure if the second lane builds a separate byte vector that is joined into the final output. Aggregate these quantities as a ratio of corpus totals. Also show per-block quartiles, extremes, block 1 and all five guarded dense blocks. Record issue rounds with two nonliteral spellings and the sum of the shorter emitted payload at each paired issue. These model concurrency opportunities; they measure no CPU time, cache-transfer latency or IPC.

Check two-lane raw emission directly against the single marked cycle and check post-RLE1 results against the libbzip2 corpus oracle. In disjoint cycles, a closed lane proves the seeds belong to different cycles. Only the primary cycle contributes bytes. Test short periodic, multiple-cycle and seed-collision cases independently.
