# Physical, conjugate and phase-shifted order

Let S be the retained ranks of the width-16 edge constructor, including its isolated primary. For physical source x in S, T(x) is its next retained row under the original forward byte permutation Ψ; the emitted positive spelling W(x) covers exactly those Ψ steps. Equal W means equal step count and byte-prefix cylinder. Stable Ψ is strictly increasing on a fixed first byte, hence Ψ to the word length is increasing between any two ranks with that same prefix. Restricting sources and destinations to S preserves their relative order. This holds for equal infinite rotations and separate cycles. It proves strict destination order globally within each *exact spelling* regardless of source gaps. Numeric equality implies spelling equality. Source convexity is a separate condition. If a word starts at two rows and a different spelling lies between them, assigning one F bucket to that word in the original coordinates would incorrectly include the intervening row.

For any proposed source label and rank order, the minimal contiguous stable refinement splits at every label change and additionally at every adjacent destination descent inside a constant-label run. These splits are necessary; between successive splits labels are constant and destinations increase. Giving the pieces separate IDs yields an ordinary weighted stable column. Its incoming symbol at row T(x) is x's piece ID. In physical order, equal weights have no descents, so only the maximal spelling-run boundaries matter. Distinct refined IDs may have the same byte spelling.

Let c(x) be the retained original L byte and let σ sort the pairs (c(x),x), equivalently ranking LF(S). Conjugating T by σ preserves the graph, but the same old outgoing W is no longer a prefix of the original byte itinerary at the shifted physical rank. Whole-word classes can consequently lose stability. One *byte* phase correction instead gives the source σ(x) the spelling c(x) concatenated with W(x) minus its last byte. Indeed the removed last byte always equals c(T(x)). This new word is the forward physical spelling from LF(x) to LF(T(x)) and has the same positive length. Mark at σ(mark), skip the single prepended byte, and repeat as needed. Stable Ψ again proves global same-word monotonicity. This phase adjustment changes the weight convention from the earlier coordinate-only conjugate kernel.

The old edge factorization has a physical interval for each band: for an ordinal y running from k to k+w in the concatenated byte queues, the source is x=d+y−k and T(x)=Q(y), with Q increasing throughout a byte queue. A dense-free constructor marks the physical starts in an m-bit vector and assembles the physical band order with prefix popcounts; assign the maximal-piece IDs in that order and attach them to the original queue-order bands. Now scan the independently reconstructed retained L runs, maintaining one cursor per byte in the queue-order band list. A span of c consumes widths from that byte's bands, emitting their IDs, merging equal adjacent outputs. Since c's occurrences ordered in the retained L tape are exactly Q's c queue, this outputs the incoming label at every physical destination without dense T or a partner walk. It needs O(B+m/word+t+J) work after the original strips/bands, for B bands, t retained-byte runs and J band/span intersections; J ≤ t+B−1. Separately obtaining the retained runs requires a scan of the capped input, unless they are retained during construction. Word aliases add their own dictionary-classification cost. This excludes construction of a later discovery index, dictionary and final marked output.
