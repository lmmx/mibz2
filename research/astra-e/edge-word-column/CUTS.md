The fixed greedy XOR/gap route at 37c3bfd reduced each first root by more than a quarter while matching every marked byte of all 466 complete grammars. An old source weight is a leaf referring to an already interned byte string. Seeding refines the actual marked row into its own singleton, whose leaves share the original weight. Stock cut choice, stable alias publication and any guarded power fallback then operate on the ordinary ID column. Each join/power retains its full positive byte length. At terminal the grammar emits the marked lap, repeating and clipping to the original horizon. These are untimed structural checks on 233 real admissions. All first columns were published by the band-and-byte routines, independently of the available dense oracle.
