# Can the i9 edge worker publish an ordinary weighted column?

Yes. Its raw outgoing byte spellings have strictly increasing destinations on equal spellings, but often occupy several source intervals. Splitting these intervals gives an exact ordinary weighted BWT on the original rows. The new band/queue publisher was checked against every one of **26,567,581 original successor rows**, without using the dense successor to acquire its incoming column. A second positive construction on the union of the retained and LF-shifted ranks factors almost all its incoming through the retained byte tape. The deeper quote sections of both constructions still require millions of dependent interval queries. [Order proof and publication](PROOF.md) · [Factored positive construction](UNION.md)

All numbers below are offline exact structural counts across the 233 admissions of the frozen i9 edge worker, not time or peak-memory predictions. The joined old edge and dictionary tapes are from badb2ae. Their originating audit verified the real marked bytes against independent Segmented inversion, libbzip2 expansion and block/stream CRCs. This study separately reconstructed the retained original bytes and membership geometry from a fresh scan of the same compressed corpus, then verified every ordinary transition and all **209,697,662 marked coded bytes** against that archived oracle. It did not run a new end-to-end decoder or gate. [Frozen oracle, tape lineage, and real CRCs](../edge-displacements/README.md) · [Corpus checks](check.txt) · [Finite adversarial checks](small.txt)

Full-spelling classification assumes an interning pass and byte equality. The existing worker stores distinct terminal spellings by band. Its previous native byte-interning trial showed no complete-inverse gain compared to an identical-code twin. A new candidate hoping to exploit lexical equality needs to price recognition. The direct one-phase publisher begins after the existing queue and 827,858 endpoint bands have been discovered. Its extra retained-source capture scans 16,320,934 raw capped records. Those form 15,141,602 maximal input runs, from which the width-16 primary-preserving rule gives 14,291,364 true thin-byte spans. Physical ordering requires an m-bit band boundary directory, prefix counts and a band-ID permutation. Publication then processes 14,354,168 thin-span/band intersections; 10,814,447 have width one. A naive eight-byte (ID,width) record for each resulting full-spelling run would initialize 114,826,904 bytes over the inputs, before any index, versus 106,270,324 bytes for the original four-byte packed successor and label. A tighter record encoding or streaming reader may use less. Actual process RSS and dependent access need separate measurement. [Per-block acquisition](geometry.csv)

These dictionary counts price expanding each distinct typed return once without grammar sharing. The heavy convex piece offers little shortening of construction despite preserving an ordinary column. The quote first-byte **union** is far stronger than its most frequent individual word and contains several source classes. Any recurrence that treats all these classes as one stable outgoing symbol loses word length, spelling or order. The exact per-block section traversals account for all incidences and marked relocation. [All forced sections](sections.csv)

The distinct alternatives have separate resource profiles. The two-phase lift avoids unshuffling high IDs, but doubles the state count and increases all three tested discovery counts. A safe one-step pair rule can recognize singleton partners of convex classes with two endpoint lookups each, but even maximum-compatible selection covers only 7.98% of the original rows. Across all blocks, a single fixed source word and partner saves at most 14,871 of the 26.57 million old visits on these tapes before recognition. The positive physical union reduces high-ID publication to 339,722 runs alongside base claims, but its quote recurrence requires 16.13 million queries. None of this evidence supports replacing the i9 winner with these exact native constructions on speed or memory. ## Prior results and reproduction

The prior band/queue EF32 reader at a098919 used the same original equation for random access and went directly from bands to a 1.383-byte/row successor format. Its measured native full inverse was 1.8760× the original and its emitter 2.7001×. It had no ordinary weighted incoming publication. The coordinate-only Open conjugate at 97ed555 built a dense reindexed table. The endpoint study at 368710e pointed out that the bands in retained-byte-sorted coordinates could not simply be called ordinary BWT runs. The present construction recovers that missing order explicitly and charges its class and RLE cost. [Band/queue reader](../edge-displacements/README.md) · [Open conjugate](../open/README.md) · [First-level endpoint analysis](../formulations/f2-endpoint/README.md)

The earlier ASCII-pair patch at 28ae0cf took 15.16 million indexed reads and 8.91 million rewrites for 24.05% fewer marked hops on 228 original OpenGated admissions. The 9a93d9d anchor scheme also starts with that dense acquired graph, scans it, and pays 12 bytes/anchor plus intervening literals. The present endpoint partner theorem avoids that scan for deciding class eligibility, and permits dictionary words. It still has to construct and emit the result. Queue-span packing at 8208d50 compresses the original queues used by the dense constructor. [ASCII pairs](../pairs/README.md) · [Anchor staging](../anchors/README.md) · [Queue-span representation](../queue-spans/README.md)
