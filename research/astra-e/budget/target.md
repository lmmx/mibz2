# Phase information already measured on the i9

The sustained comparison ran LazyCells and density-gated Open-Return with the same workers, 4W slots and the same Wikidata×4 input. Its role counters are per-thread CPU seconds sampled at 0.01 s resolution. For each reading we summed the named worker threads, took ratios against that reading's wall time and then took the median of the 12 readings. This avoids combining a slow scanner sample with a fast worker sample. The scanner, workers, reader/feeder and main thread overlap. The worker column measures average occupancy per worker. On each run one scanner must perform all parsing. Given unchanged scanner CPU demand, its demand divided by the baseline wall time is a lower bound on the candidate wall ratio. At W9 and W10 its observed median Lazy share is only .027 and .022 of baseline wall below the target. Clock precision, co-running frequency, cache contention and core placement affect this calculation. The opend W9/W10 scanner percentages use the faster opend wall denominator. Interpreting .829 and .824 as floors relative to Lazy would use the wrong denominator. The paired measured opend/Lazy ratios at these four W are .9336, .9522, .9349 and .9334. The raw census matches 912 Lazy admissions out of 1,724 blocks on four copies, 1,532 on the run route and 192 on the column route. No per-worker breakdown into front, inverse, RLE1 and CRC appears in this comparison. [Matched campaign evidence](../prior.md)

The older phase capture used eager Cells with a slot per letter and measured worker task-clock across all 431 blocks. Induction plus writer accounted for (103+46)/312 = 47.8% of W1 worker CPU and (156+73)/487 = 47.0% at W4. The remaining scopes include slot preparation and inversions on fallback blocks along with the front, RLE1 and CRC. The W10 MTF estimate of 9–16% in the later writeup combines a CI stage price with assumed inflation. Both scopes and kernel differ from W7–10 LazyCells. An older W8–10 eight-copy Lazy occupancy run gave scanner shares of 61%, 65% and 68% and average per-worker shares of 77%, 80% and 74%. It demonstrates dependence on the run configuration and corpus repetition.
