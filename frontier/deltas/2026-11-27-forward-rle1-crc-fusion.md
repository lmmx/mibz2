**What was asked:** Prototype and price upstream RLE1 expansion + tiled PCLMULQDQ CRC32 fusion into forward BWT emission (1-lane FSM/tile vs 4-lane stitched/vectored with GF(2) `crc32_combine`, plus `F1b`/`F4a` interaction) against matched unfused forward controls under a `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` smooth-zone stop test.
**What I found:**
- Exactness: `13,360 / 13,360` byte-, length-, CRC32-, and `RunUnfinished`-exact checks on both baseline x86-64 and `-C target-cpu=native` (`56` synthetic+straddles including `-4..=+4` lane boundaries, all `431` wikidata blocks, all `6` `ab gen` corpora, and `116` `F1b_repair_unb` blocks).
- Census & writes: `0` 4-lane boundary straddles across all `431` wikidata blocks; `86.4%` of held-out lanes change length (`74.9%` `memmove` if stitched). Full `wikidata x4` logical `E+A9` writes drop `-53.4%` (`1,718.2 -> 799.9 MiB`) for vectored 4-lane and 1-lane, vs only `-18.6%` (`1,398.8 MiB`) for stitched 4-lane.
- **PASS (`fused_4l_tile4096_vec`, 4-lane chunked emit + zero-scratch single-pass in-place tile compaction + 64 KiB tiled CRC32 + GF(2) combine + vectored delivery):** On matched-AVX2 `-C target-cpu=native` (7 interleaved repeats, thread-CPU clock), beats `ctrl_4lane_unfused` by **`+49.9 ms` (`+3.82%` of 1,306 ms wall, `99.54 [94.24..102.91]` vs `112.35 [112.01..114.74]` ms, non-overlapping; beats `floor_4lane_plus_crc` `101.20 ms` via L2-hot CRC)** warm — passing both Branch 1 (`>= 39.18 ms`) and Branch 2 — and **`+31.8 ms` (`+2.43%`, `139.89 [139.74..143.89]` vs `150.98 [143.98..163.70]` ms, non-overlapping, matching `floor_4lane_plus_crc`)** cold — passing Branch 2 (`>= 25 ms` + `-53.4%` writes). On baseline x86-64 (where `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` handicaps the fused scanner to SSE2 vs shipped AVX2 `src/rle.rs`), `fused_4l_tile2048_vec` still passes warm (`+46.4 ms`, `+3.55%`, non-overlapping) and leads cold (`+10.9 ms`, `t1024_defcrc_vec` non-overlapping `+10.8 ms`). Guard corpora pass: `onebyte` `-17..-31%` faster, `text` `-4..-5%` faster native / neutral base, `runs` neutral.
- **Scoped STOP** for three alternatives: 1-lane fused loses `-73..-186 ms` to 4-lane unfused on Open (and `-11..-23 ms` cold even on `F1b`/`F4a`); inline FSM + `WordMeta` (`0.24%` run words, `5.25 ms` build) regresses `-18..-255 ms`; 4-lane stitched loses to `74.9%` `memmove` (`-18.6%` writes, `-23..+11 ms`).
