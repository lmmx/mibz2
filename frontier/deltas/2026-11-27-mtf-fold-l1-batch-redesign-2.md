**What was asked:** Integrate PR #1132 (the L1d-batched rewrite of the `mtf-fold` fold): independent B5 review of its new AVX2 `unsafe`, bring it current, apply the frozen stop_test honestly, merge or stop.
**What I found:**
- **B5 review (`src/runs/fold.rs`).** The bitmap pass, MTF steps, `count!`/`seek!` reads and every bail check match shipped `runs::front_avx2` line for line. The batch writes are bounded by `out < out_end` at every iteration's start. The state carried across batches (`wi`/`w`/`tz`, with `after`/`spw` recomputed from `wi`) resumes at an unprocessed literal, and oversized runs leave in L order after their batch. One soundness defect, fixed here: `fill_batch_avx2` does aligned `_mm_load_si128`/`_mm_store_si128` on `BatchState::front`, whose SAFETY comment said "offset 0", but the struct was `repr(Rust)`. Today's rustc put `front` at offset 256, 16-aligned only by luck. Now `#[repr(C, align(64))]`, so the stated invariant holds by construction. `open.rs`/`outq.rs` add no `unsafe`. The sink's no-merge fast path is sound under its injectivity argument: each literal moves a byte distinct from the previous front, and the opening and oversized runs are flushed first.
