**What I found:**
- **Cap-127 splitting & memory traffic (`wikidata`, 233 Open blocks, `209.70 MB`; `wikidata ×4`, 932 Open blocks, `838.79 MB`):** Only **1.14%** of maximal runs (`172,682 / 15,141,602` on `×1`) exceed 127, yet they hold **76.19%** of all bytes (`50.94%` in just `0.25%` runs `> 1024`, max `29,665`) and inflate `runs: Vec<u16>` by **+7.79%** (`+1,179,332` extra records on `×1`; `+4,717,328` on `×4`). Meanwhile `95.01%` of runs are thin (`≤ 16`, `71.67%` `w == 1`). Intermediate `runs` is `119.4 / 202.8 / 361.8 KiB/block` (`p50/p90/max`, **`124.52 MiB`** on `×4`), spilling the 256 KiB L2 alongside `syms` (`188–390 KiB`) + bitmaps (`24–49 KiB`), and A5's `counts[256] + per[256]` (`2 KiB/blk`) are never read by `OpenCells`. Admission sensitivity (`maximal_runs` vs capped `records.len()`): 0 blocks change Open vs LazyCells routing on any corpus (3 dense-band Wikidata blocks shift `Cap::dense → Cap::NONE`), and exact `recs` can be tracked cheaply on the 1.14% split runs.
- **A6a decomposition (`wikidata ×4`, 5 interleaved repeats; VM A5:A6a = `322.1 : 547.0 ms` = `37.1% : 62.9%`, matching i9 `character-stage1` `132.4 : 200.3 ms` = `39.8% : 60.2%`):** `OutQ16::push` is **61.3%** (`336.4 ms`, `8.41×` 32B-iota write amp; adding a `w == 1` 2B branch regresses A6a by **−10.9%** due to mispredictions), classify+`edges` is **36.8%**, and `maximal()` alone is **7.0%** vs 4B packed maximal (`0.930×` `[0.897..0.948]`, `19.2%` vs 8B unpacked).
- **Direct A5+A6a fold (100% bit-for-bit A6a state-identical on all 932 blocks, `wikidata ×4`):**
  - `folded_scalar / unfolded_scalar` = **`0.767×`** `[0.762..0.773]` (−23.3%); `folded_bitmap_u128 / unfolded_bitmap_u128` = **`0.862×`** `[0.857..0.874]` (−13.8%); `p2_folded / p2_unfolded` (pre-built bitmaps, pre-sized slice writes, L2-hot `runs`) = **`0.816×`** `[0.782..0.827]` (−18.4%, saved **`+239.8 ms`** = **27.6%** of `prod_a5_avx2 + prod_a6a`; `27.9%` on `×1`). Cross-corpus `p2_folded / p2_unfolded`: `pattern` `0.046×` (−95.4%), `text` `0.833×`, `runs` `0.899×`, `records` `0.988×`.
  - **Key mechanism:** Beyond A5 emit (`+15.6 ms`, 1.8%) and `maximal()` (`+38.4 ms`, 4.4%), eliminating the `124.5 MiB` L2/L3 round-trip **+ OoO ILP overlap** saves **`+185.8 ms`** (21.4%): `OutQ16::push`'s marginal cost drops **46%** (`336.4 → 181.9 ms`) as its scattered 32B stores hide behind MTF's serial register chain.
