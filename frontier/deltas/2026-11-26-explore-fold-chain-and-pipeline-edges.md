**What I found:**
- **Falsification try on `mtf-fold-build`: its expected room rests on overlap measured behind an MTF chain ~3x slower than production.** Every folded arm in `mtf_fold_census` runs on the safe `MtfU128` list. Here (4-vCPU Xeon @ 2.1 GHz, container-only, wikidata x1, 233 Open blocks, medians of 5 and 11 repeats): `p2_mtf_core` alone (no A6a work at all) is 132.9 / 135.1 ms, against `prod_a5_avx2` 44.4 / 43.2 ms *including* its pass 1: the stand-in chain is >= 3.0-3.1x slower (the census said ~2.5x). The best folded arm, `p2_folded`, is 163.2 / 158.6 ms, **1.50-1.53x slower than production unfolded** `prod_a5_avx2 + prod_a6a` (106.4 / 103.6 ms). So the census never beat production; its 27.6% is a ratio between two slow-chain arms, scaled onto production's ms.
- **Split the claimed saving by whether it needs the overlap.** The census's own decomposition here: (1) A5 emit 9.30 / 6.56 ms, (2) `maximal()` rescan 5.06 / 4.67 ms, (3) round-trip + OoO overlap 27.9 / 26.0 ms. Only (3) depends on idle issue slots in the chain, and a 3x faster chain leaves fewer. Without (3) the census prints a "conservative floor" of 14.2% / 10.8% of A5+A6a = **2.53-3.68% of the 1w wall** here, and 1.5-1.6% on its original x4 VM run. K1 kills at an F/none 95% upper bound above 0.975, i.e. it needs about 2.5% plus noise. **Zero-overlap room straddles the K1 line on both machines.** Scaling (3) by the chain ratio (1/3) gives ~5-6% of 1w wall instead, a pass. Verdict: not falsified (the frozen i9 stop test still decides), but the "40-85 ms" expectation is an overlap bet, not a floor: the build should first check, locally, that its AVX2-folded arm beats `prod_a5_avx2_plus_a6a` (the census's arm of that name), not `p2_unfolded`.
- **Off the map: pipeline fill and drain (null).** From the pooled-scan bundle (x4, 1724 blocks, 3 runs a cell), arm `none`: drain idle summed over all workers is 0.01 ms at k=6,7,8,10 and 0.47 ms at k=9; startup idle is 8.7 / 10.9 / 15.2 ms summed at k=6/8/10, a flat ~1.4-1.5 ms a worker, 0.70% of the 8W wall (193.7 ms). Not a lever: the last blocks are drained inside the scanner's own tail, and fill costs one first-block latency. Drain does grow in the pooled arms (`P` 4.5-25.0 ms, `PC` 12.8-31.1 ms summed at k=8-10), one more cost of a design already killed. `S`/`SC` show ~0 for both, as their oracle supplies blocks.
