**Policy (decided here):** TTL 2 h for "fresh"; older is "stale or unknown", never dead or available. Unchanged records re-push at most every 15 min; any change pushes at once. Updates are a fast-forward chain with `--force-with-lease` on the last known SHA, one re-read and retry on CAS loss. Arrival and departure prune at most 3 records (departed > 2 h, or unseen > 7 days), each by CAS on its exact SHA, so a refresh between read and delete survives and a pruned owner recreates its record. Records unseen 7+ days are counted as "expired" so undeletable ones don't inflate the stale count. At most 256 records are read, one `cat-file --batch`; every presence git call has a 30 s timeout and no failure changes a command's exit code. No hold/* or admission-epoch write, no slot, no CI (every push trigger is filtered to master/main).
