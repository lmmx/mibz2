**What was asked:** Characterise delivery's serial copy-out (839 MB) across four options (baseline `io::Read`, zero-copy block lending, caller-provided buffers / `read()` into user slice, multi-thread copy / L3 working-set control) and propose a design with costs.
**What I found:**
- **Premise check (`#952`/`#954`, verified in `c71c4a56` `runs.jsonl`):** The "48 ms flat" floor was a cold-store oracle artifact (`cc8ba137` `SFCMR` reading 853.5 MiB cold from the 209.7 MiB `R` store). Real i9 `copy_out` is **29.1 ms** (1W), **27.6 ms** (6W), **34.0 ms** (8W), degrading to **43.7–77.9 ms** at 10W (`ready_bytes` hwm `12.0–16.3 MiB` spilling 20 MiB L3), while `read_wait` stays **65–169 ms** (so delivery harms 9–10W wall indirectly via L3 eviction + reader SMT cycles, not yet as a serial floor). Zero-copy lending ALREADY exists publicly on `BzReader` (`BufRead::fill_buf`, `src/read.rs:339`) and `#[doc(hidden)] pub` on `StreamDecoder::read_block` (`src/parallel.rs:880`).
- **Option 1 (`io::Read` buffer sweep, 838.8 MB, 7 repeats, SHA-256 verified):** Warm L3 (4.29 MiB): `8 KiB` pure copy `35.8 ms` (23.4 GB/s) vs `64 KiB` `59.4 ms` vs `4 MiB` `71.5 ms`; with 4-lane `Hash`, `64 KiB` (`99.3 ms`) beats `ab.rs`'s `4 MiB` (`122.5 ms`, −19%) because 879 KiB blocks spill 256 KiB L2 before hashing. Cold DRAM (200 MiB): `4 MiB+Hash` = `156.5 ms` (`64 KiB` = `143.1 ms`); pre-faulted contiguous 839 MB `Vec` = `151.3 ms` warm / `189.4 ms` cold.
- **Option 2 (Zero-copy lending vs `read(4M)`):** On `Hash` / `CRC32`, lending is **0.48–0.51×** warm (`58.8`/`65.0 ms` vs `122.6`/`128.5 ms`) and **0.62–0.64×** cold (`100.6`/`101.3 ms` vs `157.7`/`162.5 ms`); combined with L3-hot residency, **2.68× faster** (`157.7 -> 58.8 ms`, projecting i9 `~13–17 ms` warm). End-to-end on `wikidata x4` (2T, 5 repeats): `BzReader::BufRead` **−5.73%** (−98.1 ms), `read_block(recycled)` **−4.31%** (−73.9 ms) vs `BzReader::read(4M)`. Unrecycled `Vec<Vec<u8>>` regresses **+76.5%** (+1311 ms) from 1,724 fresh page-faulted allocations—proving recycling is mandatory. Also spotted double copy in `src/python.rs:121-139`.
- **Option 3 (Caller buffers / `read()` into `&mut [u8]`):** `read_block(spent)` already pools `Vec<u8>` with `0/431` reallocations on `wikidata.bz2` (`shift > 0` on 13.7%, max `193 B`), whereas fixed non-`Vec` slabs break on bzip2's 51.8× worst-case RLE1. `read()` into `&mut [u8]` fails on 54.1% of blocks for 64–512 KiB buffers, and deferred serial `unrle+CRC` is **1.85× slower warm** (`223.6 vs 121.2 ms`) / **1.62× cold** (`254.9 vs 157.2 ms`, ~2.5× on i9). Dominated.
- **Option 4 (MT copy vs L3 working set):** Per-block 2T `memcpy` is **0.66–0.75× slower** (`109.7`/`143.7 ms` vs `72.7`/`107.8 ms`); batch-8 2T into 839 MB `Vec` gets `1.49–1.53×` only on idle cores. Working-set sweep across VM's 45 MiB L3 shows onset at **`W/L3 = 0.36×`** (`16.3 MiB`) and 97% cold saturation by **`0.72×`** (`32.6 MiB`), scaling onto the i9's 20 MiB L3 (`7.2–14.4 MiB`) right where `c71c4a56` degrades (`6.9–16.3 MiB`). `slots` sweep warns `slots=1-2xT` stalls **+9.4..+71%** on bimodal blocks: throttle `READY_BYTES` (2–4 MiB) via `Cap`, NOT low `slots`.
