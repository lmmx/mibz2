Because `21.9%–32.7%` of bursts (`43%–50%` of idle-ms) end on `3 µs` runts, filtering helper claims to `full_only` via `Finder`'s O(1) candidate byte gap (`>= 512 B`) cuts helper handoffs `−35%..−41%` (`105 → 68` at `145/135`; `195 → 116` at `145/125`) at identical wall time.
- **Q2 (work-bound capture, `k = 6` safety, `140 ms` contour):**
  - Realized 8W wall obeys `wall_8W ≈ (8*W'/8 + S')/9 + 4.85 ms` (`2.62 ms` startup + `1.15 ms` validated `#1035` handoff residual + `0.83–1.18 ms` scheduling/drain tail), or `+3.50 ms` with small chunk 0 (`in_wait = 0.05 ms`).
  - **Zero `#942` regression at `k = 6..7`:** Priority-1 `job_queue` draining limits `k = 6` to `3–4` helper blocks (`0.5–0.7 ms`), giving `0.997–0.999×` vs shipped serial across all regimes (vs `#942` `P`'s `1.105–1.149×`). At `k = 8..10`: `0.934 / 0.855 / 0.882×` at `145/135` (`141.05 / 132.69 / 138.59 ms`) and `0.877 / 0.801 / 0.830×` at `145/125` (`132.00 / 124.19 / 129.53 ms`).

Serial-scanner overhead divides by `k + 1 = 9` (`+5 ms` on scanner adds only `+0.4–0.7 ms` wall), while startup/lock stalls hit 1:1.
