**What was asked:** Price Open's Rauzy machinery end-to-end (`A6a + A6b + A6c + A7`, 1,068 ms of the 1,306 ms 1w i9 wall) across (1) thin/thick `LIMIT` sweep + adaptive rules, (2) word representation (SLP copy/DFS vs flat `spelling`) & dictionary dedup, and (3) cheaper induction steps (dependent-load chain test, prefetch, interleaving, compaction), under the frozen 3.0% (39.18 ms) 1-worker wall gate and exact bytes.
**What I found:** (7 interleaved repeats on `wikidata`, 233 Open blocks; 5 repeats on `wikidata ×4`, 932 blocks; all exact arms 100% byte-identical to `bwt::walk` on every block; 2-vCPU Xeon @ 2.30 GHz VM pinned to CPU 0, scaled to i9 anchors.)
- **Idea 1 (`LIMIT` sweep & adaptive rules) — STOP:** Production `LIMIT = 16` is already at the global `C+E` optimum (`limit_12` `1.005×`, `limit_8` `1.018×`, `limit_4` `1.096×`, `limit_2` `1.308×`, `limit_24` `1.038×`, `limit_64` `1.169×`). Lowering `LIMIT` to 2 cuts `m` −47% yet leaves `lit%` invariant at **81.6–82.2%** while exploding `A6b` 3.6× and `spelling` 6.6× (`281 KiB/blk`, spilling L2). Under fixed forward dispatch, `fwd_limit_16` wins on 158/233 blocks. Adaptive rules (`adapt_density` `1.001×`, `adapt_hist` `1.054×`, `adapt_per_byte` `1.073×`) and even a zero-cost per-block oracle (`0.966×` prod = `+2.78%`, confounded by `m<65536` switching 71 blocks to forward; `0.979×` fixed-forward = `+1.45%` 1w wall) fail the 3% gate.
- **Idea 2a (SLP during emission) — KILL:** Controlled against `prod_forward_emit`, skipping flat `spelling` cuts `A6b-dict` −41% but `slp_emit_copy_out` (`out.copy_within` from 900 KiB `out`) adds +25–26% to `A7` (**1.077–1.080× slower**); `slp_emit_dfs` is **1.556–1.562× slower**; unmemoized A6b expansion (`no_memo_expand`) is 1.012–1.021× slower.
- **Idea 2b (dictionary dedup & slack trim) — STOP:** Trimming 32.3% `memo_dedup_slack` (`42.4 → 28.7 KiB/blk`) is neutral (`0.998–1.012×`, since 42 KiB fits L2). Skipping `contents` dedup (`dict_no_contents_dedup`) saves 15% of `A6b-dict` with zero `A7` penalty, but caps at `+8.7..+12.9 ms` (**+0.67–0.99%** 1w wall < 3%).
- **Idea 2c (pre-shifted `inline_back` in `Dictionary`) — PASS:** Pre-computing right-aligned `inline_back` (~1,059 entries/blk) cuts full `A7` (with real bidi pointer-chase walk!) by **−15.0% to −16.9%** at ~0 `A6b` cost: `C+E` **`0.9353×`** on `wikidata ×4` (`2.7489..2.8389` vs `2.9662..3.0459`, zero spread overlap; `0.9284×` on `wikidata`), saving **`+76.4 ms` anchored / `+69.1 ms` prop (+5.3–5.9% of 1w wall)** (~`10.4 ms/wkr` at k=8, ~2–4 ms wall alone, ~10 ms with `S ideal`). Note: superseded if `forward-emission` replaces bidi (`prod_forward_emit` reached `0.856–0.871×`, `+10.9–12.1%`).
- **Idea 3 (cheaper induction steps) — STOP:** Not a dependent-load chain: `ablat_no_join` drops steps **−50.3–51.3%** (`23.76 → 11.67 ns/step`); `aw == bw` is **36.98%** of steps (`26.58%` 2-bit miss on `aw <= bw`); L2-fit (`≤120 KiB`) vs `>200 KiB` differs only 5.3% despite 27% of blocks exceeding 256 KiB L2; prefetch/hoist (`1.14–1.22×`) and 2/4-way interleaving (`1.23–1.68×`) all regress even inside L2. Compact 8 B SLP rules cut steps −34% but double `A6b-dict` (net 1.11× slower). Best layout (`steps_inline7_node20`, 20 B node with 7 B inline word, arena `231 → 204 KiB`) cuts steps **−17.4–18.4%** and `A6b` **−12.5–13.3%** (`+29.5..+32.4 ms` = **+2.26–2.48%** 1w wall; ~`4 ms/wkr` at k=8), still below the frozen 3% gate (C2 enforced).
