### Root Cause
1. **`trim_cell` (`src/parallel.rs:3603-3613`) hardcodes only even pinned counts `{4, 6, 8, >=10}`:** odd pinned counts `5T`, `7T`, and `9T` return `None` and fall through to un-trimmed `Plan::Workers(t)`. At `5T` and `7T` (`< LAZY_FROM_THREADS`), that selects `5` and `7` `EagerCells` workers with zero core left for the serial scanner: `7T` explodes to `0.103 s` wall (`+27.2%` slower than `6T` `0.081 s`), `0.611 s` CPU (`+43.4%`), `129.7 MB` RSS (`3.25×` `6T` `40.2 MB`), and `113.4 ms` `runq_s`; `5T` hits `0.114 s`, `0.512 s` CPU, `97.5 MB` RSS, and `123.9 ms` `runq_s`; `9T` runs 9 `OpenCells` workers with `60.1 ms` `runq_s` and `51.5 MB` RSS.
2. **Stale `LAZY_FROM_THREADS = 8` (`src/parallel.rs:3001`):** calibrated for pre-`v0.5.7` `LazyCells` and never updated after `OpenCells` replaced `LazyCells` (Journal §76 only patched `unpinned_cell` on the exact uncapped Linux i9-10900K). Every non-i9 machine, cgroup-quoted container, and non-exact affinity mask still runs `EagerCells` at `4T–7T`, where physical i9 `kernel-count` already proved `OpenCells` wins by `-7.8%` (`4W`), `-16.6%` (`5W`), `-26.3%` (`6W`), and `-36.1%` (`7W`) at `0.39–0.49×` peak RSS.
3. **Secondary bugs identified:** `window()` (`src/parallel.rs:3507-3513`) fails to scale slots when `plan_with` caps `Plan::Workers(n)` below `threads` (`trimmed == false`, producing `80` slots for `8` workers on an 8-core non-i9 at `t = 20`); and `phys >= 11` non-i9 machines still enter `Cooperative` `Plan::Teams { k: 2 }` at `t >= 11` (`2.85–4.53×` slower in `45ae3ff3`).
