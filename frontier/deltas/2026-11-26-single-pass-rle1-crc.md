**What was asked:** Census RLE1 block structure (`runs == 0`, `shift`, `min_grown`, `reserve_exact` reallocs) and per-pass A9 breakdown on `wikidata` (`×4`) and `ab gen` corpora, prototype single-write-pass and single-pass tiled RLE1+CRC32 designs byte-identical to shipped `rle::unrle_in_place + crc32`, and evaluate the frozen stop test (≥35% A9 cut at 1W OR ≥2.0% worker CPU saved via LLC/DRAM at 8–10W).
**What I found:** **Verdict: STOP.** All 15 arms verified 100% byte- and CRC32-identical across synthetic edge cases and every block of all 7 corpora. Neither frozen gate can be met by a standalone RLE1+CRC pass on this 2-vCPU Haswell Xeon (32 KiB L1d / 256 KiB L2 / 45 MiB L3, AVX2 + PCLMULQDQ):
- **Census (`wikidata.bz2`, 431 blocks, 199.99 MiB):** `runs == 0` is 196/431 blocks (45.5%) but **0.0% of bytes** (all runts); `runs > 0, shift == 0` is 176/431 (**74.7% of bytes**); `shift > 0` (4-pass route) is 59/431 (**13.7% blocks, 25.3% bytes**, max `193 B`), of which only 12/431 (**2.8% blocks, 5.1% bytes**) have `min_grown >= 0` (backward-inplace eligible; 47/59 are mixed shrink+grow). `reserve_exact` reallocs under `src/parallel.rs:1210` (`n + n/16 + 64`) are **0/431 (0.0%)** on `wikidata` (vs 14/14 on `text.bz2`, `len/n = 1.148×`). Of 12,029 `wikidata` runs, **89.4% have `extra == 0`** (`first_mut_run/n` p50 = `0.055`), forcing **90.8% of bytes** (`726.0 MiB` on `×4`) to be compacted in place.
- **L1d compute floor & per-pass breakdown (`wikidata ×4` = 838.80 MB, 7 interleaved repeats, `-C target-cpu=native` exact AVX2 + PCLMULQDQ):** L1d-hot PCLMULQDQ `crc32` (`61.76 ms`) + L1d-hot AVX2 scan (`41.98 ms`) alone total **`103.74 ms` = 72.3%** of shipped per-block L3-warm A9 (`143.49 ms`, `[139.61..152.52]`), capping even a zero-write RLE1 at **−27.7%** (< 35% gate). Isolated passes L3-warm: `find_runs` `56.68 ms` (40.0%), `shift-up` `7.51 ms` (5.3%, confirming `third-pass.md` §3), `expand` `31.31 ms` (22.1%), `crc32` `60.44 ms` (42.6% — compute-bound at L1d speed even from L3; shipped 64K tiled CRC already overlaps ~14 ms).
- **Prototype A/B (`wikidata ×4`, native AVX2 | baseline x86-64):** Best single-pass in-place (`sp_inplace_q_tiled16k`, L1d lookahead queue + 16K tiled CRC, zero extra L3 buffer) runs **`138.43 ms` (−3.52%, overlap)** L3-warm and **`182.96 ms` (−6.12%, non-overlap FASTER)** cold-stream vs `143.49 / 194.90 ms` shipped (`−2.67% / −1.42%` baseline). `backward_inplace_tiled32k` is `−1.56% / −0.24%` (noise). Out-of-place (`shipped_oop_avx2_sep_crc` and `sp_oop_fused_tiled*`) regresses **+13.6%..+31.9%** warm and **+17.3%..+56.1%** cold native while doubling per-worker L3 footprint (`0.86 → 1.72 MiB`). (Single-pass in-place does win `−18%..−30%` on synthetic `runs`/`onebyte` and `−7%..−12%` on `pattern`/`random`/`records`.)
- **8–10W LLC/DRAM accounting:** Single-pass in-place cuts logical writes **−20.8%** (`916.4 → 726.0 MiB` on `×4`, −190.4 MiB shift-up) and total logical R+W −55.5% (`3,432.7 → 1,526.0 MiB`). Against real `c71c4a56` `none`/`S` A9 (`102.8 ms` = 7.1% worker CPU @ 8W; `131.3/127.7 ms` = 8.3% @ 10W, `third-pass.md` §1), −6.1%..−20.8% bounds 8–10W worker CPU savings to **0.4–1.7%** (realistically **~0.6–1.1%** after SMT overhead) — below the ≥2.0% gate.

**What's next:** Standalone RLE1+CRC optimisation is closed. Breaking the `crc32 + scan + compact` compute floor (~87% of A9) requires fusing RLE1 expansion + tiled CRC32 upstream into `forward-emission` (eliminating the post-BWT scan and 726 MiB compaction pass once BWT emits strictly forward).
**Caveats:** Timed on a 2-vCPU Haswell Xeon VM (no vPMU, 45 MiB shared L3), not the 10-core i9-10900K (20 MiB L3); however, the L1d-hot compute floor (−27.7% ceiling vs 35% gate) and the exact −20.8% write-traffic bound against real i9 `none`/`S` A9 (≤1.7% vs 2.0% gate) close both branches independently of VM hardware.
