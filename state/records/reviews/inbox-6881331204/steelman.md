# Steelman: in-sweep equal one-row cell elision

Inbox [6881331204](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/160#issuecomment-6881331204); Coordinator scope [6881329630](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6881329630), proposal [6881317756](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6881317756), steelman rule [6880672957](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6880672957). Paper analysis only. "P" below is a proof obligation or source proof as stated; "C" an existing count; "E" an estimate. No new candidate, count, timing, registration or gate.

## Mechanism and proof obligations

Keep the existing right-end sweep and its two slot orders. At the point a cell would be made, test **ra = rb = 1**; merely one-row on one side is insufficient. The top and bottom interval pieces then each have one point, so their induced translation is a single point and the concatenation w_A w_B can be represented as a directed successor link rather than a materialised pair of slots/rule. This is the narrow case MONODROMY §6.4 calls elidable. Record the endpoint/byte or word reference needed to emit the ordered itinerary, and carry the marked origin Z through the link: the mark cannot be dropped merely because no rule was written. Existing unequal cells take the old path. The next front must see the same endpoints, predecessor slots, lengths and closing condition as if the equal cell had been materialised. **P required:** prove this local simulation for all three branch outcomes and for closed cycles, including arbitrary non-BWT columns and the `A=B` drop rule. The journal proves the old sweep's invariant and mark transport; it does not prove this new representation.

A deferred walk starts at each indegree-zero linked chain, then follows each successor once, consuming its ordered word/byte and phase. For a closed chain, use an explicit cycle start, and select the one containing marked Z when appropriate. Origin is not just a chain ID: journal §2 tracks `(α,o,v)`, or alternatively marks the isolated Z and descends marked rules to its phase `j+1`; the elided edge must preserve that exact offset and rotation. The final writer must still handle long factors, first-occurrence placement, copies, repetition and CRC, and write all n output bytes. A link must never be traversed once **per grammar occurrence**: that could multiply z by DAG fan-out. Resolve each linked point once into a stable occurrence/position (or prove an equivalent one-touch schedule), then let existing copy logic reuse the result. **P required:** a monotone consumed flag/indegree or equivalent, O(1) discovery per edge, no rescanning chains after a new rule, and a bound on all writer revisits. A chain-start scan of O(r+z) metadata is admissible; a scan of n rows is not hidden in the claimed metadata bound.

## Counted opportunity, with cohort correction

[MONODROMY §6.4](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/docs/journal/monodromy/2026-11-10-rauzy-veech.md) counts 1.812r cells on 233 Wikidata blocks, 1.432r one-row, **0.842r equal one-row**, leaving 0.970r cells if all eligible ones are linked. This is C for *that* older cohort, not a current Cells count. The pinned [phase census](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b5617a8/docs/journal/2026-11-14-cells-census/summary-wikidata.json) and its 431 block records give **196 admitted, n=176,398,965, r=12,101,142, cells=20,456,299 =1.6904r** (direct sum of `census-wikidata.jsonl`). Applying 0.842r to those blocks is E, about 10.19 million linked points, 0.05776 per output byte, leaving **0.8484r**, *not* 0.970r. Exact eligibility on the current admitted cohort is uncounted. Text/pattern admit none; refused and fallback costs cannot be wished away.

The same census gives admitted inclusive Ir: slots 790,102,938 (4.479/n), induction 1,155,202,206 (6.549/n), writer 676,350,531 (3.834/n), serial share 0.742. Induction averages **56.47 Ir per existing cell** on this cohort. MONODROMY's later historical step is about **64 instructions/cell**, but neither average identifies the equal branch's own `c_cell^eq`. A naive 0.842r × 56.47 is ~575 million Ir or **3.26 Ir/n** before link/visit/bookkeeping; 64 would be **3.70 Ir/n**. These are E opportunity scales, **not savings or upper bounds**: the equal branch may be cheaper, and the induced writer may cost more. The test on all examined steps, origin tracking, link writes, chain discovery, walk, capacity and fallback must be subtracted. The existing writer's 3.834 Ir/n and unavoidable output are retained. A total whole-reader net cannot be inferred from this stage Ir or from the historical 15% kernel ceiling. At 10 workers the critical worker and shared bandwidth matter, not aggregate Ir.

The [memory census](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/95b98eda/docs/journal/2026-11-14-cells-memory/README.md) counts 0.1025 distinct 64 B lines per output byte; slot orders 0.0360+0.0311=**0.0671**, 65% of total and 70% of modelled 2 MiB misses. If the old cohort's fraction 0.842/1.812=0.465 applies *and* elided cells are proportionate in slot lines, the avoided footprint is **0.0312 distinct lines/n** (~2.0 B of line footprint per output byte), leaving 0.0713 lines/n before link metadata. This is E, not an upper bound or measured DRAM saving. Two 24 B slots per cell suggest 48 B of logical slot writes removed per eligible cell, **~2.77 B/n** under the same z proxy; actual stores/readbacks, line sharing, slot reuse and a 20 B diet change that arithmetic. A 4 B next index plus any indegree/mark/phase/position metadata adds at least one link store and a later load per eligible point, and extra metadata lines and peak live allocation. The old slot buffer must not remain fully reserved if RSS is claimed; replacing 48 B of touched slot storage with 4–16 B of live link metadata could reduce footprint, but no peak bound exists yet. Output writes (at least n logical bytes), grammar arena/rules and fallback storage stay.

## Work bound and physical break-even

Literal **total** `W=O(S+r+z)` cannot include the writer/output: n bytes must be emitted, and n may dwarf S+r+z (the journal's Fibonacci case has n≈900,000, r=16, S=175). The defensible target is **metadata/induction work `O(S+r+z)`; complete work `O(n+S+r+z)`**, with the existing writer's copy/repetition terms explicitly retained. Old cells S and initial runs r are counted. The new bound is conditional on one O(1) eligibility test per examined cell, one link per eligible cell, O(r+z) chain-start discovery including closed chains, one deferred visit per linked point and no DAG re-expansion. The old sweep's monotone step count gives S; it does not establish the new link/writer bound. Fallback must be bounded by the existing admission/cell budget and must either reuse the safe old path without duplicate full construction or charge the paid prefix. Peak memory is O(S+r+z+n) in the broad sense, but the constant and simultaneous old/new lifetimes are unresolved.

Break-even per eligible cell, on the **critical block**:
`c_link + c_visit(L, start geometry) + c_bookkeeping + Δc_writer + Δc_fallback < c_cell^eq`.
Also require positive net stream bytes after link stores/reads, and no countervailing peak/RSS loss. The old one-container §6.4 measurement: deferred walk alone **5.2 ns/point at one lane**, **1.4 ns/point at four evenly spaced starts**, versus historical **~7.7 ns/cell** average. On that model one lane leaves **<2.5 ns** for link+bookkeeping+writer; four lanes leave **<6.3 ns**. Neither is a current equal-cell price; chain starts may be uneven or too few. A four-lane figure cannot be silently assigned to four Workers, since each Worker owns a different block.

At 1 thread, the old model gives no full-kernel gain: it prices the point walk alone and omits all positive costs. At 4, the [dose](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6880764544) has P16/P0 wall penalties 1.862 versus 1.415 at 1, a ratio **1.32**; chase penalties 6.256 versus 6.914, ratio **0.91**. At 10, the *registered CPU* streaming amplification ratio is **1.583 [1.516,1.654]** and the chase ratio **0.53**. Thus stream savings become more valuable relative to a dependent visit as concurrency rises. **Extrapolation:** those factors are for artificial 16 MB/block perturbations, not multiplicative prices for one cell. The proof of profit needs per-cell stream fraction, actual chain starts, changed writer and current-source codegen; 1.58×7.7 or 0.53×5.2 would be unjustified. A ten-worker bandwidth plateau can still null a CPU Ir gain if link metadata streams equally many bytes, or if the critical worker handles few admitted blocks.

The pre-pass failure does not refute this: §6.1 joined 0.635r letters and cut induction 1.826→0.928 ns/row, but a separate scan raised ordering 0.525→2.448 ns/row. This scheme has no separate letter-join scan; it tests at the existing step and defers only eligible points. It can still fail for the same economic reason if chain discovery or phase repair recreates that pass.

## Decision

**STEELMAN HOLDS**, narrowly: a local, sparse link plus one-touch deferred walk has a specific, falsifiable route to removing induction stores and slot traffic on the already binding stage. This is worth **one registered screen**, after the diet step 1 and working-set premise test, not an implementation or performance claim. Before that screen, freeze the current-source equal-branch count, actual lane-start geometry, operation/line ledger including origin and writer, and a proof obligation for one-touch chains and O(n+S+r+z) total work. Stop the line if the origin/phase invariant fails, a full pre-pass is required, deferred visits multiply with grammar occurrences, or conservative link+visit+bookkeeping/traffic reaches the equal-cell price. Existing MONODROMY owns reconciliation; no tower backend or repeated-global-pass route is reopened.
