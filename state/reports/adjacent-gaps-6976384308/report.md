## 1. The two tracks and the retained interval bound

In an elementary LF atom, the same byte precedes adjacent rows and their image gap is adjacent. After conjugating both ends by the rotation that places the primary at zero, split any atom crossing the new origin in domain or image. Relative comparisons retain their meaning in the new rank order. These cuts add at most two to the original maximal run count. For a gap component, the corresponding vertices on the two tracks are related by subtraction of one. Therefore the order of every pair of vertices within that component agrees. This is a claim about a synchronized track, with no claim of agreement after the gap loses its edge.

The gap map is injective where defined since LF is a permutation. It has n−R edges on n−1 linear gaps, hence R−1 paths, including isolated gaps. A path has no repeated upper or lower vertex: one repetition would repeat the pair and imply a cycle. On a cycle, consecutive vertices give a cyclic comparison and the two tracks keep the same minima, lengths and adjacency. On a finite path, keep the common consecutive list of surviving vertices at each level. Filtering its interior selects the same minima on both tracks. The only excluded candidates at an end are one possible old extra and the endmost common element. They are consecutive in the filtered orbit, so at most one remains a strict minimum. If the common list empties, at most two extras remain and later rounds can only delete them. This also covers disappearance of an unmarked singleton. For every upper common vertex except the last, its next selected lower and upper images remain adjacent. Their full return stays within gap edges and the certified merge removes that potential boundary.

Each path therefore charges at most three retained atom starts, and every positive original rank is the upper rank of one unique gap. Zero supplies at most one more. The result is k_j ≤ 3R−2 for the retained representation **after merging**. Temporary ports and rope leaves follow different bounds. For a round with k atoms, entrance-height changes give at most 2k pieces. The disjoint image intervals can be split by at most k−1 target boundaries. Seeds and later cuts bound the number of unmerged leaves by seeds plus ports, also O(k).

**Tightness.** The four-atom example really grows to five retained atoms and then two. My bounded exhaustive search covered 747,812 ordered interval exchanges with R=2–5 and width sums through 23 (through 16 for R=5). The largest observed peaks, including initial levels, are 2, 3, 5 and 6. This is a finite search. Moreover, R=2 can be bounded by two in every level. A nontrivial two-atom exchange is the rotation of widths a,b. Its minima form the prefix of width s=min(a,b). The first return to this prefix is again a rotation, with at most two height regimes. Initially the two seams are precisely sources x=0 or images f(x)=0. This characterization survives induced returns since zero survives and cannot be interior to an excursion. Thus seams add no third interval, and the argument iterates. The formula 3R−2 is already loose at R=2. I leave asymptotic sharpness of the leading coefficient open. Linear dependence itself is unavoidable, even after a contraction: for L=(ba)^m, p=0, R=2m and LF sends 2i to m+i and 2i+1 to i. The minima are the even ranks below m. These ceil(m/2) rows cannot merge across original seams, so the first contracted level needs Θ(R) atoms.

Prior art separates the components clearly: inverse BWT via list ranking, order-based LMS selection in suffix sorting, and locally consistent parsing into run-length grammars. Here the claim is the control of this first-return construction by the elementary LF discontinuities. [Edwards and Vishkin](https://drum.lib.umd.edu/items/7b674b30-00f2-4a11-9509-ace1fc3aa3f7) · [Nong, Zhang and Chan](https://doi.org/10.1109/TC.2010.188) · [Navarro, Ochoa and Prezza](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09517)

## 2. What the certificate certifies

The bit is set when the excursion takes an elementary step **from** an initial atom start. Reaching the final destination does not count as taking a step there. Thus a clear right-hand bit proves the elementary trace can shift left by one, reading exactly the same labels. The adjacent actual source, consecutive compact destinations and equal length ensure the shifted trace is the left interval's specified first return. The bit alone is insufficient if the proposed source is absent from the active section or if one return ends earlier. A set bit blocks this certificate and is not a proof of unequal words.

Propagation is exact: combine old flags by OR, use the upward first visit or downward last visit for the only possible start hit in a passage, set it on the port's leftmost origin, and clear the newly interior right leaf when splitting one origin interval. Merging retains the left flag. In the dense replay I verified all accepted seams by following and comparing both elementary traces. ## 3. Time, storage and the marked cycle

Keep strict local minima. Every nontrivial cycle has one and neighboring rows along the cycle cannot both be minima. Fixed points off the marked orbit may be dropped. Zero is the least rank and survives each round of its nontrivial cycle. Hence m_j ≤ n/2^j until the mark becomes fixed. At that point the recorded return label is one marked period in forward order by the composition rule: traversing labels u then v backwards means the forward concatenation is vu. Unmarked cycles need never be expanded. Repeating and truncating that period to n handles powers and arbitrary columns where the marked cycle length does not divide n. For example, L=`aba`, p=1, produces `aba` from a period of length two. This is not decoding every LF orbit and concatenating them. The result respects the original primary after rank conjugation, even if the ordinary unrotated primary was not a minimum.

With O(k_j) ports and leaves, initial target searches and sorting cost O(k_j log(2k_j)). AVL joining costs the height difference plus constant work. Weighted splitting descends one path and rejoins adjacent subtrees on the way up, with height differences telescoping to O(log(2k_j)). Delayed prepends create constant-size concatenation or power rules per charged operation. They do not copy the represented bytes. Summing min(3R−2,n/2^j) gives O(R(1+log(n/R))) interval visits, and replacing log(2k_j) by O(log(2R)) proves the stated grammar construction bound. It includes rope operations under the AVL analysis. The live interval workspace is O(R), separate from grammar rules and optional round statistics.

The writer initializes rule bookkeeping, materializes each first occurrence and copies subsequent occurrences from it. Copying powers and repeating the final period are charged to bytes produced. O(G+n) is a safe writing bound for G stored rules. The advertised construction starts at a maximal run list. The direct compressed-input route separately pays for scanner and Huffman work and an O(s) MTF/run pass over s decoded symbols on a fixed 256-symbol alphabet. The run-record route scans the capped records before coalescing them. Both then pay for RLE1, CRC, the expanded final size and delivery. Halving is a statement about work across sequential rounds and gives no worker-pool scaling guarantee.

## 4. Measurements and independent replay

The dense oracle starts from the old archived L and primary 264045. It matches the 899,931-byte reference inverse, all 12 level row/minimum counts, and the retained atom counts. It uses elementary traces, so it is a correctness oracle rather than an alternative implementation of the sparse complexity claim. It independently checked 9,601 small arbitrary-column/primary combinations against direct LF, including fixed points, repeated cycles and primaries away from the natural minimum. It reproduced the four-to-five growth example. The separate archived native tests and corpus comparisons were not rerun. [Frozen column reference](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/bf88365ba80388e17488a31c80d2f3f250b19fb2/docs/journal/2026-11-11-first-return-block/evidence/identities.json)

- **Frozen construction:** 102,233 interval visits over 12 rounds, 128,800 ports and 103,131 rules (99,368 reachable). The new route makes zero support refinements. The older path's 504,258 split contexts are a different operation count. For the separate complete-decode comparison with five measured rounds, medians are 1.047527 s for SUP1 encoder+decoder and 0.037407 s for run-record contraction, a 28.00× ratio of medians. Median paired ratio is 27.95×. This charges the earlier encoder's weighted bookkeeping and verification. It does not isolate the unweighted inverse or mean contraction beat Cells. The main frozen timing series gives 27.556 ms versus 2.551 ms for Cells; keep the two series separate.
- **Primitive control:** the word (abcd)^m! has one unique marker. L=d^m!a^mb^mc^m and LF subtracts m modulo 4m+1. The minima are the first m rows after rotation. From them f^4 adds one and an extra step wraps the last minimum to zero, so the first return is addition by one modulo m. It reduces to zero in the next round for m≥2. The five measured sizes each report nine visits and ten rules. At 875,001 bytes, 17-round medians are 0.161 ms direct versus 1.526 ms through shipped Cells dispatch (9.48×). At 256 changes, 1.045 versus 2.436 ms (2.33×). At 1,024, direct 2.911 ms and Cells 2.358 ms. The five-run input falls back under Cells' sparse-record gate. The timed comparison remains against the shipped behavior, with forced Cells reported separately.
- **Wikidata:** warmup-excluded medians are 8.370082 s Extrema versus 0.728728 s Cells with zero worker threads (one calling thread), and 2.681984 versus 0.345032 s with four workers. The timed Extrema arm uses the capped-run route and honors the fallback. The census forces construction on all 431 blocks and totals 29,092,269 visits, with 383 admitted by the normal run-front predicate in its roster. The archived independent bzip2 comparison reports matching 209,700,196 final bytes. Source and records support that result; this review did not rerun the native full-stream comparison. Direct maximal runs are a distinct arm (8.720533 s serial).
- **Cachegrind:** on the **frozen single-block process**, Dr+Dw is 73,342,216 Extrema versus 6,379,553 Cells (11.50×). That is a simulated reference count including the surrounding decoder. It applies neither to the whole Wikidata corpus nor to hardware LLC events or DRAM. Elapsed measurements were on the Xeon Platinum 8573C VM with one thread per core visible, a four-CPU quota, and Rust 1.90.0. They leave i9, SMT and native memory traffic open.

The construction gives a substantive result on small-run inputs. The sparse route remains slower on the principal workload.
