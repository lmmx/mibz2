## 2. Initial arcs and the work model

**Identity — proved.** The r source runs and the r image intervals each tile the row line; their two sets D and I of *internal* cuts each have r−1 elements. In target order the elementary overlay cells are exactly the graph arcs, since each image cell lies in one source-run image and in one target run. Hence E₀=1+|D∪I|=2r−1−|D∩I|, and d=E₀−V₀=r−1−|D∩I|. The recorded coincidences reproduce E₀ on all 233 matched rows. As a discrete gap partial map, the adjacency translation is a bijection from gaps outside D to gaps outside I. Its nontrivial finite chains start in I∖D and end in D∖I, and there are d of them. Common cuts are isolated zero-step chains. Calling these chains saddle connections is the gap-track interpretation; the elementary overlay identity needs no geometric identification.

**Model r+3d — descriptive, with substantial residual.** x has median 0.22270. Correlations of normalized work with x are 0.709, 0.633 and 0.790 for Cells, rank-gap and forest. OLS with a free intercept for work/r−1 gives intercept/slope 0.0363/3.588 (Cells), 0.4619/2.110 (rank-gap), and 0.0768/3.122 (forest). Those reproduce the 3.6/3.1 slopes, but an exact own-input baseline of one would constrain the intercept to zero and give slopes 3.752, 4.193, 3.468. With the coefficient fixed at three, median residuals are +0.175r, +0.261r and +0.115r. On frozen, r+3d=84,046, below all three measurements. This is an approximate corpus-scale model, with no unit-charge proof or equal operation units. It omits comparisons, allocations, tree building, grammar formation and bytes written. In particular “one per run reads the input” is an interpretation of the intercept. On frozen Cells already supplies an exact within-algorithm split of 51,183 equal contractions and 41,387 top/bottom comparisons, whereas the forest has 62,138 initial arcs and 28,807 at later levels.

## 3. Does the evidence show correspondence between events?

**Common partition complexity only.** The counted objects are different: an actual endpoint step, an atom at a rank-gap level, and an input arc existing at a forest level. Their totals differ even on the same block. The forest total is also schedule-sensitive at fixed initial geometry. I reversed only the equal-width Kruskal tie order, preserving the arc list and marked cut. On the primitive column `1111000011100`, mark zero, initial E₀=6 and V₀=4 in both runs, first retained mass is 5 and first output has 4 bands. Chord IDs change from [0,3,5] to [0,1,3]. The summed input arcs change **12→15**. Cells sees identical input and follows its fixed schedule. This refutes a schedule-independent bijection of the census units. A relation restricted to particular tie orders, or a many-to-one relation between operations and gap paths, remains unproved. [Tie-control script](witnesses.py)

**Cheapest discriminating test.** In untimed toy traces, stamp the initial domain/image cuts with original gap IDs and their LF-gap chain IDs. Carry interval ancestry through exact instance operations, using dense provenance on tiny columns to avoid infrastructure. Log Cells' top/bottom/equal comparison and the exact gaps or slices contracted. Separately log forest input arc scans, kept/cut choices and induced bands. Before collecting traces, state which event type and multiplicity to match, using original gap ID plus the affected slice/phase span as the key; specify whether one event consumes, creates or only visits it. Test the tie witness and its two forests first, and include a long one-strip shift to distinguish repeated visits from chain count. Check both reduced permutations and output against dense LF. Compare event keys and unmatched multiplicities after projecting to the declared type, with a gap-chain histogram as the weaker comparison. Only a link that predicts those keyed events under the declared schedule should advance to one frozen block. Matching aggregate H, d or totals cannot pass.

## 4. Forest phase timing and construction classifications

**0.22 ms and 1.21 ms — reported microprobe minima, not a measured phase split.** The supplied Rust source forms and weight-sorts arc indices and then unions endpoints. It selects the minimum of 200 for each segment independently. The production-shaped forest constructor adds the marked-chord exclusion, deterministic tied index, union-by-size, adjacency and chord-vector construction, and subsequent levels with their own width distributions. The microprobe does not implement that same loop. Record 61 labels it “this container”; the 35.30 ms is a median on the Xeon VM. No same-execution trace or matched machine identity links the two. Summed arithmetic (0.22+1.21) ×90,945/62,138 = 2.09 ms, or 5.9% of 35.30 ms, is an extrapolation from unmatched minima. This suggests a small isolated sort/union cost and leaves native phase attribution open. The cheap confirmation is a single instrumented build charging sort, selection/adjacency, potential/port order, event/slab scheduling and recipe registration at each level, with aligned intervals inside each run. No timings were taken in this review. [Microprobe](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/049464756c76addb72cbfaed42d421d006126eab/docs/journal/monodromy/2026-11-18-synthesis.kruskal.rs.txt) · [Actual reduction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/3832bb12857181adb2f46b35f5c7fa8ebaa29a77/docs/journal/2026-11-18-switch-elimination/forest.rs) · [Native phase records](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/3832bb12857181adb2f46b35f5c7fa8ebaa29a77/docs/journal/2026-11-18-switch-elimination/evidence/frozen-time.csv)

| Construction | Work it avoids | Work that remains |
|---|---|---|
| Cells | Dense per-row LF during inversion construction | Sequential right-end comparisons and row removals, rule generation and output. Tight flat-array kernel. |
| Adjacent rank gaps | Dense per-row representation and repeated visits to one atom via division | Per-round routing, rope/port operations, word rules and output. Frozen division count is zero under the measured itinerary. |
| Open return | Endpoint induction over the removed narrow runs; tracing once per shared itinerary in the tracer | Narrow-row counting sort and residual links, wide construction or tracing, label expansion and output. “90% of letters” cannot mean byte rows: on frozen about 93% of runs are narrow and about 10% of rows are retained. |
| Forest | Dense interior path discovery for routing once its boundary exists | Build and order ports, solve slabs or interval intersections, retain trees/recipes, produce output. The counted input arcs omit 48,647 first-layer slab matches, among other costs. |
| Pro copies | Separate spelling of return words, given dependencies | Recover coordinates and sources, compile a legal schedule, then emit. The 11,145 optimum uses the decoded text during parsing. A forward-only evaluator's sweeps characterize that schedule, not all evaluation algorithms. |
| Bispecial support | Supplying counts and weights as part of the representation | Reconstruction, split operations and output remain, so identifiability alone provides no speed guarantee. |

The classification is useful when scoped to each algorithm. The claim that every alternative loses only on cost per operation is too strong. The counters omit and count different work. Even on the reported data, Open Return is about 9–17% slower than eager Cells and faster than LazyCells on the VM, while rank-gap and forest have roughly an order-of-magnitude or larger losses on their tested Wikidata workloads. Their 270 ns/visit and 430 ns/arc divide time by different census units. Those quotients are descriptive and cannot attribute a causal fraction to the counted work. The constructions avoid needing a full text-coordinate map before final emission. The exact costs of obtaining only the samples or of another representation are open.

## 5. O3 — identity proved and frozen measurements reproduced

Let P_k be the maximal interval partition by the run-ID word of LF^0 through LF^(k−1). LF is a translation on any site of P_k, because it is already a translation on P₁. An arc is precisely an intersection of a P_k site with a preimage of a target P_k site. Their joint labels are the k-window and its one-shifted k-window, which together are exactly the k+1-window. Adjacency across either constituent cut changes at least one run ID. Thus the overlay is P_(k+1), so **V₀(k+1)=E₀(k)**.

Equivalently, mark original cut gaps D₁. The partial adjacent-gap map τ is injective inside runs. The next cut set is D₁∪τ⁻¹(D_k). The new frontier is the preimage of the previous frontier, with no collisions because the initial cuts have no outgoing τ edge and τ is injective. Therefore E₀(k)−V₀(k) is that new frontier size, nonincreasing with k; E₀ is nondecreasing. This constrains the initial level. It does not determine how much work disappears from later levels.

I rebuilt the partition by this gap recurrence from the digest-checked frozen column and fed the resulting arcs to the pinned forest matcher. All nine tabled rows match, including 90,945→94,488→208,766 summed arcs at k=1,3,32, with first retained rows 110,273→38,231→7,667. This confirms the negative result **for those tested frozen refinements**. Universal monotonicity of total work is false. Primitive `100`, mark zero, has initial E₀=3 at k=1 and 2; (V₀,d) changes from (2,1) to (3,0). Under the same forest/tie/mark rule it needs two reductions with five total input arcs at depth 1, and one reduction with three total at depth 2. Runs were best among the trialled frozen contexts. An E₀≥r floor requires physical rank intervals with one source byte per arc. [O3 recomputation](o3.csv) · [Gap-frontier implementation](o3.py)

## 6. O5 — counted row span, with the work-model limits

On the pinned frozen column I reproduced the 12 strict-minimum rounds. Active row counts are 899,931, 298,922, 97,100, 28,375, 9,095, 2,951, 916, 299, 80, 23, 7 and 2; longest previous-level excursions are 11,8,11,8,9,7,6,9,7,5,4,2. Their sum is **87**. Consecutive same-atom repeats within the counted excursions are zero. True executed row-visit sum in this model is 1,337,701 and atom snapshots over nonterminal levels are 102,233. Record 62's 1,337,702 and 102,234 include the one-row terminal snapshot, which performs no contraction. The earlier 102,233 counter is consistent with the nonterminal convention.

Keeping strict cyclic minima retains at most half on every nontrivial component, provided the mark is already such a minimum or the protected-mark adjustment is accounted for. In this frozen run, rotating the mark to rank 0 makes it a minimum. This is a local-minimum form of list contraction. Shrinking levels does not bound excursion length. Primitive `1 0^(n−1)` has only the marked minimum and one excursion of length n in the first round. Atom compression can reduce that example's different work measure. The 87 is a sum of row excursions under a round barrier and independent traversal, excluding atom-router, merge, memory and synchronization costs. It gives no span bound for the implementation that produced the 102,233 atom count. The ratio of those two numbers is therefore no speedup estimate. Cells' 92,570 steps form a dependence chain in its current right-end loop; this is an implementation dependency, with no lower bound on every equivalent formulation. Record 62's i9/SMT placement comparison remains a hypothesis without matched measurements. ## 7. P1–P4 — precise status

1. **P1, extra interval work — open as a general theorem.** Since d≤r−1 for the run presentation, Ω(r+d) is already Ω(r) asymptotically. To make a meaningful extra-work claim, specify allowable operations and representations and prove a separate surcharge beyond the input pass, or a lower bound on a named event family. An explicit pass enumerating initial overlay arcs pays for E₀ records by definition. It does not prove that every method must enumerate them separately. There is also no universal Θ(r) upper on structural work for the tested rank-order algorithms. The primitive `1 0^w` family from the independent #569 review holds initial r=2 and excess=1 while its forest takes w cuts; the right-end subtractive schedule can likewise keep taking one-row steps on an imbalanced two-interval exchange.
2. **P2, text-coordinate recovery beyond list ranking — open and underspecified.** A full phase vector on n rows is one list-ranking problem on marked LF, and explicit output costs Ω(n) in the word model. A method that needs only selected phases, a compact coordinate oracle or byte output alone has a different deliverable. A general permutation/list I/O bound cannot be imported as an RLBWT subclass lower bound without a size-preserving reduction and the matching model. The input has O(r) run records and O(r(log n+log σ)+log n) bits in a straightforward encoding. Avoid reading “Θ(r) input” as a bit bound.
3. **P3, minimum excess and topology — open only after restricting presentations.** A singleton-per-row presentation has E=V=n and zero excess for any permutation. Thus unrestricted minimum is already zero. A meaningful question fixes allowed translations/labels and a site budget, or minimises a combined work/storage objective. Relating that constrained optimum to genus and singularities needs a precise invariant, since cutting the same exchange more finely can change excess.
4. **P4, sharpness of 3R−2 — open.** The finite gap-path proof bounds at most two end extras plus the last common survivor, with one extra possible start at rank zero. That verifies the upper bound for the certified construction, not independent attainability on all paths at a common level. The 4→5 example shows growth, with 1.25R the largest reported witness. Neither the observed maximum nor the upper-bound charging proves tightness. The scope is the chosen certificate and compact relabelling.
