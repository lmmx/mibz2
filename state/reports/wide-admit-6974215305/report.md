## 1. What the guards actually guard

Text and random are sensible guards against a regression where the dispatch path is supposed to stay the same. Text is above the density cutoff, and random takes the column route. They exercise early rejection, dispatch effects, and whole-binary changes between builds. #506 is concrete precedent: an extra kernel moved the reference code generation despite source identity. A control with an unchanged route is relevant. Its failure should count under the existing guard rule. A clean guard does not test the newly admitted cells. [PR #506](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/506)

The mixed Wikidata stream tests the net cost and benefit in its specific mixture. It can hide a dense-block loss. For a serial counterexample, suppose the changed stratum contributes 13.7% of baseline time and slows by 20%, while the remainder improves by 7%. The aggregate ratio is 0.137 × 1.20 + 0.863 × 0.93 = 0.9670. The 13.7% here is an illustrative time share. The census provides a byte share, and time shares, overlap, tails and reader ordering are unknown in the actual parallel run. The smaller class can regress under a winning aggregate even if both off-path guards stay at 1.00. No-SLOWER also leaves losses whose intervals overlap 1 unresolved. Its guard cells are neither equivalence nor noninferiority tests.

The treatment spans two axes. Exact capped-record density is at least 1/64 and strictly below 3/20 in the wide path, with a budget of floor(8n/25) instead of floor(4n/25). Blocks below the old density gate can become eligible through the budget alone, which explains why r/n 0.097 can occur among the 32. Larger budgets can also affect blocks admitted by both. In slots_lazy the two budget-dependent 16-byte regions move the layout and increase requested scratch by 32 × (wide budget − old budget), or 4,608,000 B for n=900,000 before any other allocation changes. Physical page commitment, retained allocation and process RSS must be measured separately. The changed-class diagnostic should distinguish newly admitted, admitted by both with a larger budget, and refused by both. [Wide gate implementation](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/8e6d7ed1f84fd480cb41ac1336e5489243de2eab/src/cells_wide.rs) and [layout](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/8e6d7ed1f84fd480cb41ac1336e5489243de2eab/src/cells_lazy.rs)

**Before timing**, record both arms' admit/refuse/fallback census on the exact workload, including the 32 changed full blocks and which refusal was lifted. Verify text and random exercise unchanged routes. If any directed check is desired, separately report a sufficiently long valid stream rewrapped from the 32 preidentified changed blocks, keeping all originals and avoiding outcome-based selection. When there is a policy need for a high-r no-harm guarantee, freeze a distinct cell, meaningful loss margin, and precision plan before data. The same broad-corpus SLOWER screen cannot furnish that guarantee. A rewrapped stream is a stress diagnostic and changes stream mix and reuse, so do not read its effect as a prediction for general Wikidata. Scope the current verdict to the mixed corpus if keeping the existing executable design.

The traffic case is worth the test. The prior census's modeled refused-path contribution is 0.92 write + 0.92 RFO + 1.40 read = 3.24 B per whole-stream output byte on the displayed numbers, about 29% of 11.2. Thus 1.40 alone is the T-vector walk's read contribution, about 12.5%, not a third. Clarify that sentence. Simulated agreement in total to 0.2 B does not establish per-route calibration. The rounded read and write discrepancies have opposite signs (−0.18 and +0.37). The later paired model moves 11.41→9.27 B per byte (−18.8%) at +7.8% instructions. Treat layout, the single-worker 3 MiB simulation, ownership traffic and overlap as hypotheses that the i9 counters and wall results will test. ## 2. Ten rounds are defensible as a bounded test

I rehashed the #535 fused-finish bundle (SHA-256 18dfadec8b1bfad7d085f25bc8daaf6cbdb35a4f9e18a54661be8baadd7a12f6), paired raw Wikidata ×4 rows by round and recomputed log-ratio SD and the adjudicator's statistic. Pinned 10 requested had SD .03015, ratio .984779, p=.07109. Unpinned 12 requested had SD .01990, ratio .986269, p=.02781. Neither clears the first Holm threshold .025. The historical cells use a different treatment and are scenarios for noise transfer. [Fused finish result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6947507581)

For a single cell at the two-cell family's first threshold, power under independent normal paired log ratios follows a noncentral t with n−1 df and shift sqrt(n) log(R)/SD. The minimum p qualifies a gain at either cell. Its joint power depends on the dependence and the two true effects. The table concerns each cell clearing the first threshold, excluding guard and RSS failure.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| .01990 (fused unpinned 12) | 57.2% | 99.0% | 100.0% | 6 |
| .03015 (fused pinned 10) | 29.4% | 81.1% | 96.7% | 10 |

At an **observed** .970 with ten rounds, .04258 is the largest sample log SD that clears the first cutoff. That is a resolution threshold, distinct from prospective power. The registered [.93,1.01] is a forecast for the effect and does not determine the paired-run SD. Four exact workload copies lengthen the measurement. They do not increase the number of independent rounds or test corpus diversity. Time-blocked drift or serial dependence can reduce effective precision.

Retain ten rounds given the plausible effect, the cost cap and the qualitative value of the IMC contrasts. Accept NOT ADOPTED when gain is unresolved. A useful 3% effect could then remain open. If an 80%-power requirement at 5% paired noise is desired, fix at least 24 rounds before collecting any data under that new design and pay the extra workstation time. Do not extend this run after seeing a near miss. [Reproduction](power.py) and [numeric results](power.json)

## 3. Score the knee separately

The adoption question compares wide against master **at fixed requested count and plan**. The knee question compares eight requested threads and eight workers against ten requested threads and seven workers **within wide**. An intervention can improve both plans while leaving their ordering intact. It can also reverse the ordering by hurting seven, which is no production gain.

For round i define x_i=log(Wide8/Master8), y_i=log(Wide10/Master10), and b_i=log(Master8/Master10). Report the knee contrast b_i+x_i−y_i=log(Wide8/Wide10) and the departure from baseline x_i−y_i, with observed geometric means and descriptive paired intervals. Do not infer the crossover by comparing two separate significance labels. Keep the original forecast and score it against its stated range. Flag the point tension now: transferring the cited historic 1.024 baseline ratio and using the frozen point ratios .960 and .970 gives 1.024×.960/.970=1.0134. Reaching a wide ratio of 1 would require Wide8/Master8 ≤ .9473 if Wide10/Master10=.97 and that historical baseline transferred. A baseline from the same rounds is preferable. This explains uncertainty in the crossover prediction. It does not justify changing the wall criterion or promoting the diagnostic to a veto.

IMC likewise decides nothing under this rule, and a DRAM reduction with flat wall is an informative outcome. At the reviewed head the optional parser can silently accept a partial count if one controller reports the same event first as `<not counted>` and another then reports 10 MiB. I replayed that two-line case and obtained a seemingly complete 10 MiB at 100%. For diagnostic scoring, require all expected controller/event records or an explicitly verified aggregate, preserve failed channels, and flag idle subtraction that clips or dominates a channel. The reader also trusts positive finite child wall and a present reference hash from its producer more than it verifies them. Treat suspect or missing IMC as unscorable and keep the wall bundle and its original verdict.
