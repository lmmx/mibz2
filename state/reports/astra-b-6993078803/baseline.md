# Astra B · evidence baseline before reading the summary

The exact methods under comparison contract portions of the marked LF permutation, attach the skipped emitted bytes to a retained arrow and track the marked byte's offset. The tile screen measures obstruction to collective representations and the tile prototype deletes only clean paths, preserving dirty members for exact residual induction. Equal-byte excursions have a different arithmetic compression: scan the two monotone FIFO sides of a byte bucket, batch internal spans by division, represent contiguous token ranges, then restore external arrows in top order. Exit order and LF order differ, so the staged implementation stores and consumes records to rejoin them.

The frozen queue removes 15,756 rows and changes 92,570 residual cuts to 89,708. On the ten sampled blocks the staged queue's modeled source/residual record operations alone total 2,643,708. The generous induction, word and removed-band setup credits are 983,329 eager and 971,386 lazy. Frozen gives 195,568 versus 73,068 or 72,838. Those are mixed source-level event counts, with record accesses and 32-bit fields priced as one unit each in the simple comparison. Every block has a debit on that convention. The separate staged-array replay counts modeled 64-byte fills and dirty drains, with 77,138 and 73,306 transfers on frozen at 32 and 256 KiB. It never simulates the removed baseline operations in the same replay. Structural debits and credits therefore support a negative result for the chosen staged layout and unit accounting. They do not order hardware time, measure shared-memory bandwidth, or rule out another ordering.

The evidence has an executable exhaustive marked/arrow checker, one pinned example of full-period arithmetic rotation, ten original/per-method marked output and libbz2 envelope receipts, and a deliberately limited packed-layout cache model. The screen's ideal tile obstruction, exact semantics and performance conjecture require different evidence.
