## Finite re-derivations and counterexamples

**Coverage.** For a finite permutation T and nonempty K, every x in K has a first return h(x). The half-open orbit segments x,T(x),...,T^(h(x)-1)(x), for x in K, are disjoint and partition exactly the union U of cycles meeting K: every point of U has a unique preceding K point along its cycle. Thus sum over x in K of h(x)=|U|. Grouping points with one affine itinerary into a base interval of width w gives sum(w*h)=|U|, **not automatically n**. The n identity needs all cycles covered. K={0} for L=`ab`, LF=[0,1], misses origptr1 entirely; this is an arbitrary-column contract witness, not a claim that `ab` is a valid primitive one-text cyclic BWT. Even valid periodic words can have several LF cycles. A selected-cycle decoder may intentionally omit others, but must preserve the chosen cycle, byte orientation, phase and requested output length, including repetition. A count matrix or a return-time sum cannot certify those properties.

Refining a candidate section is indispensable: first-return time alone does not ensure a shared ordered label. A fixed small number of section intervals does not supply a small bound on their itinerary fragments. Integer endpoints and strict positive steps can support a new finite proof, but importing infinite/minimal dynamics supplies neither equality handling nor a cheap endpoint data structure. Likewise, commuting legal contractions means identical induced semantics, not identical transient records or cache cost.

**Order.** `ab` and `ba` have identical letter counts and different emitted strings. The same example defeats a matrix-only reconstruction certificate. A finite macro may replace an ordered list of grammar updates by composition, but each occurrence still needs routing/mark information. CRC or byte-count agreement alone is also weaker than byte equality.

**Topology.** With tau(x)=x+1 mod n and D=T tau T^-1 tau^-1, if x-1 and x lie inside one image interval, T^-1(x-1)+1=T^-1(x), so D fixes x. Boundary support sparsity follows. But both L=`baaaa` (LF shift4) and L=`bbbba` (LF shift1) have D=identity and different LF maps and words. Topology alone loses essential data even for single-cycle examples. Since tau is transitive, the surface is connected; with m moved points and k nontrivial commutator cycles, Euler characteristic gives g=1+(m-k)/2. Five cycles with m near r means high genus, not five-state decoding. A boundary-only representation also needs distances, labels and return itineraries.

The accompanying `finite_checks.py` verifies tower coverage and commutator support on all126 binary columns of lengths1–6 and all5,334 nonempty sections, plus the explicit witnesses. It is an independent finite sanity check, not a general proof or decoder benchmark; the proofs above carry the general claims. No real corpus, candidate or timing was run.
