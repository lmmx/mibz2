## Admissibility, operating characteristics and decision

The 1.01 pooled margin and 1.02 flag boundary apply to different estimands. Halving a margin on a group mean is a clear policy choice. It does not preserve a per-cell point cap. I authenticated the original archive and reproduced the draft rule's retrospective B1/B2 estimates: **0.999463 [0.993715, 1.005055]** and **0.992285 [0.985762, 0.998965]**, with no flags. Those are declared prior observations. They are not evidence for the fresh verdict. The outcome-derived context must remain visible.

The **B1 replacement is admissible prospectively** under the maintainer's documented direction and merged §4a: same input set, same baseline, static and diagnostic evidence for the refused route, a separate pooled native bound, and a flag for clear individual regressions. The weaker power below a clear individual regression must remain explicit. Choosing criteria after the pilot was seen requires an articulated causal justification and independent new pairs. It does not turn the old failure into a pass.

The same §4a exception applies to demonstrably near-unchanged execution paths and explicitly preserves the usual protection where new code executes. B2's admitted held-out inputs and B4's sustained Wikidata are outside B1's causal case. The generic authorization of a proportionate validation and the defined B1 exception do not establish that the B2 and B4 relaxations comply with §2. Preserve the original wall protections for those paths, or record a prospective, exact-scope approval of the trade-off. This is distinct from expecting #635's per-cell power on the zero-route set. [Merged policy §2](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/f85bf8a96a384acfac14a1eae25f7e20de059d58/docs/team/PERFORMANCE-POLICY.md#L56) · [§4a](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/f85bf8a96a384acfac14a1eae25f7e20de059d58/docs/team/PERFORMANCE-POLICY.md#L78)

The about-0.12 number is the **modeled chance of at least one guard failure under zero guard effect**, conditional on other gates passing. A global no-effect run is expected to fail B3, so this is not a global false-NOT-ADOPTED rate. The exact combined 0.12 calculation uses an independence approximation even though the pooled and flag outcomes for B1 use the same data. The displayed own-cell rates give an approximate union bound of 0.123 on those three components (0.015 + 0.035 + 0.073); zero simulated hits in B2 flags are not a proof of zero risk. Accepting a conservative roughly 12% guard false-fail estimate within the model is reasonable. The small residual samples, unmodeled shared round effects and pooled estimand should remain in the registration language.
