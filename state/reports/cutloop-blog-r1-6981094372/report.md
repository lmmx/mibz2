## Factual patch

1. **LF can close before visiting every row.** The opening says following LF from the primary visits each row once. For periodic `abab` the valid BWT is `bbaa`, LF = [2,3,0,1], and with primary 0 the walk cycles 0→2→0. It reads `ba` backwards and extends that orbit word to the known block length. Say that LF follows the marked orbit, with a whole-column tour when there is a single cycle. The banana stepper may keep its exact per-row assertion as an example-specific fact. The opening's “loop falls into open paths” should likewise refer to the cut component, allowing disjoint uncut cycles as §1 already explains. [Opening](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e7bae604b2fb3b508cee4d3fb2a6d8af12c92f8c/docs/site/blog/2026-11-18-cutting-the-loop-open.md) · [Proof](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e6df6aee5055840b8b6ed780e29727e23da6d42f/docs/journal/2026-11-18-open-return/proof.md#restricting-a-stable-match)
2. **Narrow the threshold claim to the frozen sweep.** §6 says increasing the threshold leaves less to trace and that the threshold sets multiplicity more than the block does. That describes the frozen example through 16, and the survey has typical multiplicity 15.92 at 16. It is no law for the method. A valid BWT of periodic `010010` is `110000`, primary 2, LF = [4,5,0,1,2,3]. At threshold 1 only the mark is kept; the one open path contributing visits has two complementary letters. At threshold 2 rows {0,1,2} are kept. The second LF cycle opens and the nonempty paths have lengths 1 and 2, so U increases from 2 to 3. Keep the empirical claim scoped, and use the 118/176 contrast to teach the residual variation. The one-strip SVG caption should give both heights only for 0<|d|≤w where they occur, and state height one for |d|≥w, as the notes do. Its headline should say a width-one bundle has nothing to share, since one representative is still traced. [§§5–6](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e7bae604b2fb3b508cee4d3fb2a6d8af12c92f8c/docs/site/blog/2026-11-18-cutting-the-loop-open.md) · [One-strip notes](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e7bae604b2fb3b508cee4d3fb2a6d8af12c92f8c/docs/site/blog/2026-11-18-cutting-the-loop-open-notes.md#one-strips-heights)
3. **Report the prose comparison as a median.** The six area shares range from 27.59% to 48.98%, with median 32.34%. “A third of each block” overstates uniformity. Say “a median of about one third across the six prose blocks.” The upper-bin run share says “256 rows or longer.” The committed t=256 counters identify strips from runs **longer than 256** (subject to marking), with 496 strips and 664,650 open occurrences. Use >256 and the derived rounded shares, or cite a histogram that includes exactly-256 runs. The singleton shares 68.6% and 3.9% are supported even allowing one forced primary in m=35,116.
