# Independent baseline before reading the live post

The inverse BWT follows a permutation of the rows. Removing the arrows that leave a chosen set of rows makes entrances and exits. The remaining open paths and their letters depend only on the complement, while a stable sort of the retained letters tells us which entrance each removed arrow used. Reconnecting those two maps gives the first-return decoder on the retained rows.

- A permutation has one predecessor and successor per row. An entrance has lost its predecessor, so it cannot join a cycle or another path. The erased edges are reconstructed in the same order by a stable sort of the smaller column; this is about image rank, not equal original row numbers. The mark chooses which cycle spells the text and in which phase.
- Adjacent rows on the same sequence of wide strips move together. Trace one representative and reuse its exact word, in reverse order at emission. Gaps delimit bundles. Thin strips determine many of the original runs but relatively few rows. The narrow rows enter a counting sort and a residual table. Wide paths can be solved by tracing or by endpoint induction on the complement.
- The structural and performance outcomes differ. At threshold 16, the 233 large Wikidata blocks have a median 88.6689% wide area and median 5.4943% representative visits per original row. The frozen example has 810,057 wide occurrences and 50,709 traced letters, or 15.975×. The decoder still writes the output and builds/walks its residual table. Small H or few switches can leave long, unit-width words. Equal run density leaves discovery cost largely unconstrained.
- On one Xeon VM with a four-CPU quota and no exposed SMT siblings, the queued endpoint's paired whole-Wikidata wall ratios are 1.171/0.918, 1.211/0.931, and 1.091/0.928 versus eager/lazy for the calling thread, two workers and four workers. The frozen heap figure is 6.287 MB versus 10.353 MB additional requested bytes. The target i9 result and any default decision remain open.
- The factorisation and band bound are proven. Constructor counts, heap and wall results are observations. Broader predictions about cut width, fragmentation and relation to Cells are interpretations. Record 57's “90% of the letters but 10% of the rows” cannot mean BWT byte occurrences on a shared denominator. Distinguish run records/labels, if that is the intended numerator, from retained byte rows. A large percentage of runs can be narrow while most byte rows lie in wide runs.
