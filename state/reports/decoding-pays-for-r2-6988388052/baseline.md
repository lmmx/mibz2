# Primary-record baseline · boundary persistence · round 2

Within a run, LF moves two adjacent rows by the same shift. A gap travels with that pair until it reaches a run boundary. Count how many gap chains last at least k steps. The short tail of those counts helps predict how the Cells cut count and the forest arc count vary across the 233 large Wikidata blocks.

- The partial map on internal gaps is injective. Its terminating chains have one vertex at each distance 0 through their lengths, so the exact-distance count q_k is the survival count. A primitive block has no gap cycles because the finite common prefix grows by one per step. A periodic block can have gap cycles between equivalent rotations. The full positive-depth sum equals n−r on primitive blocks. The truncated sum distinguishes how chain lengths are distributed.
- Overlap the two partitions at the run and run-image cuts. If C interior cuts coincide, the level-zero arc count is E₀=2r−1−C. The internal count q₁=E₀−r is one less than the number of forest chords. The archived profile counted row zero at its first positive depth d₀. This is depth one on 229 blocks and depth two on four, so the archived first bin equals the chord count only in the first group. The historical regression used the original profile; its reported outputs should be labelled with that convention.
- Cells cuts, adjacent-rank visits and forest arcs have medians 1.837, 1.929 and 1.778 per run. They count different objects. The Cells–forest Pearson correlation is about .906, with fitted residual correlation .393 after density and first excess and −.061 after adding the recorded short tail. Leave-one-out errors also improve for both. Adjacent-rank counts are less well captured. The same-column forest tie change 12→15 disproves a method-independent event count.
- Refining sites reclassifies arcs as sites. It reduced first excess but raised summed arcs on the tested block. The row model gives span 87 in 12 rounds and 1,337,701 executed steps. Neither this depth nor the exact profile identities give a machine-time formula. Division accelerates repeated passes through an atom on some synthetic inputs. The copy approach pays to obtain LF coordinates; its small grammar or replay estimate by itself does not solve that.
- Whole-stream times require the right reference and worker mode. Open Return's paired ratios against eager on the VM are 1.171, 1.211 and 1.091 on the calling thread, at two workers and at four workers. The adjacent-rank run-record comparison is about 11.5× on the calling thread and 7.8× at four workers. Its sparse five-run speedup uses the direct arm and the default route's Segmented fallback.
- The safe conclusion is empirical: these particular counters track short-range survival under these designs. No exact common accounting identity, structural lower bound or optimality follows. A new method could change its event schedule, so ask what it counts, whether its residual work tracks the profile, and what it costs end to end.
