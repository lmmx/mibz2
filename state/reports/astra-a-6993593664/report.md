## Scope of the exactness claims

For a domain U with known translation e and emitted word u, the next domain V can supply a second word everywhere on U only when U+e is contained in V. Under that condition, the already written adjacent pair supplies the combined spelling, and adding shifts gives the correct exit. The source ends in completed output. If the whole-domain check fails, U+e straddles immutable original domains, so one longer common original-domain itinerary cannot cover all entrances in U. Equal emitted bytes alone do not certify equal exit displacement. The five-row witness distinguishes the two. This failure certificate is about the permanent-domain rule and does not rule out subdomain splits or other representations.

The small oracle checks both variants over 14,209 column/mark cases apiece and 30 additional capped cases apiece. Native capture for the inputs and Plain oracle does not turn the Python candidate or cache prices into production benchmark evidence. Corpus equality and the proof of marked output are distinct from malformed-stream integration, throughput and a shipping decision.

## Cost-model boundaries

Each private cache is a fully associative 256 KiB LRU. The team replay namespace is per worker, with a shared noninvalidating fully associative LLC and one line-reference opportunity per active worker per round. Dirty evictions and the final drains are charged. A shared-cache allocation from a complete private writeback need not read memory. The ordered cohort is 0, 48, 72, 96, 120, 168, 192, 216, 1, 2. These are synthetic concurrently assigned independent blocks, with eight jointly admitted spaced blocks and two consecutive extensions. The all-233 correctness set, 228-block span comparison and small priced cohorts answer different questions.

The model tracks setup, induction/baseline writing and candidate output until the marked bytes. It uses aligned logical addresses, pinned release buffer aliases in the comparator, and recyclable capacities. It grants Cells the common capped scans and charges the candidate's scans. It omits the compressed frontend, RLE1, CRC, allocator/OS effects and native copy ordering; the scheduling model has no hardware prefetch, coherence, actual stalls or measured fill buffers. Frozen whole domains raise read fills at 32 KiB from 152,136 to 198,333 against lazy while total transfers fall, and incur 317,081 cursor-dependent metadata reads. The 58,485 that miss at 256 KiB are modeled events. In the nine-block lazy population, an extra 1.665 transfer units per candidate metadata miss would exhaust the 256 KiB advantage if baseline stalls were priced at zero. The separate resident score with a priced writer loses its lazy advantage at 5.345 units per extra span. These sensitivities show where pricing can change the sign. They cannot be converted to exposed latency using this ledger. The Q8/scalar comparison is separately labeled and explicitly price-sensitive. The study does not claim a machine gain.
