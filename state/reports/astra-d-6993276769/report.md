# Astra D · independent evidence review

**PROCEED as a research record.** The frozen headline traffic numbers reproduce from the native reference archive under the stated cache model. The compact tiled forward walk supplies an achieved modeled traffic point. The final-slot repair is the credible candidate for a matched machine comparison. Speed, actual IMC traffic and peak RSS for either candidate remain open.

The [baseline](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/6ff819c33c595cc165ad6a7bda5445027a2d1e73/state/reports/astra-d-6993276769/baseline.md) was committed before I read the journal summary. ## Storage, serial work and correctness scope

- **Allocation census.** At 7 workers and 28 slots, requested peaks are 217,712,280 / 213,093,176 / 101,243,602 bytes for stock, repair and tiles. At 10 workers and 40 slots they are 290,896,400 / 284,313,749 / 140,155,032. `pool.rs` routes each block to one worker state and one slot in deterministic round-robin order, with sequential calls and capacity reuse. The metric is requested live heap above the already-loaded input. It excludes process RSS, allocator overhead, an internal transient during realloc and actual concurrency. Final-slot capacities agree across arms. I checked the requested-heap receipts against the generator and allocation definition. I did not rerun the Rust pool binary.
- **Navigation and dependency counts.** Replaying `tiles.py` on the pinned ten tapes reproduces all 60 rows. At frozen tile width 128, 57,836 pieces, 7,031 headers and 256 byte ends give 260,492 bytes. The 899,931 header loads and 4,148,528 descriptor reads match native site attribution. Every step obtains the next row from the selected descriptor. The sequential header-dependent scan and an observed load stall are different quantities. Frozen slot dependencies regenerate from the induction tape as 16,426 top and 24,961 bottom linked refills, a 32-rule marked descent and 9,628 copy-position chains. All nine admitted dependency rows match using archived peak-pair and rule metadata. The segmented ordinal 41 reproduces 511 starts, 16,443 longest links and 9,216,681 tile descriptor probes on the exact capped tape. The separately selected ordinal 270's 13,452-link and 12,335,693-probe receipt is cross-checked against the trace attribution, without a new control tape extraction.
- **Repair semantics.** Stable occurrence matching gives the affine forward map. Iterating to the marked length covers short cycles. For the final-slot repair, disjoint ordered destinations preserve every as-yet unread literal source when left movers run forward and right movers run backward. Marker fills come last using the saved edits. Frozen carries 34 eight-byte edits, including 22 contractions and 12 expansions, with a maximum +79-byte lead. Overall expansion alone would be insufficient to choose a copy direction. The fallback leaves encoded source bytes intact when the directory or output capacity is insufficient. The archived correctness receipts report 33 libbz2 envelope comparisons, whole-stream equality for all three arms, and block/stream CRC rejection. The present review regenerated the frozen model and structural ledgers, using those native function-level results as archived evidence.

## Model and stop decision

This is an ordered Lackey data-reference replay of the scanner and worker on warmed allocations with cold cache state. It uses an inclusive 32 KiB/256 KiB/effective 1–4 MiB hierarchy, set-associative LRU, 64-byte lines, write allocation and dirty drain. It leaves out hardware prefetch and coherence timing, physical address indexing, concurrent workers, and the later copy to the caller. The lower outer traffic on tiles comes with higher inner traffic and more serial work. For example frozen L1/L2 rises from 36.79 to 97.95 B/B and guest events from 22.37 to 82.32 per final byte.

The journal separately attributes **10.97 B/B and 24.8 GB/s** to an older 0.5.4 seven-worker i9 record, and **14.11 B/B and 28.6 GB/s** to the ten-worker 0.5.4 result. The latter's approximately +18% latency and INCONCLUSIVE registered verdict agree with its scoring record. The fill-buffer and approximately 2.2-misses-in-flight figures belong to another hardware study. The 0.5.6 release scaling is a separate measurement. None validates the candidates' modeled numbers. [Seven-worker record](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/276db91c46df7027534785562a89bdac669e635c/docs/journal/monodromy/2026-11-18-knee-dram.md) · [Ten-worker record](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/276db91c46df7027534785562a89bdac669e635c/docs/journal/monodromy/2026-11-18-imc-ten-scoring.md) · [Miss-cost record](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/276db91c46df7027534785562a89bdac669e635c/docs/journal/monodromy/2026-11-17-miss-cost-scoring.md) · [Release record](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/1edb29fdc3d42dfddfc3d60e46456b611323760c/CHANGELOG.md)

At the stated 4.8 GHz and two guest events per cycle, re-evaluating the historic 24.8 and 28.6 GB/s prices gives **2.710** and **3.357** events per modeled byte saved. Tiles price at **8.860** for the ten systematic blocks and **13.292** on the nine Cells blocks. Of the two T fallbacks, one nominally clears the ten-worker price (3.285) and the other fails (3.668). That gives a defensible stop for the tested general forward kernel under the stated prices. It supplies no bound against a different method or a separately priced narrow fallback.

On the eight slot-eligible blocks, the repair saves 12,075,968 modeled bytes for 9,196,920 extra guest events (0.762 per byte). The 204,964 additional net LLC hits absorb the remaining seven-worker margin at 57.388 assumed additional exposed cycles per hit. This is a break-even threshold with a bandwidth term and assumed instruction price. The historical machine had bandwidth headroom, and hits and guest events have no fixed latency or throughput conversion. The scope “candidate for a machine comparison” is warranted. A matched wall-time, IMC and RSS comparison would test it.
