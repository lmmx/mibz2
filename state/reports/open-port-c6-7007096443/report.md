## Admission and held-out provenance

**The threshold values are inherited.** The released LazyCells census rejects `records * 20 >= n * 3` and `records * 64 < n`. The port's `lazy_counts_admit` is their conjunction in positive form, `records * 20 < n * 3 && records * 64 >= n`. The port uses run-route record counts, with 1/64 included and 0.15 excluded. The development census is evidence for choosing to reuse those two gates for open. This is a design choice informed by development data, with no newly fitted numeric threshold. [v0.5.6 gates](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/530ed404a014a414ef0b62adfd947fa14d4533e6/src/cells_lazy.rs#L143-L159) · [Port helper](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/3f09500ee3289b0dd06948003f45c89aedd8ea36/src/cells_lazy.rs#L15-L31) · [Admission decision](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7005084600)

The development input digest list covers Wikidata and six synthetic corpora, including text. The held-out Wikidata manifest supplies twelve different 1 MiB windows of the **same** dated dump. I compared all window intervals in the two manifests. The smallest gap between windows of the two sets is 2,154,965,951 compressed bytes, so they share no compressed window. Level 1 re-encodes the same held-out bytes and is a guard for block-size sensitivity. The level-9 replication represents separation by window within one dump. The held-out prose, code and structured sets are assembled from sorted paths and fixed Git blobs. Their two levels share plaintext and are related guards. This confirms documented development/holdout separation for gate selection. It leaves past private measurements outside the scope of verification. [Development hashes](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/docs/journal/2026-11-19-open-admission/inputs.sha256) · [Development windows](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/bench/wikidata.json) · [Held-out windows](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/bench/wikidata-heldout.json) · [Held-out builder](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/bench/heldout.py)

**The dry-run record qualifies the phrase “never timed.”** The pinned recipe sets `DRY-RUN.txt` and then goes through the same `ab run` calls, including the held-out cells and sustained cell, and finishes with `census-timed`. The judge correctly voids a marked dry run, but generating timings and excluding them from the verdict are separate. ## Text and multiplicity

**Text is a policy primary corpus and a valid registration guard.** The frozen recipe lists `gen/text.bz2` at 8, 10 and 20 threads. Under G, any text cell with a wall interval wholly above 1, a wall point estimate above 1.02, or a peak-RSS ratio above 1.10 blocks adoption. The 1.02 point cap catches larger estimates even when the interval straddles 1. The status of text as a policy primary is compatible with its role as a must-pass guard here. The benchmark does not require a text speedup. It does require all three text cells. [Standing performance policy](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/docs/team/PERFORMANCE-POLICY.md#2-performance-net-useful-gain-weighted-to-what-the-decoder-is-for) · [Fixed recipe inputs](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/.just/bench.just#L2290-L2309)

The multiplicity structure is defensible for the stated single adoption decision. P1 applies Holm step-down to one-sided paired log-ratio gain p-values at the three worker settings requested as 8, 10 and 20 threads. All three rejections are required. This also controls family-wise error for separately reported P1 rejections, assuming valid per-cell p-values. An all-cells gain claim is a conjunction and would by itself already require the hardest component to pass. H is a separate conjunction, requiring the upper end of each held-out two-sided 95% interval below 1 (a one-sided 2.5% cutoff for each comparison under the t model). The same dump and machine induce dependence that does not invalidate this conjunction logic. S is one predeclared 10-thread, eight-pair, no-settle screen. It offers no alternative path to pass P1 or H and creates no fourth primary efficacy test. [Holm, 1979](https://www.ime.usp.br/~abe/lista/pdf4R8xPVzCnX.pdf) · [Pinned scorer](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/bench/open_port.py#L219-L269)

P2 is a median-ratio screen. G and S are acceptance screens using a point limit plus the absence of an individually detected slowdown. Passing them does not establish statistical non-inferiority, nor does the registration promise simultaneous 95% coverage across 39 guards. Adding more guard vetoes can reduce the probability of adoption, and treating the most favorable cell as a pass would change the design. An inconclusive P1 or H that misses its cutoff yields NOT ADOPTED. An inconclusive wall interval in G or S can pass where its point estimate and any registered memory condition pass, as written. The paired t intervals assume the per-round log-ratio errors are well behaved. The harness alternates arm order within cells, which addresses order effects without proving independence from slower drift. These considerations do not require a new acceptance rule before the run. [Registration decision rule](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/docs/journal/2026-11-19-open-port-registration.md) · [Arm scheduling](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/examples/ab.rs#L2714-L2724)

## The correction

**Freeze and enforce the full roster for a single confirmatory attempt.** The scorer currently creates the expected tuples from the corpora it finds in each JSONL. It only requires the primary and replication by name. An entire missing guard never enters that roster. Furthermore, a completely empty sustained JSONL gets past the `if srows` validation and raises a KeyError during scoring. The numeric check accepts a NaN for candidate peak RSS. All 24 existing tests passed at the pinned commit, but their happy-path synthetic roster contains just five corpora. With those same fixture builders, a file consisting solely of the primary and held-out replication across all three counts scored **ADOPTS** with the ordinary sustained file. A primary candidate RSS of NaN also scored **ADOPTS**. These were synthetic inputs, with no workload results opened. [Scorer validation](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/bench/open_port.py#L132-L215) · [Existing synthetic fixtures](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b6412e1e43cbe7281c8f61033f7223f131244037/bench/test_open_port.py#L14-L73)

The corrected scorer should validate the named 15-corpus roster across three thread settings, with 12 pairs per cell, and the single sustained corpus at 10 threads with eight pairs. That is 1,080 decision records and 16 sustained records before scoring. Use the registered builds and corpus digests recorded by the recipe. A whole missing corpus, empty sustained file, missing/extra tuple, or nonfinite or nonpositive wall/RSS should produce VOID. The existing P1/P2/H/G/S cutoffs, counts and roster remain suitable.

Make this same registration identify one confirmatory attempt on the i9 and retain its bundle or failure record whatever happened. A started timed attempt that is VOID leaves no adoption evidence under that registration. An inconclusive required gain is NOT ADOPTED. Neither opens a repeat to seek a pass. Untimed setup may be repaired before the confirmatory attempt starts. Any genuinely different future experiment would require separate prospective review and would leave the original attempt and outcome intact. The queue's once-per-commit workstation marker does not supply this rule by itself: Protocol §9 allows a new ready commit to be run. The standing CI language about one repeat for a flagged primary regression gives no retry allowance for an inconclusive adoption test. At the provenance check, no ready tag or i9 result for this registration was visible.
