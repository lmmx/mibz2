# Independent review of the open-return factorisation

Reviewed draft #565 at `e6df6aee5055840b8b6ed780e29727e23da6d42f`, for [#162 comment 6978698021](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/162#issuecomment-6978698021). The mathematical construction holds. The marked switch implementation needs one qualification, and the measured speed and heap results apply to their respective workloads and host. I inspected the committed evidence and ran independent untimed arithmetic and combinatorial checks. I did not rerun the Rust build, trial decoders, CRC mutations, allocation instrumentation or timings.

## 1. Restriction lemma · VERIFIED

Define LF by stably sorting source row indices by byte and then index. Restrict that sorted list to sources in S. Relative order is unchanged, and the row list is precisely the stable sort of the subcolumn v. Thus `rank_E(f(s_j)) = LF_v(j)`. This step works for any S, including a cut inside a run, with or without the mark. The mark is needed later to select the output orbit. [Proof under review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e6df6aee5055840b8b6ed780e29727e23da6d42f/docs/journal/2026-11-18-open-return/proof.md#restricting-a-stable-match)

## 2. Factorisation, orientation and marks · VERIFIED

Deleting all arrows sourced in S leaves in-degree zero at E and out-degree zero at S. The remaining components are entrance-to-exit paths, including zero-edge paths on E ∩ S, and cycles wholly outside S. Injectivity forbids a path joining another path or entering a closed cycle. Each entrance therefore gives one P value and word, and each exit is reached exactly once. Restoring the one retained arrow gives g = P ∘ LF_v with forward label v[j]w_k. From the marked exit, the inverse arrow traverses the complementary word backwards and appends v[j]. One lap of the marked orbit, periodically extended to output length, agrees with the ordinary marked LF convention. This includes fixed marks, disconnected complements, and multiple cycles of LF_v. P and its words remain fixed under rewiring the retained bijection with E and the labelled complement held fixed. Changing the masks is a different cut.

For `annb$aa`, f = [1,5,6,4,0,2,3], S = [0,4,5], E = [0,1,2], and v = `a$a`. The three complementary paths are 0, 1→5, and 2→6→3→4, with words ε, `n`, `nab`. Thus P = [0,2,1], LF_v = [1,0,2], and g = [2,0,1]. Inverse outputs from marked retained index 1 are `bana`, `na`, `$`. The subcolumn's two LF cycles become one return cycle here. The independent audit checks each possible retained mark for 30,557 small column/set pairs, including arbitrary subsets and all-cycles comparisons. [Constructor and emitter](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e6df6aee5055840b8b6ed780e29727e23da6d42f/docs/journal/2026-11-18-open-return/open_return.rs)

## 3. Stochastic complement · VERIFIED

Once closed complementary cycles are excluded, M_CC consists of finite shifts along residual path segments. Its nilpotence index is at most the number of surviving complementary states when that number is positive. The geometric sum in M_SS + M_SC(Σ M_CC^a)M_CS counts exactly paths whose internal vertices are complementary. For C empty only the direct term contributes. Keeping even one closed complementary cycle makes I−M_CC singular, although that cycle does not contribute to any return. For words, use the finite-language semiring, with ∅ for zero, {ε} for one, union for addition and ordered concatenation for multiplication. Determinism makes every nonempty return entry a singleton. This is the labelled deterministic extension of the matrix elimination. Meyer's irreducible-chain results apply cycle by cycle in this setting. [Meyer, SIAM Review 31 (1989), §2–3](https://doi.org/10.1137/1031050)

## 4. Band bound and tightness · VERIFIED

Represent an interior gap by the right-hand row of an adjacent pair. Its forward gap map is defined inside a complementary strip and is injective onto gaps internal to its image strip. A gap internal to a contiguous entrance interval has no predecessor. If the two rows reach the same exit component without crossing a strip boundary, they are in the same itinerary bundle. Otherwise the gap reaches a first internal boundary of the H strips plus s exit components. Distinct entrance gaps cannot meet the same boundary, so B ≤ e+H+s−1. Initial cuts, including byte cuts in F, belong in e and are not charged again. Since e ≤ H+σ and s ≤ H+1, B ≤ 3H+σ. A sharper instance count adds only those partition boundaries whose backward gap traces originate at an internal entrance gap surviving the byte cuts. That count equals the number of splits beyond e for the stated itinerary partition. The cheap cap can also use min(m,e+H+s−1).

Both bounds can attain equality. For the primitive column `110020000011`, threshold 2 and primary 9, S = [0,1,2,3,4,9,10,11], and E = [0,1,6,7,8,9,10,11]. H = 1, s = 2, e = 4 after byte cuts, σ = 3 and B = 6. Tightness also occurs without a primary split. The column `0221000000011011` at threshold 2 with primary 0 gives (B,e,H,s,σ) = (6,4,1,2,3). Concatenating ordered, disjoint-alphabet copies of this second column preserves the LF of each copy on its coordinates. Byte cuts keep its six bands per copy, H grows by one per copy and σ by three. This attains both bounds over the available alphabet. [Band construction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e6df6aee5055840b8b6ed780e29727e23da6d42f/docs/journal/2026-11-18-open-return/proof.md#how-many-pieces-survive-the-cut)

## 5. Endpoint induction and cost · VERIFIED, scope explicit

Label a prefix terminal k by the connector to e_k and the suffix exit s_i by its connector to terminal i. The connectors have empty words. The suffix path between them is the same isolated path as the dense LF construction, so first return to the prefix is P with labels w. Initially the links number H+s+e₀ ≤ 3H+2, where e₀ counts entrance components before the byte cuts.

For removed rightmost rows R, with distinct last-domain X and last-image Y, their incoming translation has source outside R and their outgoing translation has target outside R. Compose across R, retaining the reverse word X then Y. This invariant also applies after earlier eliminations, so the stored words are reversed itineraries over the original arrows. If widths are unequal the shorter record holds the composite while the longer survives at reduced width. Equal widths remove one record. When X=Y its translation is identity on the aligned rightmost interval, a component isolated in the current exchange, so deleting it cannot affect the protected prefix. The boundary is initially shared by both orders, and the suffix removal never touches it. Live strips never increase. The independent model compares every exit and strip-ID word against dense tracing and also checks itinerary boundaries, including returns outside the marked component. The supplied CSVs give matching endpoint/tracer B and U on all 438 listed block rows, the 431 Wikidata and six prose blocks plus the standalone frozen case.

The implemented bound is preprocessing work with the fixed byte alphabet and amortized vector growth. `each_run` scans R_c capped records, prefix/suffix lists take O(H), eliminate takes O(K) and creates G ≤ K nonempty binary rules, and projecting the bands into the inverse table costs O(m+B). Internally cached rules avoid repeated DAG traversal, but copying their bytes still costs O(U+B), including appended retained literals. The word copies, optional empty band processing and cached expansions fit the claimed O(R_c+H+K+G+B+m+U); the tracer instead pays O(R_c+H+⌈n/64⌉+B+m+U), with a fixed page limiting each tile lookup. K ≤ n because each step removes at least one row, and U ≤ A ≤ n−m because widths are positive and open paths are disjoint. Neither is bounded by H alone. For `L=1 0^w` with S={0}, the one complementary strip translates by −1, one entrance spells w bytes and the unaccelerated endpoint loop removes it in unit steps. Thus a small strip count cannot give a sublinear preprocessing guarantee.

The stated bound starts at capped records and ends at the built table and byte dictionary. It leaves out scanner/MTF preparation, residual walk and output writes, RLE1, CRC, pool overhead and the Segmented fallback. It also says nothing about allocator capacity or memory traffic. The 4m/8m residual table excludes per-byte queues, band records, DAG and byte dictionary, buffers and output. Logical phase work cannot predict a streaming throughput win on its own. ## 6. Switch census · VERIFIED identity, CORRECTED marking convention

Incoming narrow and outgoing wide occur on E∖S, with the opposite direction on S∖E. Their counts agree by |E|=|S|, and also on each LF cycle. Intersecting each image strip f(I) with each overlapping domain strip J counts edges I→J by overlap length. The two directional cut weights are h each, with 2h switch rows in total for that fixed colouring. This is a census over all mixed components. The emitter visits the marked component's portion. Words still require spelling, and the one-strip shift gives big discovery work even with O(1) switches.

The proof can take colours as fixed and add the mark afterwards. The executable instead passes p to `each_run`, which first reclassifies a wide primary as a singleton narrow piece. It computes the cut of that post-split tiling, then adds the mark if it is still absent. Describe the measured cut with the post-split colours. With `L=111000`, threshold 2, primary 0, the fixed original all-wide colouring has h=0 and one added port. Reclassifying the mark gives S={0}, E={3}, h=1 and ports {0,3}. This distinction leaves the counting identity and output correctness intact. ## 7. Measured claims · VERIFIED as recorded, with scope

Threshold 16 retains runs of width **at most** 16 and the mark. The 3,448 count is complementary wide pieces after that rule, rather than a claim that each wide piece is at most 16 rows. For the frozen 899,931-row pre-RLE1 block, the one-sided implementations record 89,874 retained rows, 3,204 bands (1,326 nonempty), 810,057 wide byte occurrences and 50,709 representative letters. Endpoint adds 7,786 initial records, 11,215 suffix steps and 7,334 rules. The two-sided wide trace has 50,698 letters under a different cut convention. The 233 large Wikidata blocks (n ≥65,536) have median A/n = 88.6689% and median U/n = 5.4943%. Ordinals 118 and 176 have equal n=900,000 and respective r/n 0.0634844 and 0.0634211, with U of 57,626 and 34,775 and K of 12,756 and 9,659. These are constructed near-density contrasts, so they show that density lacks enough information to predict this work.
