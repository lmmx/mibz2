# Open-return comparison · C6 registration review

**TARGETED CORRECTION before timing. Make the registered reference plan an admissibility check.** Keep the five-cell diagnostic and its decision rule. Have the scorer reject the family if the recorded `mibz2` reference fails to show exactly seven workers at ten requested threads and eight workers at eight. Bind the resulting observations to the registered i9 profile and the released source. The deterministic plan test supplies the expected LazyCells windows, 28 and 32. The thread observations supply evidence that the reference actually took that plan's worker path.

## Why the reference gate matters

The source control is pinned correctly. The annotated v0.5.6 tag resolves to `530ed404a014a414ef0b62adfd947fa14d4533e6`. Comparing it with PR #590's exact head shows only additions in `Cargo.toml`, `src/lib.rs` and `src/open_gate.rs` among the decoder files guarded by the recipe. The added paths are research-feature modules. `examples/ab.rs` builds the ordinary `mibz2` reference through `BzReader::parallel` at both counts. The plan test constructs the specified i9 profile and checks worker count, window and lazy mode. [v0.5.6 source](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/tree/530ed404a014a414ef0b62adfd947fa14d4533e6) · [Pinned plan test](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/tests/recalibrate_arms.rs)

The registration says a wrong arm worker count voids the family. The executable `--check-workers` derives expected counts from occupancy-arm names such as `mibz2-opend-7-28`. Plain `mibz2` has no expected count in that check. The topology reader checks 10 cores, 20 logical CPUs, their mapping and no quota. It does not establish the processor identity from the recorded machine details. A route or machine mismatch can therefore change the denominator while the scorer still issues a verdict. [Worker and topology checks](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/bench/two_arm.py#L238)

I passed 324 otherwise valid synthetic records through the exact registered judge invocation. All 72 reference records named just one worker, yet the judge printed ADOPTS for the synthetic `opend-7-28` gain at ten threads. Changing one candidate record to the same one-worker observation instead made the family VOID. These are validation probes with invented times, not workload measurements.

Freeze one explicit reference admission rule before the run. Require the registered i9 profile in the bundle and require the two reference worker sets in every reference record, using the same evidence standard as the candidates. Treat missing or inconsistent observations as VOID before issuing a verdict. This preserves the pinned production arm, sample count, corpus roster and gain gates.

## Remaining registration checks

- **Score.** The pinned recipe and judge fix 12 paired rounds per count and five Wikidata ×4 hypotheses across both counts. The estimator is the geometric mean of paired candidate/reference wall ratios. The gain test is one-sided paired Student t on log ratios against ratio 1, with Holm family α = 0.05 and first cutoff 0.01. The printed two-sided 95% paired-t intervals are pointwise. A lower limit above 1 yields SLOWER for the corresponding corpus, any such flag blocks that cell, and an inconclusive interval leaves any loss unresolved. The RSS gate is a ratio of median peaks at most 1.10 per corpus. The sole allowed overage is on a primary corpus, with a point estimate at least 5% faster, upper interval below 1, relative growth at most twice the gain and absolute growth at most 64 decimal MB. There is no separate percentage minimum for the ordinary Holm gain. [Registration](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/docs/journal/2026-11-18-open-return-comparison-registration.md#the-rule) · [Exact judge](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/bench/two_arm.py#L474) · [Rule 4](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/docs/team/PERFORMANCE-POLICY.md#2-performance-net-useful-gain-weighted-to-what-the-decoder-is-for)
- **Settle and history.** The registered invocation uses the default 2 s, passed into both timed `ab run` calls. `others_busy` sleeps for that sample before launching each child and repeats the sample if the host is busy. This avoids the recorded 0.25 s condition. Record 68 supports the choice with the tight admit run at 2 s and the fast back-to-back series. Distinguish the expected turbo regime from an observed clock result here. This timing invocation does not turn on per-decode clock telemetry. Preserve the literal default invocation in the evidence, including the registered settle, copies and rounds. [Recipe](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/.just/bench.just) · [Settle implementation](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/examples/ab.rs#L1720) · [Record 68 §4](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/4366e106475ac62f762066ab1d4cb742b7c55694/docs/journal/monodromy/2026-11-18-sustained-scoring.md#4-the-cold-start-candidate-tested-first-the-coordinators-addition-the-pathfinders-review-6986646879)
- **Admissions and diagnostics.** The current census is needed to interpret the contrast. `opend` follows the density line, and a below-line block may still exceed LazyCells' separate cell budget. Equality of the two selection sets on the prior Wikidata sample does not prove equality on a fresh corpus. The optional system-wide IMC/RAPL phase is a separate, five-repetition diagnostic per pair. The recipe enumerates the five judged pairs and the context pair at ten threads. It takes an idle baseline per rep for the DRAM rates and reports package energy and watts. These observations cannot change the timing verdict. [Wrapper](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/src/open_gate.rs) · [Counter implementation](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/bench/counters.py)
- **Action boundary.** ADOPTS and ADOPTS BY TRADE are labels for opening a B5 study only. An integrated candidate would require B5 receipts and a separate C6 adoption registration against then-current master. Neither timing nor counters changes 0.5.6's default in this study. Workstation evidence must be the user's archived C5 submission. [Registration decision](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/ba8f8e2e8101bc1d6eba8a99d4b323d19a01d481/docs/journal/2026-11-18-open-return-comparison-registration.md#the-rule)

The pinned scoring suite passed 145/145 locally. The mocked counters suite passed 13/14 then 14/14 on identical inputs. Its combined IMC/RAPL check requires the literal `0.7999`, while idle-rate subtraction depends on the measured mock subprocess duration. The failed run printed `0.7998` or `0.7997` for the DRAM ratios and the expected `0.9000` package-energy ratio. This is a diagnostics test stability finding. I ran no target-machine decode, native safety qualification or hardware-counter measurement.
