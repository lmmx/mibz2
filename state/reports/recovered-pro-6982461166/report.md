The copy optimum reproduces exactly. The natural width-16 isolated-rank cut reduces steps on all 233 large blocks and has a 17.02% median. The original 19% and the proposed 16,917-endpoint representation lack a recoverable exact recipe. I did not run the proposed replay experiment or collect new timings.

## 1. Rank neighbours, dependency order and Φ

**Equality — VERIFIED.** Half-open rank increases by one between adjacent rows within a constant L run, so LF(x+1)=LF(x)+1. If both walks continue through the same-run adjacencies, induction preserves adjacency and the emitted letters agree one step at a time. Stop at a run head, the marked/section cut, or another chosen boundary as appropriate. This proves a substring equality across a common itinerary. It grants no text-coordinate address or source availability.

**Well-foundedness — VERIFIED in a specified byte-level decomposition; the lost compiler is UNPROVED.** Store the first row of every L run as a literal, and define the copy parent of every other rank x to be x−1. Termination follows by strict descent to the head. This works on physical BWT rows even when LF has several cycles, as long as the required source rows and literals remain in the dependency universe. In a bundled construction use a separate representative for each word phase. For each phase instance represented by its leftmost physical rank r(x)≤x, the source for an unknown representative u can be taken from u−1 in the same run, and the source's representative satisfies r(u−1)≤u−1<u. Require equal letters across each representative's fibre, and cut or supply the literal when u is a run head. A single rank attached to an entire word or its entrance need not certify all of its phases. Source rows outside a marked orbit must be available or explicitly mapped by a proved equivalent rule. None of those implementation invariants can be read back from the lost code.

**Counterexample to whole-span scheduling or append availability.** Primitive text `aaabaab` has cyclic BWT `bbaaaaa` and rotation starts [0,4,1,5,2,6,3]. With ranks emitting the byte immediately preceding their rotation start, the run-head literals occupy text positions 0 and 6. The two maximal Φ-copy spans are target [1,4) from source [4,7) and target [4,6) from source [0,2). The two whole-span dependencies point to each other. Byte dependencies are acyclic: 2→5→1→4→0, plus 3→6. In particular target 1 reads later text position 4 even though the source has lower BWT rank. No whole-span topological order or direct append rule follows. The [independent witness search](grouping.py) reproduces it.

**Prior art.** Under standard sentinel suffix-array coordinates Φ(i)=SA[ISA[i]−1], with the usual cyclic predecessor convention at the first suffix. In emission coordinates a BWT row corresponds to its rotation-start position minus one, so translate the indices before identifying the copy parent. Navarro, Ochoa and Prezza's Lemma 6 shows contiguity under Φ away from the boundary, Theorem 7 proves the BWT-induced bidirectional scheme with at most 2r phrases, and Lemma 21 states the same smaller-ISA-rank ordering explicitly. That result concerns the macro scheme's validity. The cyclic version here uses the marked rotation phase and splits copies at the text seam. The further research question is how to obtain positions and evaluate copies collectively from the compressed input. Policriti and Prezza's run-space LZ result gives O(n log R) time for its stated reverse-BWT setting, an upper bound for that method. It supplies no lower bound against collective coordinate discovery. [Navarro–Ochoa–Prezza, Lemmas 6 and 21 and Theorem 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.09517) · [Policriti–Prezza](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.06257)

## 2. Fixed-seed greedy optimum — VERIFIED

At a common output boundary p, substitute the longest legal greedy phrase for the first phrase of an optimum continuation. Greedy is at least as long. Drop all covered phrases. If it cuts through a surviving phrase, retain only its suffix and advance that phrase's source by the same amount. The suffix still ends no later than the old phrase boundary, hence no later than the new destination. Its source bytes are still in the dictionary because the available seed-plus-prefix only grows. If the greedy step cuts through a later phrase, the first old phrase was removed, so the total count cannot rise. Repeat until the entire optimum starts with the greedy parse. The source uniqueness and copy-length distribution are separate. Every symbol must be representable from the seed if there is no literal operation. An independent exhaustive check against dynamic programming passed 13,260 binary seed/target pairs.

This is the unit-cost greedy longest-match result for a growing dictionary, specialised to non-self-referential append copies after a fixed seed. It falls in the Storer–Szymanski and LZ77 greedy literature. The fixed seed and precise copy restrictions should stay in the statement. A weighted instruction objective, a different dictionary or bit-optimality would need its own argument. [Storer–Szymanski, JACM 1982](https://doi.org/10.1145/322344.322346) · [Greedy-parsing optimality survey note](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304397514000309) · [Non-self-referential LZ77 convention](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10613516/)

## 3. Cheap reproducibility from the current corpus

### Frozen 11,145-copy parse — REPRODUCED exactly

I retrieved w00 from [the pinned bench-data commit](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/a0692452b67ec3a8833e1fc4adf58a4f09cd9e6a/windows/wikidata-20261019/w00.bin), matched its SHA-256 with the [corpus manifest](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/9cd25610131b66924eaa75813d0f649db79f354f/bench/wikidata.json), rewrapped the pinned block, and decoded it with libbz2. I recovered the pre-RLE1 input by the first-stage run encoding, then verified compressed, output and pre-RLE1 digests. Independent cyclic rotation sorting gives primary 264045 and the exact recorded BWT digest `db8764224ac4980112ddefbe85b8593f5236643fed71bf8f0b739567d4f2ac67`. A separate Huffman/MTF reader of the pinned compressed blocks reproduces the same digest.

The seed is bytes at the run-head emission positions **in forward text order**. This recovered convention matters: ordering those same bytes by BWT rank gives a different optimum. My implementation builds a suffix array for seed+target, finds the equal-prefix interval for each proposed length, and reads the earliest source position j in the interval. It accepts precisely j+length≤p. It greedily maximises the length, actually copies from already materialised bytes, and checks equality with the target. It allows a seed/output seam crossing when legal, as the recovery's D‖prefix model does; the winning schedule has zero such copies.

### Isolated ranks and 19% — qualitative claim REPRODUCED, exact median UNPROVED

I checked all twelve pinned window digests, decoded the bzip2 Huffman/MTF/RLE2 payloads into L columns, and checked all 431 pre-RLE1 sizes and window block counts against the records. I ran the published width-16 geometry and mark convention with an independent endpoint counter. Across all 233 large blocks, the original H, auxiliary-node count and K agree exactly with the committed Open-Return census before any deletion.

Define isolated rank x to mean that no complementary translation enters or leaves it. Equivalently x∈S∩LF(S). Its transport is a known empty path. Remove those ranks from both orders of the original row line, rank-compress the surviving strip coordinates, and rebuild the entrance and exit connectors for the smaller exchange. The two prefix terminal orders each drop the corresponding members of the known empty pairs. Those pairs can be restored via the saved original entrance and exit ranks. In the frozen case this drops 74,596 ranks and brings the geometry from 7,786 to 5,012 initial nodes and from 11,215 to 9,378 steps, a 16.38% reduction. Across the 233, every block improves, with 8.97% min, **17.0202% median**, and 23.56% max by per-block (old−new)/old. This is a fully specified interpretation of isolated pruning with known matching controls. The absent recipe could preserve a different connector partition, remove a different set, or use another counter. The 19% figure remains transcript-only. No time or restoration result follows from the step count. ## 4. Proposed oracle-input test — conditional commission, unrun

The proposed isolation is sound if the decoder receives a sufficient **pre-compilation** representation and the accounting matches the labelled control. “16,917 gap endpoints, translations and ordered literals” does not identify which intervals, which coordinate system, which lifetime is charged, or whether phase addresses are already supplied. The recovered document also reports other sample counts that need not identify these endpoints. A naked ordered byte seed is insufficient when its bytes stand for named positions. Freeze the representation before execution:

- **Historical-representation arm, conditional on a reproducible input builder.** Archive a versioned witness with n, primary, interval endpoints and deltas in named axes, each port's domain and image/phase anchors, and ordered literals with their destination or rank association. State any phase-to-output mapping granted for free. Establish coverage, exact byte equality, handling of wrapping and the mapping back to the marked phase with an independent checker. Require the builder to reproduce 16,917 under the declared count definition before identifying the arm with the lost test. No final phrase lengths or greedy append schedule are granted in this arm unless already implicit in the frozen tape.
- **Reproducible text-addressed surrogate for testing compilation and emission now.** Compute the full emission-position map τ from dense LF on the pinned primitive block *outside* the timed candidate. For each run-head row x, supply the pair (τ(x), L[x]). For other rows, set the source at τ(x−1). Encode the maximal half-open target spans with a constant source-minus-target delta, splitting at literal sites and the seam. Feed records ordered by destination, with n and the literals. On frozen this gives **10,954 spans** covering 848,747 copied bytes and 51,184 literal sites. The records satisfy a byte-level DAG, although whole-span scheduling can cycle. This version gives away output positions and permits a candidate to fill final offsets out of order or stage data, charging all staging and copies. A strictly append-only candidate must resolve forward dependencies at real cost. If the original tape stays unavailable, label the surrogate by its measured size and coordinate assumptions. It answers a deliberately more generous question and cannot recreate the historical endpoint count.
- **Keep replay-only as a diagnostic.** With an independently validated append list prebuilt, time applying sources, lengths and literal initialization into the final buffer. This gives a control for descriptor/application overhead and excludes compilation. The 11,145-copy oracle gives one such list. A ranking on that arm alone does not show that compilation from endpoints is cheap.

For the main cost comparison, freeze the hashed 899,931-byte target identity, exact labelled inverse build and width-16 arm, host, compiler and flags, inputs, allocation/reuse policy and CPU placement. The historical 2.392 ms is a budget marker, not a paired observation for a new test. Use prepared capped run records for labelled inverse and prepared immutable fixture records for the candidate. In both arms start with an empty length-zero output, identical buffer lifetime, and time all work until n final pre-RLE1 bytes have been materialised. Include candidate unpacking, phase recovery if absent from its input, index/graph construction, splitting, scheduling, joins, literal placement and copies. Do not include fixture preparation, I/O, RLE1, CRC or comparing to the stored target. Verify complete output outside the timer, reject incorrect runs, charge allocations symmetrically, and record descriptor and peak scratch bytes, joins, source reads, copies, and written bytes. Balance paired AB/BA order after warm-up and publish each pair. A positive paired margin measures the maximum budget left to derive the granted data. A negative margin rules out that candidate's execution, even with the oracle. A result for text-addressed input only says how much coordinate production can cost before the margin disappears.

**Budget arithmetic, not prediction.** An n-byte output within 2.392 ms requires 376 MB/s of logical writes. Counting one read for each of the 848,747 nonliteral bytes gives about 1.75 MB of logical read+write traffic or 731 MB/s. This says nothing about actual DRAM or cache reuse. At 12–16 bytes per record, 16,917 records would occupy about 203–271 kB before index and literals. The all-inclusive time averages at the historical target are 141 ns per 16,917 records, 37.2 ns per 64,258 recovered operations, or 214.6 ns per 11,145 oracle copies. These are alternative one-denominator budgets; none assigns the entire time simultaneously to each phase. The primary hazard is fragmentation and dependent address work. Thus the benchmark should distinguish input count, compiler fragments and executed copy count explicitly.
