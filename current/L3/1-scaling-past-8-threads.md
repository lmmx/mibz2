# 1. Released performance and scaling

- **Doing:** assess 0.2.8 on Louis's i9 and deliver it downstream.
- **In order to:** 0, put measured, correct decoder speed in users' hands.
- **Known:** version 0.2.8 shipped on crates.io and PyPI with #324, #316 and benchmark-only #350. The four-core CI tag comparison against 0.2.7 measured Wikidata wall −19.0%, −16.6%, −12.1% and text −25.9%, −17.4%, −10.2% at one, two and four threads. Pattern also improved; random/one thread worsened 1.2%, and one text cell's peak RSS rose 7.6% ([release LOG](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6767075813)). Louis later submitted a 0.2.8 i9 release-scaling bundle, but no Coordinator verdict is confirmed at this watermark ([submission](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6767537577)).
- **#324 adoption:** its original frozen no-harm rule FAILED on fallback cases; Louis explicitly accepted that cost for primary Wikidata/text gains, under the prospective owner-set [performance policy](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764355047). Safety work preceded merge. The change affects the Segmented path, not cooperative teams. On i9 Wikidata, a later paired run found 8–10% faster wall through four threads but no gain at 8–20, and a twenty-thread slowdown at one marginal cell was disclosed ([i9 run](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764875247)). The old FAIL is not relabelled.
- **#316 adoption:** old i9 target evidence measured +6.8% throughput and −14.7% CPU at ten threads, all 420 observations fast-mode with balanced arm positions. Its twelve-dispatch t design was held for false-acceptance risk, then retired unused. The owner-set route uses a deterministic off-target selection check, neutral paired CI, target gain and B5 safety, without treating the unsound t rule as passed ([decision](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764294229), [policy](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764355047)).
- **Release-scaling context:** the missing 0.2.7 bundle was caused by a stale-tag fetch, fixed in #342. Louis then submitted a new 0.2.7/0.2.6 i9 bundle; Coordinator checked output identity and found no measurable overall difference across one to twenty threads. One 16-thread cell favoured 0.2.6 by about 2%, without multiplicity correction; it was not read as release-wide harm ([result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764059651)).
- **Cold-start context:** W1 found that holding one spare core busy throughout decoding removed the i9 slow mode, whereas launch spin and pinning did not. #350 merged an optional held spinner for workstation benchmarks; no library cold-start change was made ([W1](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6765016578)).

- **New maintenance:** #353 merged the run-symbol-share admission check retained from the failed universal-walk construction, so short high-entropy blocks keep the column path. #358 merged a 512-byte-aligned move-to-front list to keep it within one page; its paired bench was neutral and it protects future team-path comparisons from a build-dependent records cliff ([#353](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/353), [#358](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/358), [D097](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6770483223)).
