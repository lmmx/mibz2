# 1. Released performance

- **Doing:** integrate only qualified decoder gains, in order to serve item 0. Version 0.5.14 is published; the MTF fold remains a research prototype awaiting review ([release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7159134690), [PR](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/968)).
- **Shipped sequence:** open-diet adopted on the i9 at 6, 8 and 10 threads with less memory; the later construction stack adopted at 6 and 10 and merged. Version 0.5.12 shipped that stack. These are separate tests and releases ([diet](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7062561800), [stack](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7073092298), [release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7073886076)).
- **Latest worker plan:** 0.5.14 ships lazy workers at five and seven threads (wall ratios 0.840 and 0.631) and eight workers from ten threads (0.929 at ten, 0.932 at twelve), on the i9 unpinned. The release's four-core comparison was neutral, as expected for i9-specific changes ([release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7159134690)).
- **Next candidate:** an isolated MTF-to-construction fold census showed a 27.6% saving in the two passes on a Xeon VM; the i9 wall effect is predicted with a wide range, not measured. PR #968 is open and the frozen paired-oracle test judges one and eight workers. No adoption verdict is established ([census](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/docs/journal/2026-11-24-algorithm-level/mtf-fold/README.md), [registration](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/docs/journal/2026-11-24-algorithm-level/mtf-fold/registration.md), [PR](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/968)).
