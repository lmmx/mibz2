# 4. Alternative decoder architecture

- **Doing:** follow Louis's new goal of moving the switch from independent Cells workers below six physical cores to Cooperative teams at six or more.
- **In order to:** 0, improve the released decoder across its scaling regimes.
- **Current result:** two induction builds won. #408 recycles the dead front slot; #414 uses 8 B bottom slots, saving induction lines at the price of dependent loads. Both shipped in 0.3.2/0.3.3. Equal one-row-cell elision #418 lost 8–12% on CI despite many eligible cells, so the Cells induction campaign stopped. The writer rewrite #407 also lost 5–7% and closed ([recycling](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6894374065), [layout](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6895145558), [elision](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6895882037), [writer](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6894223006)).
- **Why direct builds replaced the diagnostic plan:** the phase run identified a large unaccounted physical-traffic residual, not a lever. A residual study needed owner-gated processor writes and closed unrun. A subsequent phase-cap experiment stopped at privileged counter preflight and closed without data once direct builds had decided the writer and induction arms. Neither failed diagnostic disproves a candidate architecture ([phase](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6886389292), [residual](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6892999618), [preflight](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6894597405)).
- **Locality proof's scope:** #410 corrected the earlier fixed-address reorder witnesses: they were not byte-exact realizable executions. The amended certified compulsory floor for the present layout/tree at a modelled5MiB cache share remained CLOSED, because it never relied on those witnesses. The smaller-cache bounds remain wide; a different slot layout or grammar is outside this result ([correction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6894415432)).
- **Cooperative Cells:** a within-block scan can reduce dependency depth only if its ordered-word operators are found cheaply and its added grammar/work/traffic are affordable. At four cores, independent blocks already fill workers and the earlier shared-block compute floor (.196s) loses against independent wall (.162s). Louis left Cooperative Cells out of the first cap screen; a failed cap's wall time would not refute it because capped workers idle. The screen took no data. A high-thread construction must price its own computation, memory and synchronization against the shipped Teams path ([relay](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6885923088), [owner scope](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6893034258), [direction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896261240)).
- **Prior closed arms:** the 16 B slot had no primary wall gain and closed; the AHT/Lackenby ordered-word steelman failed; the current-tree reorder closed at its modelled floor. These verdicts do not by themselves decide the new knee-moving aim ([16 B](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6884914379), [locality](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6894415432), [direction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896261240)).
