# 4. Alternative architecture and mathematical research

- **Doing:** price decoder parts and pursue representations only where their information and runtime costs can be paid, in order to serve item 0 ([acquisition direction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/645), [surface](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/issues/943)).
- **Measured parts:** a full ideal-parts matrix showed scanner × construction as the largest interaction, but its all-ideal floor included oracle handover copies. A later zero-copy run removed those copies: residual plumbing was 5.2–5.7 ms, the scan alone 179 ms at eight workers, and serial delivery copy about 48 ms. It did not support memory as a third binding station at eight to ten workers. One workload and machine limit the inference ([matrix](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7169170402), [floor](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/docs/journal/2026-11-24-algorithm-level/ideal-parts/results/floor/README.md)).
- **Pooled scan:** the prototype ran as intended and preserved exact bytes, but the frozen rule KILLed it at both judged counts: capture 0.52 at eight and 0.48 at nine, below its interval requirement; eight-worker wall was 1.052 of baseline. At ten it improved 6.4%, an observation that does not change the verdict. Future hybrid or re-test is a separate candidate ([result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/docs/journal/2026-11-24-algorithm-level/pooled-scan/results/README.md)).
- **Open alternatives:** the frontier surface holds a research MTF fold, forward emission and a BWT-space return-map idea. Several earlier mathematical and machine-model leads were stopped or killed on measured cost, not promoted to production. Their current status and evidence sit in the [frontier status](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/issues/943).
