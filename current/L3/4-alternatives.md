# 4. Alternative decoder architecture

## Service-core

- **Purpose:** test whether dedicated service threads improve whole-decoder wall time and processor cost, in order to serve 0 ([registration](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/258)).
- **Run:** #258's frozen ten-thread comparison was reviewed, queued and executed on Louis's workstation. Its noise control passed and the result was VALID. Service-2x3 against released 0.2.6 default measured speed 1.045 (1.017–1.073), CPU default/arm 1.146 (1.123–1.170), and RSS 64 versus 74 MiB. The CPU saving is 12.7%, correcting the Coordinator's first 14.6% expression ([LOG](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6729896575), [correction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730014313), [journal](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/b5644677/docs/journal/2026-11-09-service-2x3-result.md)).
- **Other cells:** service-2x3 versus plain 2x3 speed 1.402 with CPU ratio 0.941, a CPU cost. Pipeline 2x3 versus default speed 0.866, a regression. Service-2x3 versus pipeline 2x3 speed 1.207 was reported but not judged. These compare whole bundles and do not isolate a single service-thread mechanism.
- **Premise and limit:** trace maximum service utilization was 0.70 and preparation idle fraction 0.165: spare service capacity, preparation starvation, not saturation. A larger lookahead admission threshold may help, but this is a hypothesis. The threshold can admit preparation through L+K−1; it is not a strict window cap ([Guardian](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730103867)).
- **Status:** the registered decision was FOR SVC2@10, not adoption. Guardian D045 holds further service-core timing and production admission until targeted B5 reached-path fuzz and mutation evidence is reviewed; no concrete invalid access was found ([review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730103867), [Coordinator acceptance](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730114756)). #271 may register, freeze and dry-run, but its queue entry and timing wait on the hold's closure.
