This is an owner trade-off, not a changed measurement result ([adoption](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764294229), [release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6767075813)).

The retired twelve-dispatch judge was unsound for its proposed mean claim and remains so ([D096](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764503403), [policy](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764355047)).

The Observer's authenticated task is still pinned to a2da49638ac54d7763de1c167bd054f67ffdc763, whose two named files are the complete run instruction. A repo policy change does not silently re-pin that task; applicability of new authority to future Observer actions requires Louis's re-pin path ([ownership LOG](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764820715), [pin policy](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/a2da49638ac54d7763de1c167bd054f67ffdc763/docs/team/SPEC.md)).

The new i9 0.2.8 scaling and downstream wikidata-pq effect are still unmeasured at this watermark ([release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6767075813), [i9](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764875247)).

Neither result disproves all team-path improvements or all backward walks; #353 retains a narrower admission correction ([ports](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6764570856), [walk](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6765942197)).

Independent merge rounds leave coordination cost and a full proof/census check open; no target-machine or whole-decoder gain follows ([#351](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/351), [reader review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6766776045)).
