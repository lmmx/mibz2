# Where the "why" chain is weak

- **Frozen rule is not a result:** #631 has a reviewed scorer and one started attempt, but no covered i9 adoption verdict. B5 safety readiness is not merge approval; memory bound describes logical elements, not retained capacity or process RSS ([D115](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7007260113), [freeze](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7009741436)).
- **Admission generality:** reusing LazyCells' gates follows a per-block development census. Held-out compressed inputs and non-Wikidata guards are registered to test whether that transfer holds. A threshold that merely recognises corpora was explicitly rejected ([correction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7004927411), [admission](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7005084600), [registration](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7007034930)).
- **One-attempt identity:** earlier dry runs generated timing and were excluded; the corrected preflight retains no timing. A crash after timing starts consumes the single attempt, so VOID does not justify a retry under this rule ([review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7008650123), [freeze](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7009741436)).
- **Research to production:** scalar-cap beats ungated open in the moonshot harness, but no complete production path was measured. The counter failed its incremental rule. Neither alters the frozen port study ([scalar](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7004261019), [counter](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7001987495)).
- **Neon bound scope:** F_self_ns binds only an in-place Φ-sourced class; high-r/n potential schedules require run-transition labels presently found by an O(n) walk. Different operations and Cells steps are not directly comparable ([verdict](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7008576600), [addendum](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7008639445)).
- **Downstream interpretation:** #227's quick sample wall was level; −0.6%±0.2% is modeled full-dump ETA, not a measured full release ([merge](https://github.com/lmmx/wikidata-pq/pull/227#issuecomment-6994012414)).
