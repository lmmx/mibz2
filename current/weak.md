# Where the "why" chain is weak

- **Threshold result versus cause:** Workers won at6–9t and lost at10 on one i9, but the fall in advantage alongside a rising combined working set does not measure L3 misses. DRAM bandwidth or another co-running cost remains possible. A new layout cannot inherit the cause as established ([run](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896798131), [hypothesis](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6897826122)).
- **Owner exception is bounded:** nine-thread Workers cost12.4% more CPU, over the registered10% flag. Coordinator escalated and Louis chose Teams from10. The rule did not pass at9t and the choice grants no blanket exception ([rule](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896455773), [verdict](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896798131), [choice](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896854245)).
- **First crossover run stays invalid:** its forced arm had a smaller in-flight window and failed the same-plan sanity cells. Later figures are reported only, not combined with corrected run2 ([invalid](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896638632), [valid](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896798131)).
- **Footprint construction retracted:** the first 16 B top/12 B rule estimate omitted storage for short-factor lengths. Corrected accounting reaches only about1.84MiB median and a few blocks under1.8, while packed-offset lengths violate forced-route range. MONODROMY did not build; the suggested small/large-block test is not a registered result ([first](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6897885393), [correction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6897897097)).
- **Downstream and queued runs:** decoder work is roughly4% of wikidata-pq's CPU, but its 0.3.3 version ladder has no covered result. The first attempt took no measurements; #229/#230 repaired preflight/queue. Master lists threshold/release runs awaiting ready tags after the ladder summary; no 0.3.4 workstation gain follows from listing them ([checkpoint](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/222#issuecomment-6898054629), [release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6897858125)).
- **Earlier locality and release scope:** #410's certified floor is for its then-current tree/layout at modelled5MiB, not the proposed smaller block. 0.3.3's four-thread tag CI gain and #417's mixed i9 scaling are distinct from 0.3.4's changed thread policy ([locality](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6894415432), [0.3.3](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6896261240), [#417](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/417)).
