# Where the "why" chain is weak

- **Pinned lower-count interpretation corrected:** earlier four-, six- and eight-thread worker reductions looked like cache-contention gains. Unpinned tests show those are mainly scheduling wins from freeing a CPU for the front end. The old contention fit must exclude these points; the pinned production policy itself remains measured ([unpinned](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6949008682)).
- **0.5.3 ten-thread release row:** affinity hid the7/28 trim, so that row neither confirms nor refutes it. Separate explicit-arm pinned and unpinned runs support the trim; 0.5.4 tag confirmation remains queued ([scaling](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6946756938), [unpinned](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6949008682)).
- **Main-memory bandwidth:** the knee diagnostic found rising L3-miss reads per output byte with busy workers, but IMC counters were unavailable. DRAM saturation remains an unresolved hypothesis, not a measured bottleneck ([knee](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6948385717)).
- **Downstream reach:** wikidata-pq#227 remains open at0.5.2 while the bump is deferred. Its cap4 and pipeline result do not follow from the i9 decoder's pinned/unpinned measurements ([PR](https://github.com/lmmx/wikidata-pq/pull/227), [release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6952096740)).
- **Rejected performance arms:** fused finish and stop-and-walk were not adopted under their frozen rules. Indicative effects or structural results do not overturn those verdicts ([fused](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6947507581), [walk](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6922810916)).
