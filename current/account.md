The team has enacted a new, forward-only route for judging decoder changes whose target path is absent from its usual continuous-integration test. Its first proposed use is still a draft: the guarded service candidate needs a fresh registration, and the Coordinator has asked whether a more powerful test of the unchanged off-target limit is admissible. In parallel, a merged build-stage improvement remains unreleased, and the Pathfinder is designing new decoder paths rather than running its earlier copy-walk diagnostic.

## What changed in the acceptance rule?

Louis's checked [re-pin of the four existing Work roles](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755222109) brought their saved instructions to the amended rules. The Coordinator then [merged the target-gated route](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755332475) into the standing stewardship rule. It applies only to registrations frozen after that merge, with their qualification tests specified before data. The older guarded-service workstation result remains valid evidence but cannot qualify under it.

A fresh [guarded-service registration](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/316) narrows the proposed target to Louis's i9-10900K with unlimited CPU quota. Preparing it exposed a per-thread-count hardware-discovery cost in the earlier candidate, which the new draft removes outside ten threads. Its off-target requirement covers the complete 42-cell continuous-integration grid. At the usual ten rounds, the Coordinator estimates that the unchanged 2% noninferiority bound would almost never be established even for a truly neutral change. A sharded, higher-round workflow is [before the Guardian for an admissibility ruling](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756088437). The draft is neither frozen nor queued; the evidence rule itself has not been relaxed.

## Where did the Pathfinder's work go?

The Pathfinder [corrected its copy census](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755498073) after the Researcher's boundary counterexample: the revised count is about 93% copied bytes, not the original 94% claim. Its exact local copy-walk prototype was slower than production, and overlapping its chains did not help. The proposed copy-walk workstation diagnostic was withdrawn before any run. The prior-art sweep found that copying decoded text during inversion is known; no priority claim remains.

Two other diagnostics followed. The [bandwidth-interference test](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755766766) could not isolate a memory ceiling from the core and power effects it introduced, so the frozen recipe was withdrawn. A [parallel scanner comparison](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755860961) retains information value, but needs failure propagation, bounded cleanup, reporter checks and safety evidence before re-review. No Pathfinder workstation diagnostic is queued. Separately, its backward-table design was [handed to the Coordinator's run-record emitter work](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755932529); local correctness and timing are leads, not production results.

## What is ready for release or measurement?

The [corrective build-window change](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/312) is merged but not released. It made the build stage 9–28% faster in its registered comparisons and reduced processor time on four-thread Wikidata by 1.6–3.1%; three isolated slowdown flags remain unresolved. Louis still has a release decision to make. The [workstation queue](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/3e1a7ec3/bench/workstation-queue.txt) holds only the older version 0.2.7 release-scaling check, with no submitted bundle confirmed at the Coordinator's last [report](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756088437). The run-fed leader's two i9 comparisons remain inconclusive and closed; the spin hypothesis was not confirmed.
