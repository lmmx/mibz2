Version 0.2.9 is released with a measured Wikidata gain at higher thread counts and a small one-thread loss. The next workstation comparison has been prepared and passed its checks, but it has not been integrated or run. Separately, a new harness to find why a promising inversion kernel did not speed up whole decoding has completed independent review and needs one targeted correction before it can run.

## What is ready, and what is still waiting?

The earlier [one-thread comparison](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6782294636) asks whether an inlining change recovers speed against 0.2.9. Its occupancy check initially failed because of inherited accounting assumptions, not a demonstrated decoder fault. The Guardian [accepted the corrected accounting](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6790889882) for those paths after independently replaying the traces. The corrected repair was then appended to the existing [comparison branch](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/366), preserving its candidate, baseline and ten-pair rule. Its seven Actions and legacy check are now green. One secondary random-input case is 3.3% slower in the paired bench; the Coordinator kept that result and recommended a separate, deliberate integration as diagnostic preparation, without calling it a production speedup. The branch is unmerged, no ready job has been queued, and no workstation result exists.

The [new diagnostic harness](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/373) addresses a different question. A local and untimed CI check shows it can run the actual decoder reader, compare instrumentation off and on, and retain output and error parity. The intended later programme has ten paired rounds across three corpora and two instrumentation modes, with 120 measured decodes and nine preliminary checks. Those measurements have not run. The existing occupancy ledger makes one check fail, the legacy pre-commit status fails for an unknown reason, and another CI workflow was queued at inspection. The [independent review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6821626797) checked source correspondence and paired arithmetic but found that failed child processes can lose wall time, CPU time and peak memory already collected before their error is reported. The Coordinator assigned one correction: save those measurements before rejecting a failed run, while keeping failed rows out of successful paired estimates. The reviewer found no new safety blocker in the changed unsafe code. The repaired accounting must still be integrated separately before this harness can be considered ready.

## What did the research establish?

A pinned workstation run measured the cells inversion stage at about 82% of the current stage's time, but the experimental whole Wikidata decoder was level. Codex's [all-block census](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6782495828) found that the new route admits 84.117% of Wikidata rows, so refusal alone is unlikely to explain the missing gain. The first cells implementation's specific safety objection was independently resolved, yet its corrected head failed the pre-set default-performance condition and remains unmerged. The new harness is meant to price the live reader and inversion work; it has not established a cause or a gain.

A separate phrase-level idea passed a bounded traversal screen on one real block but [failed its metadata budget](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6808483473), so that representation is not a candidate for compiled-cost timing as built. Later saved-trace pricing retained a compact global pass as a comparator, called for revision of selective batching, and stopped standalone origin optimisation on current evidence. These are research dispositions, not production changes. The proposed extra warm-inversion comparison is still a separate, unadmitted programme; it has not been added to the live-reader harness.
