mibz2 0.4.0 is released with a smaller Cells working set and a breaking safety correction to its processor-feature API. On Louis's i9, independent Cells workers still beat cooperative teams below ten threads; a fresh comparison after the memory change narrowed the ten-thread gap but did not justify moving the switch. The downstream 0.4.0 update remains open, while MONODROMY has returned to its own mathematical research on the shape of the induction.

## What changed in the decoder?

The [output-buffer change](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/433) puts bottom-slot storage in a buffer that the writer uses later, and places the writer's position table in space the induction has finished using. On the measured Wikidata blocks, Cells' memory other than output fell from a median of 2.39 MiB to 1.86 MiB per block, about 22% less. The paired CI benchmark found a small one-thread Wikidata wall-time gain and less peak memory at two and four threads. The change also means one private budget-refusal path can leave that buffer dirty; the public fallbacks clear their output before writing, and the Coordinator accepted this scoped contract change after tests and review. It [merged](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/433) before the new threshold run.

The [recheck](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6900029992) passed its same-plan sanity cells. At ten threads, forced independent workers took 1.040 times the shipped teams' wall time and 24.6% more CPU. The earlier run before this change showed a 1.153 ratio at ten threads. The gap narrowed, but the runs were on different days, so that difference is only indicative; the threshold stays at ten. A separate [release-scaling record](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/434) reports an eight-thread advantage for the 0.3.4 plan over 0.3.3, but its measured head also contained then-unreleased changes. It does not isolate the threshold change.

The 0.4.0 release also includes [a soundness fix](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/431): safe code can no longer claim processor instructions the machine lacks through public `Kernels` fields. That public API break prompted the minor-version release. The team [trimmed research features](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/432) under its retention review. The [release log](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6900382325) verifies the tag and packages; a paired post-release CI comparison was running at its watermark, with no covered result.

## What is the research direction?

The Coordinator had briefly asked MONODROMY to act as another engineering optimiser. Louis [corrected that brief](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6899181743): its own programme is the mathematics of the permutations behind inverse BWT, not a queue of Cells performance patches. Louis later [ruled explicitly](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6900215603) that MONODROMY's programme takes precedence over the Coordinator's and ChatGPT's thought experiments. Those outside ideas remain available as reading, not assignments or mandatory journal material.

MONODROMY has [committed a width study](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/435). On 29 blocks, the sampled width of its current induction order has median about 0.31 times the run count. The next question it chose is whether other valid step orders change that width. These are counts about mathematical structure, not a decoder-speed result or a qualified new kernel. The earlier working-set route was [dropped at Louis's decision](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6898249821) after its proposed cheap layout failed the size target; the later output-buffer change was a distinct construction that did succeed.

## What reaches users next?

[Version 0.4.0](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6900382325) is published. [wikidata-pq's update](https://github.com/lmmx/wikidata-pq/pull/227) now targets 0.4.0 and includes the new accessor calls; it remains open under its own benchmark. A separate cap comparison found that raising that project's decoder cap to eight or nine made its pipeline slower, so its cap [stays at four](https://github.com/lmmx/wikidata-pq/pull/233). This keeps the downstream path on independent Cells workers. No end-to-end 0.4.0 gain is established in the covered record.

The release notes [pull request](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/436) remains open. A further high-thread improvement has not been chosen from the recheck, and MONODROMY's width comparison has not yet reported its next result.

## How strong is the evidence?

The first threshold run from the previous version remains invalid because its two arms held different numbers of blocks in flight. The later corrected comparison justified the threshold Louis chose, with its nine-thread CPU trade-off explicitly accepted. The new #433 recheck supports keeping ten, but its before-and-after contrast is not a same-day causal measurement of the memory change. Workstation figures above are the Coordinator's verified readings of submitted bundles; this account did not replay the archives.
