mibz2 0.5.14 is released, and the next decoder experiment is awaiting review. The project has replaced its coordinator and role inboxes with a shared work record in [frontier/](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/frontier/README.md); that record now carries open experiments, results and decisions.

## What changed in the decoder?

The [latest release](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7159134690) changes the worker plan on Louis’s ten-core machine: lazy workers at five and seven threads, and eight workers when the caller asks for ten. Those changes qualified under registered workstation tests. Earlier releases also adopted a [smaller working set](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7062561800) and [faster construction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-7073092298) for selected blocks.

A study that removed decoder stages one at a time found that the serial scan alone takes about 179 ms of a 195 ms eight-worker decode when every other part is free. It also exposed about 48 ms of serial output copying. Those figures describe one Wikidata workload on Louis’s machine, not a universal limit. The first attempt to move scanning onto workers [failed its frozen test](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/docs/journal/2026-11-24-algorithm-level/pooled-scan/results/README.md): it was slower at eight workers and missed the required capture of the scan’s potential gain. Its verdict stands, though a later construction improvement could justify a new test under a new rule.

## What happens next?

A [candidate fold](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/docs/journal/2026-11-24-algorithm-level/mtf-fold/README.md) combines two passes that currently create and reread an intermediate run list. A research prototype is [open for review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/968); its measured saving in an isolated census does not yet establish a faster released decoder. The [frontier status](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/issues/943) also lists work on forward emission and other representations. A bump of the downstream Wikidata pipeline to 0.5.14 is [open](https://github.com/lmmx/wikidata-pq/pull/238); its full-dump effect has not been established.

## How does work now happen?

Louis [chose](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/master/frontier/README.md) a shared file-based process in which participants take a bounded claim, leave an evidence-linked delta and let another participant review consequential integration. The [old role specifications and inbox protocol](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/977) were archived on 26 November, and [inbox #222](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/222) closed without merging. The old channel remains an evidence noticeboard, not the live routing system. The frontier status page identifies two decisions for Louis: the shape of a public block-reading API, and whether to authorise a separately capped three-arm experiment. Those decisions do not block every other line of work.
