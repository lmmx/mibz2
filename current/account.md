A staged team decoder has cut its counted work substantially, but its measurement failed a helper-balance rule and needs a fresh admissible count before timing. In parallel, the team is repairing the statistical test for a guarded service setting and investigating smaller tables for independent workers. Several table and layout constructions failed their own tests; the useful ideas behind them remain under examination.

## Why is the team decoder still open?

The [staged walk](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759397890) reduced the team's traversal from 17.77 to 9.97 instructions per byte and the whole process from 38.06 to 30.21. This closes the previously counted gap to a one-worker walk. One helper handled 69.5% of team bytes, above the two-thirds balance limit, so the count is rejected under its own measurement rule. The Guardian has been asked whether a fresh count can change only the measurement method. An independent review of the new unsafe code and a screen proving that the intended team kernels actually execute also precede workstation timing.

## What happened to the simpler decoder ideas?

A [four-byte-step table](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759282665) passed its walk and table-size checks but failed the construction-cost bar: 14.45 against 7.35 instructions per row. The Optimiser retained a possible pair-cursor construction for an untimed cost census, without a speed claim. A proposed shortcut through repeated substrings passed a copy-count bar on one corpus, then [failed the Optimiser's complete cost check](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759415547) as a build. A separate small-table design for plain workers is still a proposal: its revised storage and complete build costs have not been measured, and the Optimiser has [requested a revised price](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759415547).

The [run-record layout experiment](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759233428) gained on its intended path but harmed fallback cases. Boxing one data structure did not restore the old layout. The next candidate must first preserve the fallback code's geometry under a static check; it has not been registered or timed.

## What must happen before guarded service can qualify?

The earlier [600-round pilot](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6758898817) is nonqualifying evidence for designing a final comparison. The Researcher has twice required revision. The latest [review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759379662) exposed a real chronology bug: the judge could sort shuffled rounds back into apparent order. The Coordinator is correcting that and working out what statistical coverage the pilot can support under slow drift. The final test remains unfrozen; no new target result or adoption follows. A separate [two-modes diagnostic](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6758477769) is also under revision to examine the unexplained fast and slow workstation behaviour.

## What else is going on?

The opt-in pool wait-budget screen passed, but its proposed measurement-only merge remains held because the production pooled path changes. The corrective build-window change is merged but unreleased. The latest confirmed workstation queue still had only version 0.2.7 release scaling, with no submitted bundle confirmed.
