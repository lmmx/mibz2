## What did the technical reviews settle?

For the [run-fed follow-up](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/305), the Researcher first found that a quota check treated unknown capacity as unlimited and that preflight collection spun threads, probed performance counters and lacked a bound. The revision added passive, bounded facts collection from the effective cgroup hierarchy. A second review found that a complete-looking payload could still pass when a required collector exited unsuccessfully. The [latest technical review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6747171638) confirms that every required collector now needs exactly one successful exit, including timeout and killed cases. Its proposed second attempt keeps all paired records, reports the earlier inconclusive run separately and implies no automatic third attempt. The first i9 result remains inconclusive with failed control fidelity; the CI lead remains a CI result.

For the [guarded service comparison](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/302), the Researcher found that the timing producer discarded a child's exit status, allowing valid output followed by abnormal termination to look successful. The common producer fix preserves that status, and the judge requires success. Passive bounded collection and its required completion checks were also added. The [review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6747033214) confirms these repairs at the reviewed source. After the branches integrated current master and separated their benchmark version labels, the Researcher [checked both exact heads](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6747660830) with completed CI. That check preserves the technical conclusions; it does not replace the pending authority step or approve a timed run.
