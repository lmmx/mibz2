Version 0.2.9 is released with a measured gain for team-based Wikidata decoding on Louis's workstation: about 11–15% faster from six to twenty threads. The release carries the earlier run-record method into the paths used at those thread counts. A downstream project has an open update to the new version, while a separate research kernel remains an example rather than a decoder change.

## What did the release establish?

The [team-path port](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6769926198) extends the shipped run-record front and backward table to plain teams, the pipeline and guarded service. Its early local timings suggested gains, but those were indicative and CI's ordinary bench did not exercise these paths. The [Guardian cleared](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6771767708) the missing plain-team fuzz coverage after a forced-route mode and logged reach/error summary were added. A first CI bench flagged one-thread losses; the policy's permitted rerun did not reproduce the primary loss. The paired [workstation comparison](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6773120400) then met the pre-data rule: Wikidata was about 11–15% faster at six to twenty threads, including 13.8% at ten, while text also gained at ten. Every arm produced the same bytes. The port merged and shipped in version 0.2.9.

A separate [move-to-front alignment fix](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/358) has already merged. It keeps a hot list inside one memory page and removes a stack-placement hazard that made one build's records/team case roughly 1.4 times slower. Its paired bench was neutral, so the fix is a stability measure, not a measured speedup. The workstation comparison included this fix on both sides. The proposed inlining patch to hold the non-team path's machine code fixed is now assigned as a separate maintenance change.

## What happened to the new mathematical line?

The research team's [run-induction example](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/351) has merged as research evidence. Its corrected local one-thread Wikidata measurements put the cells kernel at 0.82–0.86 times the current production inversion stage's time. It remains slower on high-run-density and adverse inputs. The example is not called by mibz2, uses unsafe code, and has not established a ten-worker or whole-decoder gain.

That result needs a correction beside it. The researcher [found](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6770249707) that the cells writer left bytes unwritten while its output check reused a buffer already containing correct bytes; an overlapping-copy call was also unsound. Both were repaired, the output buffer is now cleared before checking, and the earlier favourable timings predate the repair. Its final record reports twelve pinned local runs across machine states and discloses a disturbed thirteenth run. The old parallel schedule claim was corrected to a ceiling on possible turns; it is not an achieved speedup. Sparse-input figures from the defective writer were withdrawn until remeasured. No production qualification follows from the local kernel.

The team [published an explanation](https://lmmx.github.io/mibz2/blog/2026-11-10-rauzy-veech-reconstruction/) of the approach. After the Pathfinder's first review and Louis's feedback, the post was reworked to explain the mechanism through one concrete step and to correct the figures. The finished post now includes interactive steppers for the induction and Lyndon tree, with the Lyndon figure split into separate readings. No new independent review verdict is confirmed here. Louis asked three colleagues to support the researcher while leaving the next construction to its author.

## What has shipped, and what comes next?

The version 0.2.8 CI comparison found Wikidata decoding 12–19% and text 10–26% faster across one to four threads. The earlier registered no-harm failure of the shipped run-record path remains recorded alongside Louis's explicit adoption decision. The three cooperative ports and the universal backward walk closed as built under their fixed rules. The admission fix retained from the universal walk has since [merged](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/353) as maintenance.

The [release notes](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/360) and [wikidata-pq update](https://github.com/lmmx/wikidata-pq/pull/224) are open at inspection. Louis has been asked for a 0.2.9-versus-0.2.8 workstation scaling run. The Coordinator's four-core CI comparison found no whole-decoder wall change at one to four threads; the move-to-front and run-length stage was 3.4% slower in isolation, and one four-thread text cell used 3.6% more peak memory. The new version's downstream effect and release-scaling result are not yet established. The Coordinator recorded that the research example merged while one pre-commit status was still in error; that status passed on the same head shortly afterward, and the merge rule now checks both Actions runs and commit statuses.
