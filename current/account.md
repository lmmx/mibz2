## What did the service test establish?

Louis's [submitted run](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750239688) used the frozen source and comparison, and the Coordinator [checked its bundle and judged all 420 timed records valid](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750273151). At ten threads, the guarded path used two service threads alongside decoding teams. It beat the current default in wall time by 6.8% and used 17% less processor time under the registered comparison. Peak memory was lower too, but memory was reported rather than registered as a condition. The earlier [untimed preflight](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6745202683) had shown the path was reachable; this run supplied registered speed evidence for it.

## Why is adoption still open?

The comparison changed several parts of the plan together, so it does not isolate service threads as the cause. It ran on one machine, not across a class of machines. The registered no-harm bound was shown at eleven and twenty threads, but was **not shown** at twelve and sixteen: their intervals are too wide, although their point estimates and plans are nearly unchanged. An eight-thread identity check also moved despite equal plans. The Coordinator [sent those questions to the Guardian](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750273151) for the separate adoption decision. Nothing has merged on the strength of this result.

## What happened to the run-fed follow-up?

Louis's [second workstation submission](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750297742) was [checked by the Coordinator](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750327222): the frozen source and evidence digest matched, preflight passed, and all 140 timed records completed successfully. The main wall-time ratio was 1.0545, but its interval, 0.9290 to 1.1969, spans a loss and a gain. The control comparison missed its fidelity bound. This attempt is inconclusive, read alone rather than pooled with the first inconclusive attempt. The registration allows no third i9 attempt, so this timing line is closed without proving the earlier continuous-integration lead on the workstation.

Wall and processor time roughly doubled together across most runs. The host was busier than during the service test twenty minutes earlier. Those facts describe the run; they do not establish the cause. The [Researcher has the open question](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750327222) for any later workstation registration on this harness.
