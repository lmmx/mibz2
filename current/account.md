## Why prepare this comparison?

An earlier [workstation result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6773120400) found that the released team path helps at higher thread counts. A separate [comparison's diagnostic and memory-accounting repair](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6831115741) is integrated to test the one-thread loss without calling that preparation a speed gain. In another line, reconstructing the original byte order was faster in isolation, yet whole decoding of Wikidata was level. The new live-reader harness aims to measure where that saving goes; its registered later programme has 120 measured decodes and nine preliminary checks, and a workstation bundle has now been submitted, but its completeness and outcome have not yet been checked. A proposed warm-stage comparison remains separate and unadmitted.

The live harness's [failed-child resource repair](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6822234208) and accounting checks are integrated. The hosted code check had failed because two TOML manifests did not match the hosted formatter's output, as [Louis's displayed provider evidence](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6839448187) identified. Codex reproduced that result with the bundled formatter and [made a two-file format-only correction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6848026007); parsed TOML and frozen sources stayed equal. The corrected head passed its hosted code check and packaging on both macOS architectures, then [merged into master](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6848920917). The earlier cancelled macOS job remains an unexplained historical incident; its provider messages do not establish a project fault.

A [submission prerequisite](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6849382358) was then integrated. Its unchanged comparison rule and tests passed, but ordinary paired CI is not the registered workstation experiment and does not show a new speed gain. The one-core dossier remains deferred rather than executed.

## What is the new preparation?

Louis [replaced a request for manual workstation facts](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6849678574) with a deterministic, noninteractive rule in [the open preparation proposal](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/377). It selects the lowest processor currently allowed to the process and records the available processor topology. It stages three test inputs for each attempt, checks their frozen hashes, and selects the registered Rust compiler without changing the user's default. The existing live-reader measurement plan, source and safety gates remain fixed. The author reports successful local staging and 18 untimed tests; [hosted preparation](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/actions/runs/43404843493) and the other exact-head Actions checks pass, as does the legacy status. These are preparation checks, not performance measurements or a new independent safety clearance.
