The guarded service comparison found a gain on Louis's workstation, but its proposed production adoption was refused under the project's standing rule; the change remains unmerged. A different, prospective acceptance route has passed review as text but is still a draft, and the old run cannot qualify under it. The run-fed line's second workstation attempt remains valid but inconclusive, and release scaling for version 0.2.7 is the only remaining queue entry.

## What did the guarded service test show?

The [valid registered run](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750273151) showed **6.8% more throughput**, about **6.4% less wall time**, and about **14.7% less processor time** at ten threads on Louis's machine. These are the [corrected readings](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750401317) of the same speed and processor-time ratios; the intervals and verdict did not change. Peak memory was 13.7% lower, reported but not a registered condition. The run changed several parts of the plan together, so it does not isolate service threads as the cause or establish performance across a class of machines. Its no-harm bound was not shown at twelve and sixteen threads, which does not itself demonstrate harm.

## Why was adoption refused?

The [Guardian found](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750391425) that the proposed adoption rule weakened the standing requirement for a beneficial end-to-end comparison on continuous integration. The new path is not exercised by that comparison, but that does not exempt a default-decoder change. The Coordinator [accepted the ruling](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750401317): no merge or release of this change follows from the existing result. The eight-thread identity figure was a nominal benefit with equal plans; it is reported, not subtracted from the ten-thread finding.

## Is there another route?

The Coordinator drafted a [target-gated acceptance route](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/309) for future changes whose new path is absent from continuous integration. It would require a frozen target predicate, a complete off-target noninferiority test, target superiority with adjusted uncertainty across all required cells, no-harm evidence on fallback settings, a coverage argument and a frozen protocol. The Guardian [found the final text admissible](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750846374), after revisions to its evidence contract and decision record. **The route is not enacted.** It awaits authenticated re-pins of the role tasks, then a reviewed decision record and logged enactment under the amended rules. Even if enacted, it applies forward only: the existing guarded service run cannot qualify, and a new registration would be necessary. No new run is authorised by this draft.
