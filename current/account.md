Version 0.2.9 is released with a measured gain for Wikidata at higher thread counts and a small loss on one thread. The team has integrated the preparation for a new live-decoder comparison, but no workstation comparison has run. A proposed automatic way to choose an allowed processor and verify the test inputs now passes untimed checks; its integration and the final ready signal remain open.

## Why prepare this comparison?

An earlier [workstation result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6773120400) found that the released team path helps at higher thread counts. A separate [comparison's diagnostic and memory-accounting repair](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6831115741) is integrated to test the one-thread loss without calling that preparation a speed gain. In another line, reconstructing the original byte order was faster in isolation, yet whole decoding of Wikidata was level. The new live-reader harness aims to measure where that saving goes; its registered later programme has 120 measured decodes and nine preliminary checks, none of which has run. A proposed warm-stage comparison remains separate and unadmitted.

The live harness's [failed-child resource repair](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6822234208) and accounting checks are integrated. The hosted code check had failed because two TOML manifests did not match the hosted formatter's output, as [Louis's displayed provider evidence](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6839448187) identified. Codex reproduced that result with the bundled formatter and [made a two-file format-only correction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6848026007); parsed TOML and frozen sources stayed equal. The corrected head passed its hosted code check and packaging on both macOS architectures, then [merged into master](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6848920917). The earlier cancelled macOS job remains an unexplained historical incident; its provider messages do not establish a project fault.

A [submission prerequisite](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6849382358) was then integrated. Its unchanged comparison rule and tests passed, but ordinary paired CI is not the registered workstation experiment and does not show a new speed gain. The one-core dossier remains deferred rather than executed.

## What is the new preparation?

Louis [replaced a request for manual workstation facts](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6849678574) with a deterministic, noninteractive rule in [the open preparation proposal](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/377). It selects the lowest processor currently allowed to the process and records the available processor topology. It stages three test inputs for each attempt, checks their frozen hashes, and selects the registered Rust compiler without changing the user's default. The existing live-reader measurement plan, source and safety gates remain fixed. The author reports successful local staging and 18 untimed tests; [hosted preparation](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/actions/runs/43404843493) and the other exact-head Actions checks pass, as does the legacy status. These are preparation checks, not performance measurements or a new independent safety clearance.

## What happens next?

The Coordinator has given the frozen recipe a preliminary PROCEED check, but the proposal is still open. The existing owner-authorised executor retains integration and admission; no duplicate assignment is needed. Before any workstation job becomes ready, the team must deliberately integrate the exact reviewed head, verify final-source checks and the argument-free recipe, then use the standard ready signal and queue procedure once. The present [queue](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/e674070e1351460d27d745390b1db80ef5112b60/bench/workstation-queue.txt) has no entry. The owner's authorisation for preparation and queue admission already stands; the owner alone will execute the workstation queue. The missing ready signal is a transport step, not a request to supply processor or input paths again.

## What else remains uncertain?

The earlier cells implementation's specific safety objection was resolved, but its corrected head failed its pre-set default-performance condition and remains unmerged. The new harness may explain the whole-decoder gap; it has not measured it. A separate phrase-level representation failed its metadata budget, while other alternatives remain research dispositions. Louis's detailed technical companion has merged into the site source, but deployed publication was not separately verified in this record. No new decoder release, default change or workstation result follows from these preparations.
