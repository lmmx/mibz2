## What did the earlier run-fed results establish?

The [serial run-fed test](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730764444) stopped its arm on one CI worker: the full stage cost 1.159 times the current stage. Its fill saved work, but emission, sorting and fallback costs outweighed it. The [pair-table test](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6731656317) also stopped its full-path arm at 1.895 times production’s inverse-transform entry point. Its run-intersection builder saved 31% against the older *pair* builder, below its registered 50% positive target. It never beat production’s one-hop builder in the measured corpora.

The [corrected result journal](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/6b36fd8c/docs/journal/2026-11-09-pair-from-runs-result.md) withdraws an earlier claim that the pair walk alone costs about as much as production’s whole entry point. The apparent residual subtracts separate sweeps with different setup and admission, so it is not a measured walk-only cost or proof that no cheaper builder could rescue the path. The pair result still identifies a full-path loss. A separate consumer test would be needed to price the walk itself. Under Louis’s [recorded direction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/cb02fd95/docs/team/PROTOCOL.md), neither stopped arm closes the run-fed search.

## What is being tried next?

The first [one-hop direct construction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732062626) places run records into the table used by production’s existing consumer, avoiding the pair-table consumer and a sorted record array. Its table output matches production’s in the reported checks. The Researcher accepted the construction but found evidence checks that could mark impossible counts or metadata as valid. A [later review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732362367) found the first repairs closed those examples, yet four count bounds and a positive fixture still need correction. This design has no measured speed verdict; its safety review and fuzz coverage are also open.

The helper-side direct-placement experiment was frozen before a separate safety review. The Guardian [lifted its scoped safety hold](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732094558), but CI then [failed its fuzz smoke](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732110824): one leader case rebuilt a block after a refused check. The Coordinator classified that as a real project failure, not a flaky run, and has withheld timing until the cause is fixed and the source is re-pinned.
