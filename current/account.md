## What did the target check show?

The [submitted bundle](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6745181695) and [Coordinator's reading](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6745202683) agree on TARGET. On the i9, discovery found ten physical cores, twenty logical processors, the expected cache and an unlimited CPU quota. A public ten-thread request built the service plan with eight active threads, a window of eight and five prepared and finished blocks. Nine and eleven selected ordinary teams. Every decode was correct. The input was small and recorded no team pooling, so that part remains covered by CI rather than this check. The bundle was untimed, its queue entry has been removed, and the later timed study must repeat its own preflight. The Guardian's earlier technical repair clearance does not itself approve timing or adoption.

## What did the release change?

[Version 0.2.7](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6745555994) is published. Its user-facing change is the feeder guard: when the input source panics, a parallel reader now fails its next read instead of hanging. The #275 and #273 pipeline recovery changes in this release are test-only. A paired CI benchmark against 0.2.6 detected no change at its precision; that is narrower than proving equal performance. The CHANGELOG is merged and release notes were dispatched. The [downstream update](https://github.com/lmmx/wikidata-pq/pull/222) remains open: it showed no end-to-end gain under that repository's merge rule, and its direct decoder path does not exercise the feeder fix. The workstation release scaling run is still queued.
