Version 0.2.8 has shipped measured Wikidata and text decoding gains. The next production candidate carries its run-record method into the team paths used at higher thread counts, where the released change brought no workstation gain. The missing fuzz-coverage check has been added and independently cleared; the port now awaits its queued workstation comparison. In parallel, a research team has produced a faster local inverse-transform kernel, but it is still a research example, not a decoder change.

## What is being tested for the next release?

A [team-path port](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6769926198) would extend the shipped run-record front and backward table to plain teams, the pipeline and guarded service. Its local timings suggest gains, but they are indicative and CI's ordinary bench does not exercise these paths. The [Guardian cleared](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6771767708) the missing plain-team fuzz coverage after a forced-route mode and logged reach/error summary were added. A first CI bench flagged one-thread losses; the policy's permitted rerun did not reproduce the primary loss, and CI is green. The paired [workstation comparison](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6772170543) is queued under the rule written before its data. It will decide whether the port stays; no result is confirmed yet.

A separate [move-to-front alignment fix](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/358) has already merged. It keeps a hot list inside one memory page and removes a stack-placement hazard that made one build's records/team case roughly 1.4 times slower. Its paired bench was neutral, so the fix is a stability measure, not a measured speedup. The workstation comparison for the port includes this fix on both sides. A proposed inlining patch to hold the non-team path's machine code fixed is kept separate so it does not change that frozen contrast.

## What happened to the new mathematical line?

The research team's [run-induction example](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/351) has merged as research evidence. Its corrected local one-thread Wikidata measurements put the cells kernel at 0.82–0.86 times the current production inversion stage's time. It remains slower on high-run-density and adverse inputs. The example is not called by mibz2, uses unsafe code, and has not established a ten-worker or whole-decoder gain.

That result needs a correction beside it. The researcher [found](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6770249707) that the cells writer left bytes unwritten while its output check reused a buffer already containing correct bytes; an overlapping-copy call was also unsound. Both were repaired, the output buffer is now cleared before checking, and the earlier favourable timings predate the repair. Its final record reports twelve pinned local runs across machine states and discloses a disturbed thirteenth run. The old parallel schedule claim was corrected to a ceiling on possible turns; it is not an achieved speedup. Sparse-input figures from the defective writer were withdrawn until remeasured. No production qualification follows from the local kernel.

The team [published an explanation](https://lmmx.github.io/mibz2/blog/2026-11-10-rauzy-veech-reconstruction/) of the approach. After the Pathfinder's first review and Louis's feedback, the post was reworked to explain the mechanism through one concrete step and to correct the figures. A further review round was requested; no final post verdict is confirmed here. Louis asked three colleagues to support the researcher while leaving the next construction to its author.
