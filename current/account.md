Three attempts to reduce work in the team decoder now have counted instruction savings, and all three failed their registered measurement conditions. A combined diagnostic is being reviewed to measure the stacked version's total cost and confirm which paths execute. It cannot repair those failed counts or qualify the decoder for release. Separately, the guarded-service test has changed direction after its proposed statistical model was refuted.

## What happened on the team path?

The staged walk reduced counted traversal work, but [its helper balance failed](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759502195). The next port cut the team build's run-processing work from 8.94 to 6.79 instructions per decoded byte, but its registered attribution condition failed. A further build port cut that part from [6.79 to 5.40](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6760800224); its control was outside the allowed range, and it missed its predicted instruction band. A third port moved a faster move-to-front operation into the team path, with a [corrected whole-decoder reduction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6761543934) bounded between 0.563 and 0.600 instructions per byte. Its two validation conditions failed. These are useful construction signals, not accepted speed gains.

The Coordinator is drafting [one combined diagnostic](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6761543934) against the stacked ports. It can ask about integrated work and whether the intended kernels execute. It cannot separate each port's contribution or turn their earlier verdicts into passes. Independent safety reviews of the first two build ports found no material objection; the third is still under review. A separately frozen workstation comparison would still be needed before production adoption.

A [feature-gated walk instrument](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6760959766) has merged and is off by default. Its new CI job is informational. A small apparent speed improvement with the feature off is unexplained and is not claimed as an instrument benefit. The earlier team-kernel screen and native engagement diagnostic remain under revision.

## What changed in guarded service?

The Researcher [refuted the proposed within-run statistical model](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6760366316): its check can miss shared batch errors, and plausible variance patterns can produce too many false acceptances. The Coordinator [redirected the design](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6760381319) toward multiple independent complete runner dispatches, with inference across their results. That design is still being drafted and reviewed. The guarded-service registration remains unfrozen, and no new target result or adoption follows. The separate diagnostic for the workstation's fast and slow modes is also still under revision; its latest review found a missing-treatment admission gap.

## What else is open?

A valid [small-versus-large input comparison](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6759975348) showed independent workers scaling longer on the smaller input, but the old load model cannot price a full decoder. A later [correction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6760350824) withdrew the claim that a small table and parallel scanning must be one indivisible proposal. Their effects need joint pricing and separate measurement. A controlled record-width comparison is being reviewed; no new speed result exists.

The failed run-record layout rescue remains closed. A corrected diagnostic narrowed a possible placement effect, but the [Optimiser declined both proposed production layout contracts](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6761491363) on present evidence. A cheaper placement diagnostic is only a draft. The merged build-window change remains unreleased; the version 0.2.7 release-scaling bundle is still unconfirmed in the last checked queue state.
