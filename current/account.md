A registered test found that two service threads made decoding 4.5% faster at ten threads with 12.7% less processor time, but the team has not adopted them. The first measured attempt to build a decoding table from symbol runs was slower on a pinned CI worker and has stopped on that worker; the broader search remains open. Safety and evidence holds still govern the next service-core and run-fed tests, while the released pipeline's remaining safety question has narrowed to panic recovery.

## What did the run-fed test find?

After [targeted safety evidence cleared this one diagnostic](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730547798), the team ran its frozen, registered comparison on a pinned CI worker. The [valid result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730764444) found the run-fed path 15.9% slower on the Wikidata workload, with a measured ratio of 1.159 (95% interval 1.139–1.180). Its rule therefore stopped this implementation on that worker. The table fill itself was about 7% cheaper than today's build, but emitting records for literal symbols and sorting them cost more, and fallback blocks also lost time. Run-heavy synthetic blocks were faster; they do not overturn the judged Wikidata result. The [journal](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/66edecbe2b1924594e485e1f956dbee8ed9beda2/docs/journal/2026-11-09-run-fed-stage-result.md) gives the measured phases.

The [Optimiser cautioned](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730842547) that the fallback phase timer excludes the selection step, so its apparent cost cannot yet be assigned to selection. Removing the sort alone would still leave a nominal cost gap; phase medians are not a substitute for the judged whole-stage ratio. The next candidate places runs directly in the table with helpers, and the team is investigating a cheaper emitter and a more reliable per-block admission rule. Louis's [recorded direction](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/cb02fd95/docs/team/PROTOCOL.md) keeps the search open after a single arm stops.

## What can be tested next?

The helper-side [direct-placement design was revised and frozen](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730600503), but its timing is [held](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730740928). The Guardian found a reachable surplus-index boundary in its safe constructor and requires tests that reach the composed parallel path and outer worker panic recovery. Its physical write ownership was accepted for valid indices. A repair will need a new exact run pin before timing.

A separate pair-table construction is [stopped for unsafe public-state paths](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730359769). Its mathematical construction has supporting checks, but its exposed unchecked accesses and setup/walk state boundary need repair before even a diagnostic run. The [Coordinator is repairing it](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730367750); this stop does not reject the whole run-fed search.

## What about the released decoder and the service-core gain?

Version 0.2.6 was [released](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6706739698) after a passing confirmation, then a [diagnostic](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6720088760) found its default about 23–24% slower than 0.2.4 at low compression levels on the ten-core machine. Choosing the older worker plan removes the measured loss, but changes more than worker count. No replacement policy has qualified. For the existing pipeline, [new fuzz evidence merged](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730354611) and the Guardian [closed the reached-path gap](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730448704). Production safety admission remains held on a specific outer-worker panic recovery and mutation test; no new baseline invalid access was claimed.

The [service-core result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6729896575) is valid and favorable at ten threads, not a production adoption. The Guardian's [service-path safety hold](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730103867) remains. A proposed lookahead-threshold test has its own [admission hold](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730774618): four evidence-validation fixtures still report valid when they should fail. It cannot enter the workstation queue until those are fixed and the separate safety hold lifts. Louis has no new run ready to execute from these fronts. The [supporting levels](L2.md) retain their evidence and limits.
