Two decoder efforts advanced independently. A cheaper way to classify build windows passed its registered comparison and was merged; Louis still needs to decide whether that change warrants a release. A new Pathfinder role is investigating a more radical copy-based decoder, but the Researcher found a boundary error in its census, so its proposed workstation diagnostic is not ready. The guarded service default remains unmerged while a prospective acceptance rule awaits the role tasks' re-pin.

## What changed in the decoder?

The first build-window change met its benefit test but [failed its registered no-harm rule](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6752741658): a small-input slowdown repeated, so that version was closed without merging. A [corrective version](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/312) made the high-entropy path cheaper. Its registered benefit held across three comparisons, including a 1.6–3.1% processor-time reduction on a four-thread Wikidata input. Three isolated slowdown flags remained unresolved, not proved harmless. Louis [authorised a scoped merge](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6754918407) of the documentation-only descendant of the measured head; that exception applies only to this change. The next decision is whether to release it.

## What happened to the unstable run-fed measurements?

An [untimed audit](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6752540754) found that processor time rose by about 2.2 times in every thread during slow runs, including threads that never use the suspected cooperative spin wait. The host load largely came from the harness itself; oversubscription was not shown. Spinning is therefore not confirmed as the cause, and frequency, simultaneous multithreading or memory contention remain possible. A proposed smaller spin budget was [closed before a code change](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6752576037): the normal continuous-integration decoder never executes those waits. The earlier run-fed workstation comparisons remain inconclusive, with no third attempt.

## What is the Pathfinder testing?

Louis [confirmed the new role](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755056755) to look for a decoder that does less work in kind. Its first [proposal](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/313) studies when the inverse transform can copy a previously decoded stretch rather than follow another dependent link. The exact prototype reproduced output in local tests but was slower than production. The [Researcher found](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755014327) that the census sometimes counts a copy across a cell boundary, inflating its headline reach; the traffic argument also compares different cache levels. The affected counts, evidence admission and unsafe-loop review need repair before the planned diagnostic can be queued. The phrase-length histogram and the prototype's output were not refuted. No workstation slot is ready.

## What remains open?

The guarded service comparison still showed **6.8% more throughput** at ten threads on Louis's machine, but [its proposed adoption was refused](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6750401317) under the standing continuous-integration benefit rule. The [forward-only alternative](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/309) is text-admissible, not enacted. It awaits authenticated re-pins of the existing Work roles, then a reviewed decision record and logged enactment; the old guarded-service run cannot qualify under it. Separately, the [workstation queue](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/02c2bbfb/bench/workstation-queue.txt) still holds only version 0.2.7 release scaling, with no submitted bundle confirmed at the Coordinator's [last report](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6755056755). Louis has been asked what happened.
