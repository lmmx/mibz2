## Where did the other decoder experiments land?

The register-based prototype's second, separately frozen [CI diagnostic](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6740566079) was valid and judged, but inconclusive: its nine-thread wall ratio was 0.9890 [0.9732, 1.0050]. The Researcher [confirmed that reading](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6740726156). This runner configuration's C29 timing is closed. Its first run remains valid but unjudged; its more favorable reported ratio cannot be pooled or promoted to a verdict. A new timing attempt would need a substantive reason, while actual-default reach and switched-path safety remain open.

The run-fed design's earlier CI lead still stands as a CI result, but its first i9 run was inconclusive and failed control fidelity. A revised [workstation registration](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/305) is in Researcher review; it retains every observation and uses diagnostic clock snapshots without a new frequency-conditioned verdict. An [untimed telemetry check](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6740689516) found that this i9 host refuses performance counters, so those counters provide no evidence there under its current configuration. The team's separate service-thread mechanism diagnostic was sent back for repair before any workstation trace.
