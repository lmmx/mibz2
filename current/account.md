The team is preparing two follow-ups to a promising run-fed result: a ten-thread workstation comparison of the helper-side design, and a separate production prototype of its register-based decoder. The workstation recipe still needs its evidence checks and freeze resolved; the production prototype has no registered performance result yet. A new investigation also found that a large fallback penalty in earlier CI data came from the measurement harness’s layout, so it cannot be charged to run records or the selector.

## What changed in the run-fed evidence?

The [fallback investigation](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6734039197) examined why the sorted-record arm was 6–37% slower on three corpora where every block rejected run records and used the same fallback kernels. Disassembly showed the arms call the same kernel instances; instruction, allocation and simulated cache-miss counts were effectively equal. The penalty appeared in the judged pinned process but not its four contended copies. The arm-specific difference was the position of a stack buffer relative to the decoder’s frame. That makes these fallback ratios a harness-layout reading, not evidence that the record construction or selector costs that much. A 4 KiB address-alias explanation is plausible, but the [proposed crossed-layout control](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/adb16fb75cb7e25eb734aeebf4cc9e7bf28ff8c5/docs/journal/2026-11-09-run-fed-fallback-codegen.md) has not run, so the precise hardware cause remains open.

The registered results themselves are unchanged. The direct-placement arm [stopped](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6733116376) at 1.0525 times the current stage on its AMD CI worker. On that worker the sorted-record arm was faster than the current stage; its earlier [Intel run](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6730764444) was slower. The harness context, incumbent baseline and worker differed, so this is not an identified CPU-family effect. The helper-side [nine-thread CI lead](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732659255) also stands. Louis’s [recorded rule](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/blob/cb02fd95/docs/team/PROTOCOL.md) keeps the search open across individual stopped arms.

## What is next for the helper-side lead?

The [Researcher kept the CI verdict](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732761085) but required a revised workstation recipe: ten requested threads, twenty paired rounds, seven fixed arms, and separate checks for timing and worker-occupancy evidence. A later [review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6733680620) found five occupancy captures that were incorrectly accepted as interpretable when evidence was missing or invalid. The Guardian’s [subsequent review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6733983929) found an overstrong claim that all feeder work had joined before the capture was read; the Coordinator is repairing that claim. These are evidence and scope corrections before freeze, not a new speed result.

The register-based decoder, which supplied much of the nine-thread CI gain, is being built as a [separate prototype](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732803728). The Guardian [cleared a bounded CI diagnostic](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6733356125) at the reviewed source, while production default-on use still needs switched-path safety and its own registered performance evidence. A one-thread PR-benchmark slowdown at an earlier head [did not reproduce](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6733823754) after the timed counter was removed; the prototype remains unmerged and its registration is still in review.

## What else is waiting?

The [service-core result](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6729896575) remains favorable but unadopted. Its scoped safety and lookahead evidence holds have closed. The exact-head ready tag for the lookahead run is [still unset](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6732854512) because the Coordinator’s run-signal action was refused; Louis was asked to set or allow it. No workstation run has followed.

A [count-only packet census](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6733003073) paused a narrowly certified self-repeating packet arm on Wikidata. It does not rule out other ways to reuse repeated content. The [supporting levels](L2.md) preserve the evidence and open questions.
