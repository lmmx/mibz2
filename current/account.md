## What is holding the guarded service comparison?

The new [registration](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/316) uses the forward-only target-gated rule. The Guardian [accepted a sharded, higher-round off-target test as prospective design](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756184275), while requiring the complete grid, sample model, shard layout, invalid-run handling and power assumptions to be fixed before qualification data. The ordinary ten-round test has conditionally low power, not an impossible pass. The Researcher's statistical review is in progress.

A second [Guardian review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756634391) requires the new test machinery to land separately on the main branch before the candidate freezes. External actions must be pinned, and the workflow must fail when its qualification judge fails. The earlier workflow masked that verdict despite showing green. The Coordinator is [building those tooling fixes](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756642313); a short plumbing run is explicitly excluded from evidence. This review authorises design work, not a passing candidate or a workstation slot.

## Why is a machine diagnostic first?

Two earlier run-fed comparisons on Louis's workstation had unstable fast and slow timings. The Pathfinder [proposed a discriminator](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756108082) using a sustained block of fifty decodes before a fenced-core probe and in-run readings. The Coordinator [adopted that sequence](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756120169) so the guarded service target comparison can avoid repeating a control-fidelity failure. The Optimiser [narrowed the claims](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756418444): the probe changes capacity and power, endpoint readings cannot exclude events during a run, and no mode may be selected as favourable after seeing results. The recipe is still being built and reviewed; it has not produced a diagnosis.

## Where does the parallel scanner stand?

The [Researcher accepted](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756608043) the chain's exact-bit acceptance argument and the repaired input checks for the [parallel-scanner diagnostic](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/317). A sampler can still be stranded if its wrapper unwinds, and a timeout can leave a child group alive after the parent exits. Those need unconditional shutdown, bounded cleanup and fault tests. An independent [safety review](https://github.com/lmmx/mibz2/pull/145#issuecomment-6756767391) found the unsafe blocks sound for current callers and two latent hazards in unused paths, with fixes and regression tests being integrated. A safety addendum and Guardian ruling remain before re-freeze, dry run, exact-head checks and any queue entry.
