# Ideal permutation structures: measure the product before searching for its constructor

26 November 2026 — proposed diagnostic, not a performance result or a reopening of the frozen verdicts.

## What has actually been ruled out

The original [Rauzy–Veech record](../../monodromy/2026-11-10-rauzy-veech.md) represents the fixed LF permutation as a discrete interval exchange. Induction restricts it to a first-return map and assigns words to the eliminated trajectories. We cannot freely change LF or reorder the decoded bytes: a new representation must carry exactly the same labelled return paths and the marked-row rotation, including periodic and multicycle cases.

[Round 1](../rauzy-census/results/README.md) priced different orders of the same elementary moves on Open's actual auxiliary exchange. The step share is 51.13% of A6b (216 ms at one worker); the best counted cut is 16.69% (18.4 ms, 1.41% of the one-worker wall with scheduling free). Even the boundary-count floor, 1,831,217 versus 2,668,970 steps on the single stream, gives 34.7 ms (2.65%) under unchanged per-step cost. This is a conditional ceiling on step-count savings, not a lower bound on all possible representations or on accelerated compound operations. The frozen STOP and the decision not to port surface_dynamics for order selection stand.

[Round 2](../rauzy-census/results-2/README.md) went further: the zero-cost per-block choice among measured LIMITs caps savings at 2.78% with production dispatch or 1.45% with fixed forward emission; SLP copy-out and DFS on the tested grammar lost. The earlier [teamwork investigation](../../2026-11-22-neon-teamwork-paradigm.md) also tested non-walk grammar and tunnel alternatives, including bounds within a specified contraction algebra and examples whose consumer alone loses. These are valuable restrictions; they are not a proof that every first-return partition, coordinate representation, grammar or joint producer/consumer design is unprofitable.

The distinction is not just selecting a faster constructor. A different product might make wiring, walking and spelling cheaper together. On the stage-1 i9 workload those activities and construction cost 1,068 ms combined at one worker (200.3 + 216.0 + 161.4 + 490.3); this is overlapping work, not an additive end-to-end saving at eight workers. Forward-only wire/emit already shows 142–157 ms of projected one-worker savings in the VM census, with i9 validation pending; use it as a comparison so a new structure cannot take credit for a known consumer improvement.

## The experiment: separate three different costs

1. **Construct offline.** Generate legal alternatives on the same decoded run input, outside timed execution. Candidates can vary retained sets, first-return partitions, interval/block contractions or labelled grammar representations. Maintain a tractable finite exact-search family on small blocks, then bounded search on representative real blocks. Compare the shipped object, the known forward control and selected earlier alternatives. A row renumbering must preserve the conjugate map and word labels, not change the underlying permutation. A direct transcript of the decoded bytes, output-address table with no charged consumer, or a supplied answer to the benchmark is not a candidate structure. Use the output only as a correctness reference or a declared offline search oracle; never claim its use is an online algorithm.
2. **Measure the product.** Supply each preconstructed representation to a real consumer, charging every lookup, walk, spelling, output store and necessary metadata conversion/validation in the timed consumer. Give each candidate an honestly specified interface and byte-identical output against bwt::walk. Also replay baseline objects through the same interface with cold and warm matched conditions (record working set, actual copies, cache traffic); an oracle that skips construction must not secretly skip work in the consumer. Track representation bytes, retained visits, independent/dependent accesses, word lengths, grammar depth, write amplification and peak scratch as well as wall time. No arbitrary extrapolation of reduced steps into speed. Keep full-materialization time separate from free offline selection: for policies that can use the same cheap constructor, replay the selected policy with actual materialisation charged as its own arm.
3. **Price construction only after a worthwhile product exists.** Use the consumer savings to compute the maximum cost of constructing the candidate relative to the matched baseline. A more expensive consumer might still win by cheaper construction, but that is a distinct hypothesis. Where the product wins, derive the invariants that predict its quality from the available run representation; search for a cheap constructor and measure the whole path. Compare offline optima/bounds and held-out selection quality rather than reporting only an online heuristic that loses. Negative results specify which family, interface, corpus, cost model and budget they exclude. Do not infer a universal no-go from a finite unsuccessful search.

## Mathematical search

Start with the known boundary-count law, digram and Lyndon-grammar connections, and the earlier contraction-algebra and tunneling bounds. Exact enumerations, dynamic programming or SAT/SMT/CP-SAT on small instances can generate witnesses and bounds on finite candidate families; use symmetry reduction only when labelled paths and cost equivalence are proved. Surface/interval-exchange invariants, Rauzy classes, homology or substitution matrices may be useful if they predict an actual measured cost or identify admissible transformations; they are hypotheses, not substitutes for timings. Existing Sage/surface_dynamics or other solvers can be used offline if available under current authority; no library port, new cloud expenditure or production dependency is authorized. The earlier port STOP remains intact.

## Frozen diagnostic question and decision

Use a bounded first pass, on existing local compute, with a preregistered candidate family/budget and a train/held-out block split. Include dense, periodic, arbitrary/multicycle and run-heavy exactness cases; explicitly distinguish production-admitted blocks from forced controls. On held-out admitted blocks require byte-exactness and report interleaved repeated consumer timings for baseline and alternatives. For a candidate to justify a constructor investigation it must have at least 3% of the 1,306 ms reference one-worker wall (39.18 ms, using transparently measured i9 or separately labelled per-phase projected savings) in combined construction-plus-consumer budget: consumer gain plus removable baseline construction cost must exceed the threshold, and report the stricter consumer-only gain and the resulting allowable construction cost. A positive gross oracle budget alone is not enough to admit an implementation: identify a concrete product that improves a real consumer over the matched prebuilt baseline, or a separately demonstrated cheaper materialisation mechanism. Report k=8 with a matched scan-ideal control where existing resources permit; no unsupported i9 claims. If no candidate has useful downstream gains, record the scoped negative result and stop this batch, not all mathematics. Any next constructor or production build gets its own frozen, net-cost gate before new timings; no change to earlier frozen registrations.

This is an additional axis of the ideal-parts programme, not another attempt to tune LIMIT or find a cheaper induction order. The immediate goal is to decide whether the representation itself has enough exploitable value to deserve more mathematical search, in parallel with the already identified forward-emission and scanner work.
