 Homework 1 -- Queueing Terminology -- Due Sept 5 at 2 p.m. 
 For problem 2.2 : you should think of T as the response time for an arbitrary job; S as the size of that job; and Slow as the slowdown of that job. You are looking for E[Slow] = E[T/S] for this arbitrary job. Specifically, T and S refer to the same job. Clearly a job's response time, T, is not independent of its size, S.
