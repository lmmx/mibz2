## 1. Competing Mechanisms and Identifiability

### Mechanism set to distinguish (`M1–M11`)
- **`M1` Serial scanner compute** (`A2 + A3`, Huffman decode; `src/parallel.rs:1092`).
- **`M2` Admission / ready-cap / consumer feedback** (`src/parallel.rs:402–408`): three distinct sub-mechanisms: **`M2a`** slow consumer `A10` (`advance` + sink); **`M2b`** `READY_BYTES = 12 MiB` with `floor = k + 2` (`src/parallel.rs:74, 687`); **`M2c`** slot window `ahead >= 4k`.
- **`M3` Long-tail block heterogeneity & stream ordering** (`C²_S` + head-of-line blocking behind slow blocks in `ready`, plus 2.5× macro-window density variation across `w00..w11` in `bench/wikidata.json`: `16–47` blocks/window).
- **`M4` Worker stage & route split**: Open construction (`A6a/b/c`), Open emission (`A7`), non-Open inversion (`A8`), `A5` (`runs::front`), `A9` (`RLE1/CRC`). **`[PROVED]` factual correction to §3:** `four-arm-runs.tsv` records **`1,724` total blocks (`431` distinct × 4)**, of which **`932` (`233` distinct × 4, `54.1%`)** take Open and **`792` (`198` distinct × 4, `45.9%`)** take non-Open (`LazyCells`/fallback). §3's "233 distinct blocks … 932" omits 45.9% of the workload.
- **`M5` DRAM bandwidth (`R1`)** (~35–42 GB/s dual-channel DDR4).
- **`M6` L3 capacity cliff (`R2`)** (20 MiB L3 spilled by `k` workers + unread `ready` buffers; `[CONJECTURED]` jump in `B/B` between `k = 10` and `16`).
- **`M7` Memory latency / MLP ceiling (`R3`)** (pointer-chase latency `ℓ(k)` vs sustainable concurrent misses `M` in `A6c`/`A7`).
- **`M8` Core & SMT sharing (`R4`)** (`k + 3` threads on 10C/20T; onset `k = 8–9` unpinned, hard floor `k = 10`).
- **`M9` Turbo / power-limit droop (`R7`)** (i9-10900K all-core turbo ~5.2 → 4.8 GHz, TVB at 70 °C, PL2 250 W → PL1 125 W after `Tau ≈ 56 s`).
- **`M10` Lock & wakeup contention (`R6`)** (`Shared::lock()` acquired 5×/block across scanner, workers, and reader at `src/parallel.rs:928, 1075, 1101, 1155, 1189`; `Jobs::lock()` 2×/block at L208, L219).
- **`M11` Cold-heap VM / page-fault zeroing & TLB (`R5`)** (fresh-process `minflt` contending on kernel `mmap_lock` during startup vs warm-heap reuse).

## 2. Most Informative Design for ~2–3 Workstation Hours

### Budget reality check (`[PROVED]` from `bench-scan-profile` & `four-arm-runs.tsv`)
- `bench-scan-profile` budgets **~35 min for 800 fresh-process observations** (`~2.6 s/obs`, dominated by spawn/setup/settle rather than the `0.22–1.32 s` decode).
- **Current `briefs/character.md` + §7a blows the budget on the wrong design:** Stage 1 (`240` baseline + `6` overhead + `36` PMU + `120–240` public API) + Stage 2 (`12 × [4×9 + 2×32] = 1,200` obs after §7a's `2^(9−4)` at `k = 8, 10`) + `+10%` follow-ups (`~144`) + `A3+A7` (`24`) + §6 (`~450`) = **`2,220–2,340` obs (~1.6–2.4 h at `2.6 s/obs`, or 3–4 h if multi-decode aggregation is used)** — with **768 obs (over half of Stage 2)** spent on an aliased factorial at two pre-guessed anchors.

### Verdict on each design choice
1. **Stage 1 grid (`k = 1..20`, 12 randomised complete rounds): Keep 1–20; boost the noisy knee band (`k = 8..12`) to 16–20 rounds** `[PROVED]`.
   - From `four-arm-runs.tsv`, Arm A CV is `1.25%` (`k=1`), `0.80%` (`k=6`), and **`5.36%` (`k=10`)**. At `σ = 5.36%`, 12 rounds yields a 95% CI half-width of `±3.41%` — already wider than §7.5's `3%` median acceptance threshold! Raising the knee band `k ∈ {8..12}` to **20 rounds** (or all 20 counts to **16 rounds**: `320` obs ≈ `14 min`) cuts the `k = 10` half-width to `±2.51%` (`±2.86%` at 16 rounds) for only `+3.5 min`.
2. **Anchors (`{1, 6, 8, 10, 12, 20}`): Drop `k = 1` from Stage 2 pacing; select 4 Stage 2 pacing anchors from Stage 1** `[PROVED / ARGUED]`.
   - At `k = 1`, there is no contention, no SMT, and `C_scan = 0`, `C_j = D_j / D_worker` identically from Stage 1 timers (`108` pacing obs add zero information). Keep `k = 1` (and `20`) for **PMU anchors only** (`{1, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20}` — add `16` to catch the predicted `k = 10–16` L3 cliff!).
   - Freeze the **anchor-selection rule** now, fill values after Stage 1: `k_worker = max(4, k* − 3)`, `k* = min{k : median(Δ_k) > 0}`, `k_post = min(k* + 2, 12)`, and `k_hi = k_cliff` (if IMC `q(k)` jumps `> 25%` between adjacent PMU anchors, else `16`).
3. **Dose (`+20%` primary, `+10%` follow-ups) and `2^(9−4)` factorial (§7a): Keep `+20%` (or `+25%` at high-CV `k*`); CUT the `2^(9−4)` factorial and replace with a sequential screen → `3×3` knee response surface** `[PROVED / ARGUED]`.
   - **Why `2^(9−4)` Resolution IV is mathematically invalid here `[PROVED]`:**
     1. Across a `max(S, W/k)` corner, 3-factor interactions (`A3 × A6b × A7`) are $O(1)$ and alias onto main effects; every 2-factor interaction aliases onto other 2FIs (`36` 2FIs in `≤ 22` df), so `A3 × A7` cannot be separated from `A6b × A6c`.
     2. Seven of the nine factors (`A5, A6a, A6b, A6c, A7, A8, A9`) are **serial stages inside the same worker**. Pacing 4–5 of them simultaneously with memory-free loops only sums scalar worker delay while *diluting* DRAM/L3 pressure across the whole factorial.
     3. It consumes `768` obs on two pre-guessed counts (`8, 10`).
   - **Better sequential Stage 2 (~`680` obs total, ~`35–45 min`, 2.5× cheaper and unaliased):**
     - **Phase 2a (OFAT Screen, `240–288` obs):** At the 4 Stage-1-chosen anchors, pace the **`p_active ≤ 6`** active coordinates (collapse `A6a/b/c` into `A6` for pacing; drop any stage `< 3%` of its thread's time) at `+20%` (`+25%` if Stage 1 `σ_k > 4%`).
     - **Phase 2b (`3×3` Knee Response Surface, `96` obs):** At `k*`, run a full unaliased `3×3` grid on `(scanner A3, worker A6+A7)` at `δ ∈ {0, +10%, +25%}` (or `{−oracle, 0, +20%}` if the four-arm A3/A7 oracles are runtime flags in the same binary!). Directly measures knee curvature, `(A3, A7)` interaction, and one-sided asymmetry with zero confounding.
     - **Phase 2c (Targeted Discriminators, `~300` obs):** Run the Q1 separating checks (`READY_BYTES × W`, `2×2` SMT×turbo at `k = 4`, scanner core isolation at `k = 10`, `dose.rs` `stream`/`chase`, 12-window order shuffle, warm 2nd decode, THP, `memcpy` sink) + a **2-minute one-time i9 roofline calibration** (STREAM triad + L2/L3/DRAM pointer-chase latency across 1..10 cores).
   - **Total revised campaign:** Stage 1 (`320` baseline + `12` overhead + `42` PMU across 7 anchors + `100` public-API = `~474` obs, ~`25 min`) + Stage 2 (`~684` obs, ~`38 min`) + `2 min` machine cal ≈ **`1,160` obs (~`65–80 min`)**, leaving `> 1 h` inside the 2–3 h budget for Stage 1-triggered follow-ups or out-of-corpus validation.

### Stage 1 → Stage 2 frozen decision rules
- **Rule S1 (Anchors):** `k* = min{k : median(Δ_k) > 0}`; `k_worker = max(4, k* − 3)`; `k_post = min(k* + 2, 12)`; `k_hi =` high side of largest `q(k)` jump if `Δq/q > 25%` across `{1,6,8,10,12,16,20}`, else `16`.
- **Rule S2 (Coordinate pruning):** Drop any coordinate with `< 3%` of its owning thread's Stage 1 busy time (except `A10` if `ReadWait` or `SlotWait_cap > 2%`). Split `A6c` out of `A6` at `k*` only if `A6 > 20%` of worker time and its off-diagonal response differs from `A7`.
- **Rule S3 (Adaptive rounds):** From Stage 1 within-round SD `σ̂_k`, set $R_k = \operatorname{clamp}(\lceil (2.15\,\hat{\sigma}_k / (0.12\ln(1+\delta)))^2 \rceil,\, 10,\, 20)$ so every anchor achieves `≤ ±0.12` 95% half-width on `Ĉ_i`.
- **Rule S4 (Admission gate):** Run `(READY_BYTES, W)` unconditionally at `k*`; extend to `k_post`/`k_hi` iff Stage 1 `SlotWait_cap + SlotWait_window > 1%` of scanner wall time.
- **Rule S5 (Overhead abort):** If paired on/off overhead exceeds `1%` wall shift or `2%` wait-fraction shift, drop fine inner-loop marks before launching Stage 2.
