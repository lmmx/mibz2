# What mathematical operation would actually accelerate this computation?

26 November 2026 — owner-requested inverse problem, not a new encoding benchmark or a reopening of a failed gate.

## The question to the swarm

The owner asks: if we cannot verbalise what mathematical structure might help in a way that yields an actionable computation, what question *would*? What permutation compression, structure, or other operation could be found and made usable for mibz2? Do not merely enumerate literature about compact representations, or reassure us that negative results are useful. Derive a computational specification, then work backwards from it to mathematics capable of satisfying it. The output must identify an operation with an implementable constructor and a credible whole-cost advantage, or a precise, informative obstruction and a narrower next question. An abstract library is not the goal; a reusable package earns its existence through a concrete decoder benefit first (#990 revised).

## Formalise the desired operator, not the name of a discipline

Let p be raw LF or its inverse, in declared coordinates, and l(i) the symbol/word associated with one directed step. Define the labelled transition (i -> p(i), l(i)). Composition of two transitions at i is (p(p(i)), l(i) concatenated with l(p(i))); orientation and output order must be verified. More generally define J_k(i) = (p^k(i), W_k(i)) with W_k the ordered concatenation of the k step labels. For variable lengths use a certified stopping set and first-return length, preserve marked row, period, cycles and exact spelling. This is a skew product over the permutation with a word-monoid label, not an arbitrary quotient of the unlabelled graph. A true quotient requires compatibility of transitions and labels; otherwise retain sufficient state to distinguish occurrences. A power oracle returning the final index alone is not decoding.

The desirable operation constructs a compact executable representation of these labelled trajectories from the available L/RLE, rank, run, bucket, band or upstream symbol data; evaluates many original steps per cheap lookup, emits words in large reusable chunks, or eliminates an existing construction phase. It must avoid spending an equivalent serial walk to find its own shortcuts. Ask independently whether the right primitive is a power, composition, batch action, selective contraction, string grammar, sparse boundary transformation, or another object altogether. The existing return-map implementation is a comparator, not a mandatory architecture. Give exact input/output, permitted preprocessing, query schedule (one use per block), labels/rotation, cost model, and a reference algorithm.

## Search axes — hypotheses, not commitments

1. **Raw-space composition before loss of structure.** For piecewise translations on integer intervals, composition is computed by intersecting image and domain partitions and adding offsets; boundaries of p^k arise from preimages of original boundaries. For a fixed k, at most min(n,k*r) pieces for r translation pieces (with explicit boundary/rotation convention); derive and verify the precise statement, not just an asymptotic slogan. Test whether *label-homogeneous* pieces of p^2, p^4, p^8, adaptive powers or restricted compositions have a substantially smaller description than their row expansion. Labels may split intervals, growth may be linear in k*r, and power jumps may leave output position/rotation complications. Count those costs. Compare to previously tested tunnelling, move structures, doubling, interval-exchange and non-walk arms; demonstrate the new operator rather than simply renaming them.
2. **Selective multi-step execution.** Find regions where k-step labelled action is cheap to materialise without enumerating every vertex. Explore local interval or monotone compositions, short-word transition tables, fast rank/select over composed runs, sparse exceptions, simultaneous composition with output labels. Preserve a cheap fallback for irregular parts and charge dispatch. Ask what fraction of real visits can be replaced by one dependent load and one wide copy, not how many patterns are avoided.
3. **Operation-aware structural discovery.** Global permutation patterns were weak predictors of useful dynamic structure. Search for *orbit-local* properties: repeated labelled words, coherent images of interval bundles under successive powers, common subpaths, low boundary growth, sparse exceptional trajectories, reusable transducer states. Determine whether cheap observables before A6c predict the actual reduction in visits or constructor work, validate on held-out blocks, and abandon features whose analysis costs exceed their value.
4. **Other mathematical formulations.** Search primary work on compressed permutation powers and iteration, interval exchange/move structures, compressed functional graph navigation, list ranking and pointer jumping, grammar/transducer composition, BWT tunnelling and compressed text extraction, combinatorics on words, symbolic dynamics, finite monoid actions, algorithmic graph contraction, and anything more appropriate discovered by the participant. For each promising source state precisely the operation it computes, construction complexity and machine model, whether it emits the full labelled trajectory, and the discrepancy with our use-once workload. Existing research software may serve as an oracle; no wholesale ports or abstract package construction. A mathematical result about conjugacy or entropy alone is insufficient.
5. **Change the problem statement if necessary.** Is there a representation of *execution* rather than of p, or a direct transformation from BWT runs to the ordered output that does not build the full map? Can an algebraic operation replace induction or row wiring? What exact primitive, if provided by a library, would make that possible? Identify the weakest sufficient contract, show the dependency graph of what must be computed and what need not be computed, and look for known algorithms satisfying that contract. Explicitly distinguish a reusable operation from a precomputed answer or an oracle that silently walks the text.

The existing bwt-digram-return-map claim is already implementing one specific raw/bucket construction. Do not duplicate its digram/F-bucket experiments. Work here is upstream formulation, comparative operator discovery and a small orthogonal diagnostic (e.g. labelled power partition growth), if justified. Share a directly relevant result in that claim's evidence rather than routing a message to its holder. If the digram probe later rules out its candidate, preserve that verdict; it is not automatically a verdict on other operators.

## First bounded deliverable

One session, no decoder src edits, no unsafe, no workstation or additional paid compute. Before any new measurements choose at most two genuinely distinct operator candidates, state why each differs from prior failures, and freeze a small exact diagnostic and its selection criterion. Start with toy/permutation enumeration and a bounded representative subset of training blocks; keep a distinct holdout for any measured conclusion. Use existing corpus data if available, including text/runs guards. No exhaustive literature survey as an end in itself.

For each candidate supply:
- formal primitive signature, exact reference implementation or executable pseudocode, small witness and exactness test (including periodic/multicycle cases where relevant);
- what work disappears (dependent visits, literal stores, induction/wiring work), what new work is induced, and whether the output still needs full expansion;
- construction from actual available input without a decoded-text transcript, complexity with parameters n,r,m,k,boundaries/labels, and measured or bounded actual memory/work; price coordinate mappings, exceptions and label payloads;
- estimated or measured full build-plus-use cost versus shipped and matched forward controls, not merely space, O(1) access or a free oracle. Identify what is measured, projected or conjectured;
- smallest next falsifiable implementation test, sources checked and explicit overlap with previous results.

A useful result is either one candidate with a concrete exact construction mechanism and plausible >=39.18 ms whole C+E improvement on the 1,306 ms one-worker reference after paying every added operation (not a claim of PASS without actual full data), or a rigorous scoped obstruction that changes the next operation specification. At eight workers the scan may remain binding: do not sell one-worker gains as measured end-to-end eight-worker throughput. If no candidate survives, give the precise missing primitive and the strongest reason for believing it hard; do not admit a generic software task. A constructor follow-on must freeze its own net gate and reuse existing matched controls. The final test of a package is a measured benefit to mibz2; the package boundary remains independent if the mathematics earns it.

## The standard of an actionable answer

'Find an interesting permutation structure' is not enough. An example answer has the form: given runs/bands and rank, compute a certified k-step labelled interval map in X time and Y scratch; on these blocks it replaces V visits with V' and emits W reusable words; the paid construction consumes at most B of the measured consumer budget; here is a reference, an implementation path, and a test that can falsify it. If the literature addresses a different problem, say what missing operation must be invented instead. The swarm should challenge even this proposed formulation and replace it with a sharper one if that produces a more plausible usable algorithm.
