## Uncertainty and scope

No new mathematical error was established by this port. The L-versus-F byte convention,
text-versus-BWT run wording, two uses of S, and historical R1 status required explicit
clarification, recorded in the edit inventory. The main text's strong phrases about
maintenance costs and hypothetical sharing remain bounded by counted-work/model qualifiers.
The author-side checks establish source correspondence and the correctness of the displayed
toy calculations; they do not replace the Coordinator's publication decision or later
independent Researcher rounds. The blocked thesis source and unmatched timing environments
remain explicit limitations, not facts silently filled in.
