## The rule (`bench/two_arm.py`, #514's, one candidate)

- **A cell's step:** the rounds' paired wall ratios, `mibz2-occ-7-28-w12` over `mibz2-occ-7-28`: geometric mean and 95% interval (Student's t on the logs). FASTER below 1, SLOWER above, else INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Gain:** Holm's one-sided tests over **the 3 Wikidata cells** (12, 16, 20 threads), family α = 0.05. The gain is established if Holm rejects at one or more.
- **Loss screen:** none of the 6 judged cells (Wikidata and text) SLOWER.
- **Memory gate:** at every judged cell, the median over the rounds of each run's peak RSS, candidate over reference, at most 1.10.
- **The verdict:**
  - *ADOPTS:* gain, no SLOWER cell, memory gate passed.
  - *HELD ON MEMORY:* gain and no SLOWER cell, memory gate failed; the Coordinator's under PERFORMANCE-POLICY rule 4.
  - *A FINDING, NOT ADOPTED:* gain, but a cell SLOWER.
  - *NOT ADOPTED:* otherwise. 7/28 with 16-byte rules stays.
