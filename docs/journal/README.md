# The development journal

Dated entries (`YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`), one per experiment or line of inquiry, kept on
master. They are the intellectual history: what was tried, why, what came of it, and what
remains open. Results that are measurements live in the bench comments and the results
store; actionable hypotheses, with their gates, live in GitHub issues.

## What gets recorded

A finding goes into an entry, and into its issue where it has one, when it materially
changes our understanding of a performance mechanism, an experiment or a decision. Not
every suggestion, but every change of mind. Each records:

- **what we believed**, and **the evidence that changed it**;
- **provenance:** a primary source (cited exactly), our measurement (CI or local, said
  which), or our hypothesis (untested, said so);
- **the mechanism** it proposes, **its cheap falsification test**, and **its outcome**
  when there is one.

Rejected ideas stay visible: an entry for the negative, a line in its issue. An idea is
reopened only on a condition named when it was closed. Update an existing issue before
opening a new one.

Every figure says what it measures. In particular, it says whether it is in decode
order, or a repeat over retained working memory (`2026-10-27-walk-sweep.md`).
