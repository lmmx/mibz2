# 2026-11-16 · MONODROMY 18 · Can words be freed before the induction ends? 28% of word bytes on Wikidata (44% on prose) die early, but not in an order any cheap allocator reclaims; nothing can be written early. Below the threshold for a design

Questions: QUEUED 6904540455 and its addendum 6914069285. Words, meaning rules plus arena, are
15.6 of lazy letters' 29.5 B a letter (LOG 6907104251). Can parts of the grammar be expanded and
freed before the induction ends?

**Labels:** **P** proved · **C** counted (exact for its input) · **Conj** conjecture.

**Setting.**
- **The order:** the right end, which lazy letters runs.
- **What each step makes:** a word N = (A's word)(B's word). A word longer than 16 bytes is a
  rule (16 B); any other is its bytes in the arena.
- **Original letters' words** are single bytes in a fixed table and cost nothing.
- **The walk's row** is cut out as a one-row letter, as Cells cuts it, and its word is marked.

**The program:** `words.rs.txt`, here.
- *What it tracks:* every word instance, with its length, kind, children, parents, uses, when it
  was made, and when its letter stops carrying it.
- *The check:* every final word is checked to be a rotation of the block.

The 15.5 B a letter agrees with the kernel's measured 15.6 (LOG 6907104251).

**What can die early (P).** A short word whose parents are all short dies once its letter stops
carrying it and its last parent is made, because each parent copied its bytes. That is 68% of
the arena's bytes on Wikidata and 70% on prose. A rule, or a short word a rule points to, is
read by the writer and lives to the end.

**So the freeable share is 1 − 0.720 = 28% of the word bytes on Wikidata, and 44% on prose.**

## 2. Is a word final at closure, and can it be written then? Final yes; placed no

**Final (P).** A word instance belongs to one letter, and every use reads it from that letter.
- *When it stops changing:* once the letter's word is replaced (the letter loses a step) or the
  letter closes (it is A in an equal step), the instance gets no more uses.
- *Rules are mostly single-use:* 96% are used exactly once, so their one parent is their only
  reference.

**Not placed (P).** A word's place in the output is fixed only relative to the walk's row.
- *The marked word is the exception:* it occurs exactly once, around that row, and its place
  is known the moment it is made. Its unmarked factor is then placed too, and a single-use
  subtree under it could be written and freed at once.
- *Every other occurrence is placed only when an ancestor joins the marked word.*

**Counted (C).** In the right-end order, steps that make a marked word are 0.000 to 0.001 of all
steps. The walk's row is a one-row letter, and the fronts reach it last. Progressive writing
from the marked word was simulated:
- every marked word written when made;
- every other word when its first parent is written;
- a word kept until written;
- 4 B of position kept for a word with later occurrences.

Its peak live word state is 0.705 of the word bytes on Wikidata (0.628 to 0.744), reached at
99.8% of the steps. On prose it is 0.590, worse than not writing early at all (0.563), because
of the positions it keeps. **Writing at closure frees nothing.**

## 3. Reclaiming the dead arena: not free

**The dead word is known on the spot (P).** When a step makes a short parent, the loser's old
word, and in an equal step A's word too, is dead at once unless a rule points to it. No
look-ahead is needed.

**But not where a cheap allocator could reclaim it (C).** Of the dead short words, the share on
top of the arena when they die is 0.0001 (median), and within 4 of the top 0.0006. A stack
arena, popping dead words before pushing the parent, reclaims **0.000** of the arena on every
block.
- *Why:* the loser's old word was made long before. Its letter carried it until now, while
  other words were pushed above it.
- *What it would take:* a free list or a compaction pass. Both are per-letter bookkeeping, which
  the addendum rules out (lazy's overhead is already its instructions, +65.5 a letter).

## 4. The lower bound

**The bound (P, for any order in which positions come from the walk's row alone, as Cells' do).**
- *Why something must be held:* an occurrence can be written only once it lies inside the marked
  word. Until then a stored form of it, or of an ancestor that contains it, must exist.
- *What it is:* so the word state at any moment is at least what is needed to expand every
  occurrence not yet inside the marked word.
- *For the right end:* the marked word grows only at the end, so this is the whole writer's
  grammar: 0.720 of the word bytes on Wikidata, 0.563 on prose. That is exactly the measured
  peak. **The right end holds no more words than any such order would at its end.**

**What this bound does not cover (Conj).** It does not hold for a decoder that learns positions
another way:
- by walking (the stop-early-and-walk route, closed as priced, §6.4 of the Rauzy–Veech entry);
- from LF itself.

**Against the information.** It is far from the information content: bzip2's own compressed size
is about 1 B a letter on these blocks. The bound is a property of the induction's way of
placing words, not of the data.

## Verdict, against the addendum's threshold

| | Wikidata | prose |
|---|--:|--:|
| word bytes that could ever be freed early | 28% | 44% |
| freed by writing at closure | about 0 | about 0 |
| freed by a stack arena (no bookkeeping) | 0 | 0 |

The threshold for a design was about a third of the word bytes with no extra instructions a
letter.
- *On Wikidata:* the possible share (28%) is below it.
- *On either corpus:* the share reachable without bookkeeping is 0.
- **No design follows. The word side's remaining state is the writer's grammar, and its size is
  set by when positions become known.**

**Open (Conj), for anyone pursuing it.**
- *Changing when positions become known* is the only structural lever left: an order that grows
  the marked word early. Growing it is walking the cycle at the level of letters, which §6.4
  priced for time.
- *Pricing it for memory* means walking an induced exchange early enough that its words can be
  written as the walk passes them. That is a different construction from Cells.
