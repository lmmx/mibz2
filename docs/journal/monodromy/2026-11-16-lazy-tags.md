# 2026-11-16 · MONODROMY 24 · Lazy tags, judged by word bytes: streak words are half the word store, but tags can remove at most 3.3 B a letter (21%), before a tag field's cost; below the single stop's 5.0 B, with n-ary nodes in the writer. Closed as priced

Programme item named in record 22 (and the Coordinator's follow-up): the writer for words
shared within winners' streaks, the "lazy tags" of §6.4 of the Rauzy–Veech entry, judged by word
bytes saved. Word bytes are the lever since 0.5.1 (LOG 6916535874).

**Labels:** **P** proved · **C** counted (exact for its input) · **M** modelled.

## 0. Result

- **Census (C, 233 Wikidata blocks).** Words made in streak steps hold **49.9% of the word bytes**,
  7.54 B a letter.
  - *Rules (long):* 33.9%, almost all used at most once: 32.3%, 4.88 B a letter.
  - *Arena (short):* 16.0%. Of that, the winner's bytes copied into the new words are 6.1%,
    0.92 B a letter.
  - *Prose:* streak words are 27.8%, mostly short.
- **What sharing can remove (P, then M).** A streak's k new words are w_A · w_{B_i}. A rule
  already shares w_A by pointer, so the only bytes a tag can remove are:
  - **the losers' own rules,** if each is folded into its one later use. A parent of three
    factors is 24 B, against 16 + 16, so a fold saves 8 B: at most 4.88 / 2 = **2.44 B a
    letter**;
  - **the winner's bytes copied into short words:** at most **0.92 B a letter**, and only if
    those words give up being contiguous bytes, which the writer reads in 16-byte stores.
  - *Together:* at most **3.3 B a letter, 21% of the word store**.
- **What a tag costs (M).** The loser's pending prefix must live with the letter until it next
  takes part, as one more field in its slot.
  - *In the top slots:* about 4 B a live letter.
  - *In the bottom slots, in the dead output buffer:* free in working set while 12 B a slot
    fits in n bytes, that is r/n ≤ 0.083. Wikidata's r/n is about 0.063.
  - *Beyond the field:* nodes become variable-length, so the writer, the first-place pass and
    the copy pass all read n-ary nodes.
- **Verdict.** The ceiling (3.3 B) is below the single stop's 5.0 B (record 21), which is built
  and through Gate 1 at +12 a letter.
  - *Tags would cost in instructions and code,* and nothing here prices them in instructions.
  - *Closed as priced on bytes.*

## 1. Which bytes a tag can touch (P)

**A streak.** Take a top-wins streak of A over the bottom order's last k letters B₁ … B_k
(record 20, §3). It makes k words N_i = w_A · w_{B_i}.

**Long N_i.** The rule is (|w_A|, ptr w_A, |w_{B_i}|, ptr w_{B_i}), 16 B, and w_A is already
shared by pointer.
- *Removing a rule:* that means not making it. The loser carries a pending tag (w_A), and the
  pair is made only when the loser next takes part, inside that step's own new word, as a third
  factor.
- *The saving:* 16 + 16 becomes 24, so 8 B a fold.
- *When a fold is impossible:* if the loser next wins, its word is a factor of several new
  words, and N_i must be made as it is. So only rules used at most once can fold: 32.3% of the
  word bytes.

**Short N_i.** Its bytes are w_A's bytes then w_{B_i}'s, copied into the arena.
- *Sharing w_A's bytes:* N_i would become a reference plus a suffix, no longer one contiguous
  run that the writer copies in one 16-byte store.
- *The bound:* the copied winner bytes, 0.92 B a letter.

**The ceiling is the sum,** 2.44 + 0.92 = 3.36 B a letter. ∎

## 3. What this leaves

**The structure is real but already mostly shared.** Streaks are the induction's dominant
pattern (record 20). The pointer that a rule already holds to the winner's word is the sharing.
What remains is a constant-factor fold of single-use rules, at most 8 B in 16, and it is not
streak-specific.

**General folding is the same idea.** A 16-byte rule used once could be folded into its parent
anywhere, not only after a streak.
- *What it needs:* knowing at the rule's making that it will be used once. A per-letter bit, "has
  won since its word was set", says so at the next use, but by then the rule has been made.
- *The allocation question:* the bump arena cannot unmake it, so folding saves bytes only if
  making is deferred.

That is the same tag field, so the same price applies.
