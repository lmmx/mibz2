# 2026-11-15 · MONODROMY 6 · The width of an induction path: right-end Rauzy–Veech holds about r/3 non-trivial letters at its busiest

Prompt: Louis's thought experiment, relayed verbatim on #145 as 6900062843 and adopted as the
programme by QUEUED 6900246233. This entry commits the two censuses behind its 0.31 r figure,
so that the figure can be reproduced.

**Labels:** **P** proved · **C** counted (exact for its input) · **Conj** conjecture.

## Definitions

An induction path shrinks the domain of LF from [0, n) to one point. The right end shrinks it
as [0, m). Its two quantities are:
- **S, the steps** (the work): counted in `2026-11-10-rauzy-veech.md` §5.6;
- **W, the width** (the live state): the most letters, at any m, of π_m (LF's first return to
  [0, m)) whose return word is longer than one byte.

Original letters, those of length one, are derivable from the run records (R1–R3 of
`2026-11-15-elision-bounds.md`). The non-trivial ones are what any induction must hold.

## The boundary lemma (P, from §5.5's proof in the Rauzy–Veech entry)

The top boundaries of π_m are the first points below m on the backward orbits from the run
starts. So π_m's non-trivial letters are one per element, counted once, of the union of:
- the run starts t < m with LF t ≥ m;
- the excursions of LF into [m, n) that pass through a run start.

**Exact non-trivial letters** (`crossing2.rs.txt`): every 8th of the 233 blocks (29 blocks),

- **W (right end):** 0.287 / 0.311 / 0.325 r (min, median, max).
- **Where it peaks:** at m = 0.28 to 0.34 n.

Every line, per block, is in `output.txt`.

A cut grid of 1/32 can miss the true peak between cuts. These W values are therefore lower
bounds on the right end's width, not its exact value.

## What it says

**Right-end Cells over-allocates (C).** Cells holds r slots for the whole induction, and its
live non-trivial state never exceeds about r/3.

**Is the width a property of the block? (Conj, open).** It may depend on the order of steps, as
S does (1.279 r to 2.137 r).
- **The next count:** W for the left end, two-sided (meet in the middle), free steps first and
  cylinder first.
- **The falsifier:** every order at 0.28 r or above. Then width doesn't depend on order, and the
  conjecture that work and width trade off along a front of best orders is dropped.
- **The lazy-letters hand-off** (materialise a letter on its first non-trivial cell) waits on
  this count (QUEUED 6900246233).

at master `06aa585` with the same output as `output.txt`.
