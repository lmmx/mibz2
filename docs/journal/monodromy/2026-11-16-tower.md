# 2026-11-16 · MONODROMY 22 · Stop-and-walk recursed: a tower of stopped exchanges writes the block (byte-exact at every depth), but each level's snapshot costs about what its words would. One extra level saves at most 1.3 B a letter beyond the single stop; no words at all costs more than words. The single stop at 1.5 r stays the design

Question: the Coordinator's, relayed after HANDOFF 6916839849. Does the budget-stop exchange admit
a second stop-and-walk on its own tail, or do the 0.3 to 1.2 points a letter at the stop bound what
any further stop can save?

**Labels:** **P** proved · **C** counted (exact for its input) · **M** modelled.

## 0. Result

- **The tail after a stop is never run, so a recursion must replace what is run.**
  - *What it does:* words are made only up to an earlier stop β₀ r. The induction goes on with
    lengths only, snapshotting the exchange at β₁ r < β₂ r < …. The top is walked, and each
    level's first occurrences are written by walking the level below.
  - *Correct:* it is byte-exact at every depth tried, from two levels to eight, and down to no
    words at all (Lemma, §1).
- **It does not pay (C, every 8th Wikidata block; words over all 233 blocks):**

  | tower (β of each level) | word bytes made | words saved, B a letter | snapshots, B a letter | emitter, instructions a letter |
  |---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
  | single stop: 1.5 | 0.669 | 5.0 | 1.5 | 36 |
  | 1.2 (words), 1.5 | 0.401 | 9.1 | 4.3 | 59 (`emit2`) |
  | 1.0 (words), 1.25, 1.5 | 0.264 | 11.0 | 8.3 | 158 (`emit_n`) |
  | no words: 0, 1.0, 1.5 | 0 | 15.0 | 20.5 | 286 (`emit_n`) |
  | no words: 0, 0.4, 0.7, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5 | 0 | 15.0 | 40.2 | 384 (`emit_n`) |

  - *Snapshot cost:* a bitmap of m bits, its rank directory, and 12 B a live letter (offset back,
    word length, first place). The bottom level of originals is counted too. In the kernel it
    is partly the streams.
  - *The best case, 1.2 then 1.5:* 1.3 B a letter beyond the single stop (9.1 − 5.0 −
    2.8), for about +16 to +26 instructions a letter (M: +22.5 in the emitter; −6 for 0.3 r cells
    made with lengths only, at about 20 a cell; up to +10 to read the extra level).
  - *Every deeper tower is worse,* in memory and in instructions.
- **The answer to the question (P, with the C above).** What bounds a further stop is not the
  0.3 to 1.2 points a letter at the top. It is the price of describing a level: about 12 B a live
  letter plus m/8 bytes, against the word bytes the induction would make between that level and
  the next. A level is worth adding only where the words it saves exceed its snapshot. On
  Wikidata, from 1.2 r to 1.5 r, they barely do: 4.1 B against 2.8. Below 1.2 they do not.
- **So words are already the compact form.** A long word's rule (16 B) or a short word's bytes
  describe what an exchange letter would describe in 12 B plus a bitmap, and a letter needs a
  first-occurrence walk where a rule needs none.
  - *The recursion is closed as priced.*
  - *The single stop at β = 1.5 (HANDOFF 6916839849) stays the design.*

## 1. The tower (P)

**Lemma (towers).** Let D_k ⊂ … ⊂ D_1 ⊂ D_0 be the domains of stops along one induction:
D_j = [0, m_j), with T_j the induced map on D_j and w^j its words. Then:
- *(i)* T_{j+1} is the first return of T_j to D_{j+1}, and w^{j+1}_β for x ∈ D_{j+1} is the
  concatenation of w^j along T_j's excursion from x back into D_{j+1}.
- *(ii)* Walking T_k⁻¹ upward from a point of known place writes the block if each
  level-j point's word is either copied from its letter's first place, or, at the letter's first
  point, written by walking T_{j−1}⁻¹ upward from the same point until it lands in D_j. At level
  0 the words are expanded from the grammar.

*Proof.*
- *(i)* is the definition of the first-return map, and invariant I composes along the
  excursion.
- *(ii)* The walk writes in increasing places, so every copy's source is written before it is
  read. A copy is safe as in record 21: occurrences are disjoint, the earlier one lies wholly
  below, and an over-read lands only past the word.
- By (i), the level-(j−1) walk from the point y writes exactly w^j at place(y), and ends at
  T_j⁻¹(y), the next level-j point. ∎

**Memory of a tower (P, an identity of accounting).** Its peak is words(β₀) + Σ_j snapshot_j +
the induction's own state.
- *Against the single stop at β_k:* level j earns its place only if words(β_{j+1}) −
  words(β_j) > snapshot_j.

## 2. Counts (C)

**Programs and checks.**
- *Programs:* `emit2`, two levels, and `emit_n`, any number of levels, in `stopwalk.rs.txt`,
  here.
- *Byte-exact:* `emit2` at (1.0, 1.5), (1.2, 1.5), (1.3, 1.5) and (1.2, 1.6), on all 233
  blocks; `emit_n` at every tower in §0, on every 8th block. Each is checked against the block's
  text, and the single-level emitter likewise.

**Two levels, all 233 blocks for the words; every 8th block for instructions** (callgrind,
hardware popcount):

| β₀ (words), β₁ | words made | saved, B a letter | `emit2` | `emit1` at β₁ | level-2 points / firsts | level-1 points walked / expansions (a letter) |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1.0, 1.5 | 0.266 | 11.1 | 71.4 | 36.3 | 0.64 / 0.118 | 0.93 / 0.32 |
| 1.2, 1.5 | 0.401 | 9.1 | 58.8 | 36.3 | 0.64 / 0.118 | 0.61 / 0.19 |
| 1.3, 1.5 | 0.485 | 7.8 | 51.9 | 36.3 | 0.64 / 0.118 | 0.41 / 0.14 |
| 1.2, 1.6 | 0.401 | 9.1 | 61.7 | 31.7 | 0.32 / 0.091 | 1.04 / 0.19 |
| ≈0 (no words), 1.5 | 0 | 15.0 | 193.3 | | 0.64 / 0.118 | 3.81 / 1.00 |

**Live letters a letter at each level:** 0.191 at 1.2 r, 0.118 at 1.5 r. The domain there: 0.195 n
and 0.047 n. So level 1.2's snapshot is 2.8 B a letter, and the top's 1.5.

**`emit_n` is a general frame-stack version.** It runs 2 to 3 times `emit2` on the same
two-level tower (127 against 59). Its rows in §0 rank the towers against each other, not against
the kernel.
