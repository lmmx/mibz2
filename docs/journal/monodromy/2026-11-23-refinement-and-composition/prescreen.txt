Pre-screen, 23 November 2026. NOT the i9-10900K: this session's container,
Intel Xeon @ 2.30GHz (VM), L2 2 MiB/core, gcc -O2 -march=native, best of 15 repetitions.
Synthetic uniform random m-cycle (Law 3 stand-in for the retained core), 8-byte entries {next, word id}.
"chase T": two chains, half the cycle each, one word id written per row.
"gather T^2 + chase T^2": one pass N2[i] = T[T[i].next].next (4-byte array), then two chains on N2;
the two word ids of each step are read from T at s and T(s), off the critical path.
