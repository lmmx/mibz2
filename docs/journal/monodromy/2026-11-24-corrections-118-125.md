## 1. Record 118: "exactly 1/(j+2)" is withdrawn for j ≥ 2

The reduction stands: the step fails iff S^(j+1)u is the least of u, …, S^(j+1)u. The step from there to probability 1/(j+2) assumed the j+2 orbit points are exchangeable. They are not.

**Re-measured,** n = 150,000, ties in text order:

| σ | j = 0 | j = 1 | j = 2 | j = 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.501 | 0.334 | 0.239 | 0.187 |
| 4 | 0.500 | 0.333 | 0.247 | 0.196 |
| 16 | 0.500 | 0.334 | 0.250 | 0.199 |

**Corrected statement.** ε(τ_0) = 1/2 exactly (proved). ε(τ_1) = 1/3 at every σ tried (measured, not proved). ε(τ_j) → 1/(j+2) as σ grows (argued; measured at 16), and lies below it at small σ.

**Withdrawn:** the conjecture that 1/(k+2) is optimal on 𝓜_k. At σ = 2, τ_2 itself goes under 1/4.

**Unaffected:** Theorem I; the break of record 116 §3.1, which the small-σ figures strengthen.

## 6. Why the model overstates row-order failures (argued)

Real retained rows are not independent digits. A templated block has ascending structure the model lacks: after Open, a retained row's successor tends to sit earlier in row order more often than half the time. So Theorems O and I bound the model, and the measured 0.365 is a reminder that their constants do not transfer to real blocks. The transfer is the open step, and M-25 and M-31 are its measurements.
