## Final revision after the second reading

The three readers converged on two clarifications: recovery/concurrency status and the human authority channel. The final prose explicitly says the recorded exchanges demonstrate delivery, while interruption and overlapping-execution guarantees need their own validation. It names the Coordinator's session, charter-amendment provenance and the route for a restrictive review to hold dependent work. No third full reader trial is claimed.
