# Text knee on the i9: CONFIRMED, k = 5 for text vs 9 for wikidata

One line: on the physical i9-10900K, the shipped decoder's wall knee is 5 for a pinned text panel's median corpus and 9 for wikidata x4, so for typical text k = 8 is past the knee and extra workers cost wall.

- **Claim / rule:** `text-knee-i9`, stop_test frozen in the claim and `docs/journal/2026-11-27-text-knee-i9/registration.md` (byte-identical copy in the bundle).
- **Recipe / run:** `just bench-text-knee` at af7ff482 (#1139), rustc 1.99.1, default codegen, unpinned, 9 interleaved shuffled rounds x 4 corpora x k = 4..10 (252/252 rows exact against libbzip2), `station_balance` 3 rounds pinned CPU 0.
- **Evidence:** `origin/evidence` 5e3f28cc, `text-knee/run-20261127T172728Z.tar.gz`, sha256 `786084e29990fa4bf9791e2b3e5f737a8b87ba5c1e4ac7566e5ee8b0d86ab7d4` (checked against the archived `.sha256`).

## What it changes

- The VM-derived prediction (text k* ~4.6-6.8 on the i9) was conservative. The i9's own 1T ratio is r = 0.36-0.44, not 0.58-0.85, and the measured knee is 4-5 for two of the three corpora.
- Past the knee, text median walls rise (`src_rs` +26%, `repo_json` +19% at k = 8). Within a mode they do not: fast-mode text walls are flat across k = 4..10. The rise is the mode mix. repo_json and src_rs ran in the slow mode on 27/27 runs at k = 8..10, against 1/27 at k = 4..6. Whether high k *causes* the slow mode is `i9-clock-modes`' question.
- For text, scanner levers (A2/A3 builds, seam window, slack-only hybrid) set the k = 8 wall, and worker levers are masked. Pricing at k = 8 has to name its corpus. DECODER.md has the row.

## Caveat that matters: bimodal walls (`i9-clock-modes` mode (b))

Per-cell samples split into two modes (~190-200 ms and ~260-300 ms on text; ~190 ms and ~290-300 ms on wikidata x4 at k = 8-9). Every thread's CPU scales together in the slow mode: wikidata k = 8 scan 0.16 -> 0.245 s and workers 1.37 -> 2.05 s, scan/worker ratio ~0.93-0.96 in both modes. So this is not scanner placement. It is the unpinned 6-10T machine-wide mode (b) that `i9-clock-modes` (#1201) is probing. (I first read it as scanner-specific; a sibling's regrouping in the sixteenth synthesis pass, #1209, corrected that, and I re-checked it on the bundle.) The mode does not follow round order.

The verdict holds within each mode. Fast: text is flat from k = 4, while wikidata falls until k = 9. Slow: text knees at 5-7 and wikidata at 9. docs_md's registered knee of 8 is a mode-mix artefact. The frozen rule used medians of 9 as registered, and CONFIRMED stands. Absolute walls and any cross-k gain on this machine need `i9-clock-modes`' mode-robust estimator.
