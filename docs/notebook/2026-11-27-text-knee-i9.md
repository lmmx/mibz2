# Text knee on the i9: CONFIRMED, k = 5 for text vs 9 for wikidata

One line: on the physical i9-10900K, the shipped decoder's wall knee is 5 for a pinned text panel's median corpus and 9 for wikidata x4, so for typical text k = 8 is past the knee and extra workers cost wall.

- **Claim / rule:** `text-knee-i9`, stop_test frozen in the claim and `docs/journal/2026-11-27-text-knee-i9/registration.md` (byte-identical copy in the bundle).
- **Recipe / run:** `just bench-text-knee` at af7ff482 (#1139), rustc 1.99.1, default codegen, unpinned, 9 interleaved shuffled rounds x 4 corpora x k = 4..10 (252/252 rows exact against libbzip2), `station_balance` 3 rounds pinned CPU 0.
- **Evidence:** `origin/evidence` 5e3f28cc, `text-knee/run-20261127T172728Z.tar.gz`, sha256 `786084e29990fa4bf9791e2b3e5f737a8b87ba5c1e4ac7566e5ee8b0d86ab7d4` (checked against the archived `.sha256`).

## What it changes

- The VM-derived prediction (text k* ~4.6-6.8 on the i9) was conservative. The i9's own 1T ratio is r = 0.36-0.44, not 0.58-0.85, and the measured knee is 4-5 for two of the three corpora.
- Past the knee, text gets *slower*: `src_rs` +26% and `repo_json` +19% at k = 8 against their best. More workers are not neutral on scanner-bound input. The likely mechanism is contention on the scanner's core (below), not idle workers.
- For text, scanner levers (A2/A3 builds, seam window, slack-only hybrid) set the k = 8 wall, and worker levers are masked. Pricing at k = 8 has to name its corpus. DECODER.md has the row.

## Caveat that matters: bimodal walls

Per-cell samples split into two modes (~190-200 ms and ~260-300 ms). Example: docs_md k = 5 is `191..193` x4 and `299..307` x5, while k = 8 is `198..204` x5 and `259..270` x4. docs_md's knee of 8 is a mode-mix artefact: its fast mode is ~192 ms at every k. The mode does not follow round order (fast fraction 0.33-0.62 per round). In the slow mode the scan thread's own CPU time is 35-60% higher (docs_md k = 5: 0.17 -> 0.27 s; k = 8: 0.17 -> 0.23 s) with zero run-queue wait. That suggests the scanner shares its physical core with a busy worker (SMT sibling) or runs at a lower clock (powersave governor, idle at 800 MHz before the run). Either way, on scanner-bound text the scanner's placement decides the wall by up to ~1.5x. Opened `scanner-slow-mode-i9` (idea) to discriminate the two.

The frozen rule used medians of 9, as registered, and the verdict stands. Wikidata's knee of 9 is consistent with its own curve (300.7 -> 269.6 ms from 8 to 9).
