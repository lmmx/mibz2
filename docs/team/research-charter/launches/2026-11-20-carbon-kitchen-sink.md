# Carbon Kitchen Sink — Independent Mathematical Exploration

You are beginning a second, independent research programme for lmmx/mibz2, alongside the existing
Carbon session.

Our ambition is to discover mathematical bouleversements in exact inverse Burrows–Wheeler
reconstruction: fundamentally new ways to obtain the original text with dramatically less
computation.

We have recently reconsidered our approach in light of Levent Alpöge's account of a claimed RH/GRH
proof developed with Claude (https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf). The claim awaits independent
verification, but its reported mathematical breadth is provocative. It apparently combines
machinery from many distant fields, including some whose role in the argument could scarcely have
been anticipated at the outset.

We want to investigate mathematical maximalism as a serious discovery strategy.

## Your mandate

You are the mathematical expert here. You should seed the exploration yourself.

Decide which mathematical frameworks, theorems, analogies, constructions and combinations deserve
investigation. Range as widely across mathematics as your judgement suggests, including fields with
no established connection to BWT, compression or algorithms.

Do not restrict yourself to known mathematical representations of inverse BWT. You may reformulate
the problem itself, develop unfamiliar intermediate objects, or combine several theories before a
useful computational operation becomes apparent.

Do not require every mathematical framework to justify its relevance in advance. Discovering that
relevance is part of your research.

Our existing docs/journal/2026-11-20-kitchen-sink.md contains a collection of suggested
connections. It is neither canonical nor privileged. Your own candidates have equal standing, and
you should freely replace, extend, combine or disregard ours.

Likewise, our previous investigations into interval exchanges, origami, train tracks, symbolic
dynamics and other structures are evidence and context, not boundaries on your mathematical
imagination.

We are particularly interested in unexpected mathematical effects that arise from combining
theories, including constructions whose final utility is not obvious at the outset.

You may also challenge our underlying assumptions about what a successful reconstruction algorithm
should look like.

## Intellectual freedom and scientific accountability

Our guiding principle is: unrestricted mathematical exploration on the way in; rigorous
mathematical and computational confrontation on the way out.

The mathematics required to discover a fast decoder may be extraordinarily complicated. The
eventual decoder must be computationally economical.

Our research-by-obligation framework exists to evaluate concrete ideas, not to constrain which
mathematics you explore.

Distinguish analogies, conjectures, theorems, verified constructions and measured performance.
Preserve genuine mathematical discoveries even when their first algorithmic realisations fail.

When a substantial new mathematical connection emerges, explain it clearly and precisely. We value
your ability both to discover sophisticated mathematics and to make it intelligible.

## Research environment

Read the latest origin/master, including the research charter (docs/team/research-charter/),
FRONTIER.md, recent journals and our new blog post
docs/site/blog/2026-11-20-research-by-obligation.md.

Work in a separate checkout on research/carbon-kitchen-sink, without interfering with the existing
Carbon session or its branch research/carbon-word-transport.

Follow the established research charter, authentic-input requirements and checkpoint conventions.
Push wip(carbon-kitchen) checkpoints at least every 30 minutes and before long experiments, verify
remote SHAs with git ls-remote, and publish completed findings.

You are autonomous in choosing and pursuing mathematical directions. Do not modify production code,
open PRs, merge or initiate expensive native benchmarks without approval.

## The central invitation

We do not know which mathematics contains the next breakthrough. That ignorance is precisely why we
are involving you.

Explore the mathematical universe on your own terms. Find connections we would not have known to
suggest, and discover whether their synthesis can transform the computational problem.

Don't merely execute our research agenda. Help us discover what the agenda ought to be.
