## 1. Governance Rules & Schema (Addendum Rules 1–5)

1. **Rule 1 — Classical Atlas Record Format:** Every entry records:
   - **ID:** Stable canonical identifier: `CA-XX` for **genuine load-bearing pre-1950 classical** entries; `MB-XX` for **load-bearing post-1950 mathematical bridges**; `CS-XX` for **load-bearing modern CS systems primitives & scoped empirical boundaries** (kept strictly distinct from the classical share). *No `analogy`, `relabel`, or `decorative` tag may ever receive a `CA-XX` ID; all are named explicitly in §6.*
   - **Classical Object & Primary Source:** Author, publication year, and the **original treatise/memoir itself** (never a modern survey).
   - **BWT/LF Translation:** Exact mathematical correspondence into $(L, F, \mathrm{LF}, \mathrm{FL}, \pi_J, G_{\mathrm{ov}}, \text{strips/bands})$.
   - **Links (`Uses` / `Used by`):** Directed acyclic graph (DAG) edges where `Y` mathematically or algorithmically depends on `X` (strictly reciprocal, verified acyclic).
   - **Weight:** Ruthless Weiss RH-anatomy classification (`load-bearing` / `analogy` / `relabel` / `decorative`). Only `load-bearing` counts toward the classical share.
   - **Status & Reason:** `theorem` / `construction` / `killed` (with quantitative kill reason and reusable translation).
   - **Dependents & Evidence:** Downstream Atlas IDs, `FRONTIER.md` / kernel artifacts, and exact verified file:line citations.
2. **Rule 2 — Depth Quota, Not Era Quota:** Every cycle extends at least one existing Atlas chain by $\ge 1$ concrete load-bearing link and reports **Longest Chain** (nodes / links) and **Option Value** (entries cited by later entries).
3. **Rule 3 — Primary Sources First:** Enter the 9 foundational classical primary-source bodies directly from their original treatments (tracked in §7).
4. **Rule 4 — Weight, Not Tags:** Strip every `relabel`, `analogy`, and `decorative` citation into §6 so the classical share reflects genuine load-bearing mathematics only.
5. **Rule 5 — Preserve Killed Chains:** Retain every killed chain with its exact quantitative kill threshold and reusable BWT/LF translation as a map of where not to go.
