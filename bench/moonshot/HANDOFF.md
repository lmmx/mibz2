# Moonshot scorer: handoff

The state of `bench/moonshot-scorer` when this work moved to a dedicated session. The design is
in [README.md](README.md). PR #606 is open; it was opened before the change of plan, and its
body's "Generated by" footer has been removed. Don't merge it: only the Coordinator merges.

## Done

- **The candidate interface.** One Rust file defines `Candidate: mibz2::bwt::InverseBwt`.
  `build.rs` compiles it as `crate::candidate`, the file named by `MOONSHOT_CANDIDATE`. The
  template and two worked examples are in `candidates/`: the null candidate (LazyCells), and
  opend compiled from the open-return journal.
- **One change to `src/`:** `Runs::records` is public under the new research feature
  `moonshot` (Cargo.toml). It is `pub(crate)` without it, as shipped.
- **The correctness gate** (`moonshot check`, `src/check.rs`). Byte-exact against libbzip2, with
  every block CRC and stream CRC rechecked. Its inputs: samples, corpora, multi-stream inputs,
  crafted periodic and multiple-cycle blocks, Cells' witnesses, Wikidata, and mutations that
  must match 0.5.6's result.
  - *Passed by:* lazy, opend, open and cells (the quick set); opend on the full set with
    Wikidata (338 cases, 34 s).
  - *Shown to fail:* a deliberately wrong kernel fails it (a unit test).
- **Container timing** (`moonshot run`; `moonshot.py`'s `timing`). Paired and rotated rounds at
  1 to the CPUs, a settle of 0 or at least 2 s, a 95% t interval on the log ratios, peak RSS.
- **The proxy** (`chaingrind/chaingrind.c`, a Valgrind tool, built by `chaingrind/build.sh`).
  - *The models:* an L1/L2/LLC cache model with an L2 streamer and in-flight fills; a dataflow
    timing model (a 224-entry ROB, 4 wide, 12 fill buffers, a 56-entry store buffer, store
    forwarding).
  - *What it reports:* transfers, dependent DRAM, past-L2 and any-level links, overlap (memory-level
    parallelism and the misses beside each dependent link), model cycles, and the
    out-of-calibration flag.
  - *Its speed:* about 0.8 s a Wikidata block.
- **Calibration.** κ = 4 is fitted on opend 7/28 alone. The held-out results are in the README:
  - every held-out cell is on the right side of 1;
  - opend is ranked above LazyCells at 7, 8 and 10 workers;
  - 6 of 9 held-out pairs are in the i9's order; open against opend is wrong;
  - the gain is attributed to fewer instructions, not to shorter chains;
  - no low-W crossover is predicted.
- **The tests.** `test_moonshot.py` has 26 Python tests. `cargo test` in `bench/moonshot` runs 6
  Rust tests, the journal's own checks included.
- **CI.** A new `moonshot` job in `.github/workflows/test.yml`. CI had not reported on it at
  handoff.
- **The i9 recipe** `just bench-moonshot-calibrate` (`.just/bench.just`), and `corpus.sh`.

## Open questions

- **The mechanism.** The model says opend's gain is work and L2/L3 traffic, with longer
  dependent past-L2 chains than lazy. Record 71 says shorter chains. Which does the i9 sweep's
  stall data support?
- **The DRAM ratio.** The model overpredicts it for the open kernels (1.14–1.17 against
  0.95–1.04). Is a missing hardware prefetch or writeback behaviour to blame?
- **The chain counts of records 72–75** (0.2–0.25 stages a byte for lazy) are algorithmic. This
  model's any-level dependent loads are about 1.4 a byte. A comparable definition is open, and
  Astras A and C aren't `InverseBwt` kernels yet.
- **Historical bundles.** eager-trim (8426934a) predates 0.5.6's kernels, so it isn't used. Can
  any other bundle on `evidence` be rebuilt at a 0.5.6-equivalent kernel?
- **Attribution of the #145 LOG.** No role named "scorer" exists. Post it as the Coordinator's
  delegate, with an `ACTOR:` line?

## Draft calibration rule (for the #145 LOG)

> bench-moonshot-calibrate at <commit>.
> - *What it measures:* lazy (0.5.6), opend, open and eager cells in the shipped workers,
>   kernel-only, at W = 1, 2, 4, 7, 8 and 10; unpinned; 10 paired rounds; 2 s settle; Wikidata
>   ×4; perf stall counters per run.
> - *Use:* κ is refit on lazy and opend at W = 7 only. Every other (arm, W) cell is held out.
> - *What is reported:* the predicted ratio against the measured one; the count of cells on the
>   right side of 1; the pairs ranked right at each W; and the Spearman rank of the proxy's
>   dependent-chain and overlap metrics against `stalls_l3_miss` per byte, across arms and W.
> - *What it decides:* nothing in production.

## Commands

```sh
# the corpus, once (corpora/wikidata.bz2)
bash bench/moonshot/corpus.sh
# score a candidate (--quick: the gate's short set; --with-opend: time opend too)
just moonshot-score bench/moonshot/candidates/opend.rs --rounds 5
python3 bench/moonshot/moonshot.py report bench/moonshot/target/moonshot/score-<time>
# refit kappa (lazy and opend at 7 workers only)
python3 bench/moonshot/moonshot.py calibrate --grid 2,3,4,5,6
# the tests
python3 bench/moonshot/test_moonshot.py
(cd bench/moonshot && cargo fmt --check && cargo clippy --release --all-targets -- -D warnings && cargo test --release)
# the i9 recipe, a dry run here (DRY is the 5th positional argument)
just bench-moonshot-calibrate 10 2 4 1,2,4,7,8,10 1
```
